Elden Ring Nightreign — 100% Completion

All 9 Nightfarers with optimal builds, all 8 Nightlords with phase-by-phase strategies, 3 Everdark Sovereigns, all 12 Relics, and all 20 trophies. Nightreign is a co-op roguelite — each expedition is a 3-day cycle ending in a Nightlord fight.

⚠️ Expedition Structure: Day 1 and Day 2 are open-world exploration and preparation. Day 3 is the Nightlord boss fight. Dying during exploration respawns you at the Roundtable Hold. Dying during the Nightlord fight ends the expedition.

⚔ Nightfarers (9)

All 9 playable characters with unlock conditions, passive abilities, skills, and optimal builds. Check off each Nightfarer as you unlock them.

▶

Wylder The Drifter

  1. Unlock: Available from the start.
  2. Passive: Grappling Hook — dash to any surface or enemy in range.
  3. Skill: Rallying Standard — plant a banner that buffs nearby allies' attack and defense.
  4. Best build: Quality (Str/Dex) — use Rivers of Blood or Moonveil for late-game.
  5. Relic priority: Erdtree Favor, Crimson Amber Medallion, Shard of Alexander.
  6. Nightlord synergy: Strongest against Gnoster (Gravity) — grapple to avoid ground slams.
▶

Ironeye The Ranger

  1. Unlock: Available from the start.
  2. Passive: Eagle Eye — increased range and reduced arrow drop on all bows.
  3. Skill: Rain of Arrows — fires a spread of arrows in a wide arc.
  4. Best build: Dex/Arc — use Serpent Bow with Rot arrows or Pulley Crossbow.
  5. Relic priority: Arrow's Sting, Radagon's Soreseal, Millicent's Prosthesis.
  6. Nightlord synergy: Strongest against Maris (Water) — attack from range to avoid waves.
▶

Duchess The Assassin

  1. Unlock: Complete 3 expeditions with any Nightfarer.
  2. Passive: Shadowstep — brief invisibility after a successful backstab.
  3. Skill: Poison Mist — release a cloud of toxic mist that builds Poison on enemies.
  4. Best build: Arc/Dex — use Reduvia or Eleonora's Poleblade for bleed/poison combo.
  5. Relic priority: White Mask, Lord of Blood's Exultation, Kindred of Rot's Exultation.
  6. Nightlord synergy: Strongest against Fulghor (Lightning) — bleed bypasses his shield.
▶

Recluse The Sorcerer

  1. Unlock: Complete 5 expeditions total.
  2. Passive: Sorcery Mastery — all sorceries cost 20% less FP.
  3. Skill: Comet Azur — channel the full power of Azur's comet (requires FP management).
  4. Best build: Int — use Lusat's Glintstone Staff with Comet Azur and Ranni's Dark Moon.
  5. Relic priority: Graven-Mass Talisman, Graven-School Talisman, Magic Scorpion Charm.
  6. Nightlord synergy: Strongest against Gnoster — gravity spells stagger him frequently.
▶

Guardian The Paladin

  1. Unlock: Complete 7 expeditions total.
  2. Passive: Sacred Vow — party members within 10m receive a 15% damage reduction aura.
  3. Skill: Golden Vow — buff all party members with increased attack and defense.
  4. Best build: Str/Fai — use Blasphemous Blade for lifesteal + Sacred Relic Sword for AoE.
  5. Relic priority: Two Fingers Heirloom, Sacred Scorpion Charm, Erdtree's Favor +2.
  6. Nightlord synergy: Strongest against Maris — sacred damage deals bonus vs. her water form.
▶

Executor The Berserker

  1. Unlock: Complete 10 expeditions total.
  2. Passive: Poise Mastery — cannot be staggered by attacks under 500 damage.
  3. Skill: Endure — temporarily boost poise to maximum and reduce incoming damage by 30%.
  4. Best build: Str — use Giant-Crusher or Greatsword with Flame Grant Me Strength.
  5. Relic priority: Arsenal Charm, Dragoncrest Greatshield Talisman, Shard of Alexander.
  6. Nightlord synergy: Strongest against Adel (Flame) — high poise tanks through fire attacks.
▶

Raider The Berserker (Alt)

  1. Unlock: Complete 15 expeditions total.
  2. Passive: Momentum — consecutive hits increase attack power by 5% per hit (max 25%).
  3. Skill: Barbaric Roar — roar that buffs all attacks with bonus physical damage.
  4. Best build: Str/Dex — use Bloodhound's Fang or Claymore with jump attack talismans.
  5. Relic priority: Claw Talisman, Spear Talisman, Dragoncrest Greatshield Talisman.
  6. Nightlord synergy: Strongest against Fulghor — jump attacks avoid his ground lightning.
▶

Revenant The Necromancer

  1. Unlock: Complete 20 expeditions total.
  2. Passive: Death's Embrace — when below 20% HP, gain 30% damage increase.
  3. Skill: Summon Undead — call 3 undead warriors that fight independently.
  4. Best build: Arc — use Mohgwyn's Sacred Spear with Blood Oath Ritual.
  5. Relic priority: Godskin Swaddling Cloth, Bloodsoaked Exultation, Millicent's Prosthesis.
  6. Nightlord synergy: Strongest against Gnoster — undead minions distract gravity pulls.
▶

Stormcaller The Elemental

  1. Unlock: Defeat all 8 Nightlords at least once.
  2. Passive: Storm Affinity — all wind and lightning spells deal 20% bonus damage.
  3. Skill: Tempest — summon a massive storm that deals AoE lightning damage for 10 seconds.
  4. Best build: Dex/Int — use Meteorite Staff with Gavel of Haima and Storm Blade.
  5. Relic priority: Lightning Scorpion Charm, Graven-Mass Talisman, Radagon's Soreseal.
  6. Nightlord synergy: Strongest against Maris — storm spells evaporate her water shields.

👁 Nightlords (8)

All 8 Nightlord bosses with phase-by-phase strategies, weaknesses, and recommended Nightfarers. Check off each Nightlord as you defeat them.

▶

Gnoster, Wisdom of Gravity Gravity

  1. Phase 1: Stay at medium range — gravity pulls are short-range only.
  2. When he raises both arms, dodge toward him to avoid the gravity slam.
  3. Use Spirit Ashes to distract him during the orbital debris phase.
  4. Phase 2 trigger: 50% HP — he launches into the air and creates a debris field.
  5. During Phase 2, stay moving — the debris tracks your last position, not current.
  6. Weakness: Frostbite and Bleed both proc quickly due to his large hitbox.
  7. Recommended: Wylder (grapple to avoid pulls) or Recluse (gravity spells stagger him).
▶

Maris, Daughter of the Flood Water

  1. Phase 1: Stay on the elevated platforms — water jets only hit ground level.
  2. Her wave attack has a 2-second telegraph — dodge toward the wave, not away.
  3. Interrupt her healing by dealing 300+ damage during the glowing animation.
  4. Phase 2 trigger: 40% HP — she floods the arena floor, forcing platform combat.
  5. During Phase 2, prioritize the water spouts that appear — they deal massive damage.
  6. Weakness: Lightning deals 30% bonus damage to her water form.
  7. Recommended: Ironeye (range from platforms) or Guardian (sacred damage bonus).
▶

Fulghor, the Undying Storm Lightning

  1. Phase 1: Do not stand in groups — chain lightning jumps between players.
  2. His shield phase makes him invulnerable — wait for the shield to drop before attacking.
  3. Bleed is the fastest way to break his shield — use Reduvia or Bloodhound's Fang.
  4. Phase 2 trigger: 35% HP — he transforms into pure lightning, gaining new attacks.
  5. During Phase 2, stay close — his long-range lightning misses at melee range.
  6. Weakness: Holy damage bypasses his lightning resistance.
  7. Recommended: Duchess (bleed to break shield) or Raider (jump attacks avoid ground lightning).
▶

Adel, Baron of the Flame Fire

  1. Phase 1: The flame pillars appear in a set pattern — memorize it after the first attempt.
  2. Fire breath has a 3-second duration — dodge behind him and attack his tail.
  3. Frost buildup is extremely effective — Freezing Grease or Hoarfrost Stomp.
  4. Phase 2 trigger: 45% HP — the arena floor becomes lava, dealing constant damage.
  5. During Phase 2, use the stone platforms to avoid lava damage.
  6. Weakness: Frostbite deals 40% bonus damage to fire-type enemies.
  7. Recommended: Executor (high poise tanks fire attacks) or Recluse (Ranni's Dark Moon for frost).
▶

Heolstor, the Nightlord Darkness

  1. Phase 1: Attack the clone that has a shadow beneath it — the others are illusions.
  2. Darkness pools deal damage over time — avoid stepping in them.
  3. He is vulnerable to Holy damage — use Sacred Blade or Golden Vow.
  4. Phase 2 trigger: 30% HP — all clones vanish and he reveals his true form.
  5. During Phase 2, he gains a new darkness beam attack — dodge to the side, not back.
  6. Weakness: Holy damage and Torrent (if accessible in the arena).
  7. Recommended: Guardian (Holy aura) or Stormcaller (AoE to hit true form).
▶

Calyx, the Crucible Nature

  1. Phase 1: The tail sweep has a wide arc — dodge toward his body, not away.
  2. His wing buffet creates a wind field — stay grounded and wait for it to end.
  3. Scarlet Rot is effective — use Rot Grease or Rotten Breath incantation.
  4. Phase 2 trigger: 40% HP — he fully transforms, gaining all Crucible aspects.
  5. During Phase 2, he gains a breath attack — dodge to his left side.
  6. Weakness: Scarlet Rot and Poison both apply quickly.
  7. Recommended: Duchess (Poison Mist) or Revenant (undead minions distract him).
▶

Libra, the Balancer Cosmic

  1. Phase 1: Deal equal damage to both his left and right sides — the scales must stay balanced.
  2. If one side takes 30% more damage, he enrages and deals double damage for 20 seconds.
  3. Split your party — one player attacks each side simultaneously.
  4. Phase 2 trigger: 50% HP — the scales break and he enters a rage phase.
  5. During Phase 2, focus all damage on his glowing core.
  6. Weakness: No elemental weakness — pure physical damage is most consistent.
  7. Recommended: Balanced party composition — one tank, one healer, one DPS each side.
▶

Zullie, the Witch Sorcery

  1. Phase 1: She drains FP with her aura — stay at mid-range to minimize drain.
  2. Her sorcery barrage is blockable — use a 100% physical shield for the first wave.
  3. Interrupt her casting by dealing 200+ damage during the glowing staff animation.
  4. Phase 2 trigger: 35% HP — she summons Alberich as a second boss.
  5. During Phase 2, prioritize Zullie — Alberich becomes passive if she dies first.
  6. Weakness: Bleed and Frostbite both interrupt her casting animations.
  7. Recommended: Raider (momentum stacks) or Ironeye (interrupt casts from range).

🌑 Everdark Sovereigns (3)

Enhanced versions of Nightlords unlocked after defeating the base version 3 times. Significantly harder with new attacks.

▶

Gnoster Prime Gravity (Enhanced)

  1. Singularity attack: When he raises both arms and glows purple, immediately sprint away.
  2. He uses Phase 2 orbital debris from the start — keep moving at all times.
  3. Bring Immunizing Horn Charm to resist the gravity debuff.
  4. His health pool is 40% larger than standard Gnoster.
▶

Maris Prime Water (Enhanced)

  1. The arena starts flooded — bring Immunizing Horn Charm for the slow debuff.
  2. Tidal Surge: A massive wave that covers the entire arena — climb to the highest platform.
  3. Her healing is faster in Prime form — interrupt it within 1 second.
  4. Lightning damage is still her weakness — prioritize Fulghor's Relic for this fight.
▶

Fulghor Prime Lightning (Enhanced)

  1. Thunderclap: A screen-wide lightning strike — only dodge roll avoids it.
  2. Chain lightning now jumps 5 times — never stand within 5m of an ally.
  3. His shield in Phase 2 regenerates — you must break it twice.
  4. Holy damage is critical here — bring Sacred Scorpion Charm.
