How 100% Works in Nightreign

Read First

Nightreign has 37 trophies (1 Platinum, 3 Gold, 16 Silver, 17 Bronze). Unlike the base game, there are no missable trophies — everything can be done in any order. Estimated time: ~50 hours. Can be done entirely solo.

►

Recommended Completion Order

Nightfarers (Characters)

0%
0 / 8

Unlock all 8 Nightfarers. 6 are available from the start. Duchess and Revenant must be unlocked. "A Champion's Path" requires defeating a Nightlord with every character.

Wylder

Melee / Versatile

Default starter. Balanced stats, Claw Shot grapple skill. Best all-rounder for new players.

Available from start

Guardian

Tank / Support

Shield-focused tank. Highest defense. Protects allies with Boulderstone skill.

Available from start

Ironeye

Ranged DPS / Support

Bow specialist. Can revive allies from range. Marking skill buffs team damage.

Available from start

Raider

Brawler / Off-tank

Barbarian-type. Immune to knockdown during Retaliate skill. Totem Stela ultimate buffs allies.

Available from start

Recluse

Mage / Squishy

Highest FP, lowest HP. Soulblood Song marks enemies so allies heal on hit. Best for magic builds.

Available from start

Executor

Katana / Parry

Sekiro-inspired. Revolves around parrying and katana combos. High skill ceiling, high reward.

Available from start

Duchess

Agile / Debuffer

DEX/INT hybrid. Unlocked after your first successful Tricephalos (Gladius) expedition.

⚠ Must Unlock — Complete first Gladius run

Revenant

Summoner / Spirit Ashes

Uses Spirit Ashes to summon creatures. Unlocked by reaching Roundtable Hold and speaking with the Revenant NPC.

⚠ Must Unlock — Speak with Revenant at Roundtable Hold

Nightlords (Final Bosses)

0%
0 / 8

Defeat all 8 Nightlords at least once for the Nightlord Conqueror trophy. Each Nightlord also has its own individual trophy. Heolstor is the final boss — defeat him for the Night Aspect and Dawn trophies.

🦀

Gladius, Beast of Night

The first Nightlord. Three-headed beast. Weaknesses: Lightning, Holy. Completing this run unlocks the Duchess Nightfarer.

Trophy: Tricephalos (Bronze)
🦎

Adel, Baron of Night

Massive armored giant. Weaknesses: Fire, Bleed. Watch for the ground slam AOE — roll toward him, not away.

Trophy: Gaping Jaw (Bronze)
🐛

Gnoster, Wisdom of Night

Insectoid sorcerer. Weaknesses: Physical, Frostbite. Interrupt his casting windows with aggressive pressure.

Trophy: Sentient Pest (Bronze)
🌊

Maris, Fathom of Night

Deep-sea entity. Weaknesses: Lightning, Frost. Phase 2 adds water arena — stay mobile and avoid the tidal sweeps.

Trophy: Augur (Bronze)
⚖

Libra, Creature of Night

Scales-themed boss. Weaknesses: Holy, Bleed. Balances between two attack modes — read the scale tip to predict phase.

Trophy: Equilibrious Beast (Bronze)
🛡

Fulghor, Champion of Nightglow

Armored knight with glowing sword. Weaknesses: Bleed, Lightning. Aggressive melee fighter — use Guard Counter frequently.

Trophy: Darkdrift Knight (Bronze)
🌫

Caligo, Miasma of Night

Fog/poison entity. Weaknesses: Fire, Holy. Constantly moves through the arena — use AOE attacks and track his core.

Trophy: Fissure in the Fog (Bronze)
★

Heolstor the Nightlord

Final Boss. Unlocked after defeating all other Nightlords. Weaknesses: Holy, Bleed. Multiple phases. Defeating him gives Night Aspect and Dawn trophies.

Trophies: Night Aspect (Silver) + Dawn (Gold)
