Chapter 1 — New Austin (Missions 1–22)

0/22
  1. Follow the Marshal into Armadillo
  2. Speak with Marshal Johnson at the saloon
  3. Ride to the MacFarlane Ranch with Bonnie
Tip: Kill 5 flying birds during the ride for Sharpshooter 1
  1. Ride with Bonnie to find the stolen horses
  2. Chase down and lasso the horse thieves
  3. Return the horses to the ranch
Tip: Kill 5 rabbits and 5 coyotes here for Sharpshooter 2 and 3. Also start Master Hunter 1 by skinning coyotes.
  1. Board the train at Armadillo station
  2. Defend the train from bandits
  3. Kill 5 birds from the moving train for Sharpshooter 4
Tip: This is your best window for Sharpshooter 4 — kill birds from the train roof.
  1. Find the drunk ranch hand at the saloon
  2. Escort him back to the ranch
Tip: Shoot 2 hats off people's heads here for Sharpshooter 6.
