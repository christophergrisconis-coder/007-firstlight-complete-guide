Red Dead Redemption — 100% Completion

0%

All 57 story missions, 19 Stranger tasks, 9 treasure locations, 6 challenge categories, rare weapons, all outfits, and all 22 trophies including the Legend of the West Platinum. John Marston's journey from outlaw to rancher — and Jack's revenge.

⚠️ Missable: Dastardly and Manifest Destiny trophies require specific actions. Dastardly = tie a woman to train tracks. Manifest Destiny = kill the last Buffalo in Great Plains. Both are permanently missable if you progress too far without doing them.

📍 New Austin▶
▶

Mission: Exodus in America

📍 Armadillo · 👤 Marshal Johnson
  1. Ride into Armadillo and speak to Marshal Johnson at the jail.
  2. Follow the Marshal to the outskirts of town to meet your contact.
  3. Ride to Ridgewood Farm and clear out the outlaws holding the area.
  4. Loot the outlaw camp and return to the Marshal with your report.
  5. Complete without dying to build early reputation with Armadillo.

Mission: New Friends Old Problems

📍 Armadillo · 👤 Marshal Johnson
  1. Speak to Marshal Johnson about the Williamson gang's movements.
  2. Ride to the ambush point north of Armadillo with the Marshal's posse.
  3. Fight off the Williamson gang ambush — use cover behind the rocks.
  4. Chase down the fleeing outlaws on horseback and lasso the leader.
  5. Return the captured outlaw to the Marshal for interrogation.

Mission: Obstacles in Our Path

📍 Armadillo · 👤 Marshal Johnson
  1. Meet the Marshal at the north road out of Armadillo.
  2. Escort the wagon through the canyon pass — outlaws will ambush from both sides.
  3. Use Dead Eye to clear the snipers on the canyon walls quickly.
  4. Protect the wagon from being destroyed — it cannot take more than 3 hits.
  5. Deliver the wagon safely to Ridgewood Farm.

  4. Return the cattle to the ranch pen before nightfall.
  5. Speak to Bonnie to learn about the local outlaw situation.
