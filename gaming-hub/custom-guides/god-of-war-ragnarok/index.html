God of War Ragnarök — 100% Completion

Fimbulwinter grinds toward Ragnarök. Kratos and Atreus travel all nine realms to stop — or start — the end of everything, with Thor and Odin waiting at the top of the world tree. This guide covers the full story path, the favors that outshine the main quest, every raven and Nornir chest, the Draupnir Spear, and the Berserker King who is harder than the final boss.

⏱ 30-60 hrs · Scales (Story → GMGOW) · ⭐ 9.4/10

⚔ Main Story Path

▶

Surviving Fimbulwinter — Thor & Odin come knockingMedium

📍 Midgard, the homestead · 🎁 The opening duel of the generation
  1. The sled prologue teaches the new Blades/Axe swap flow — practice triangle-recall weapon switching immediately.
  2. Thor duel: he punishes greed; his hammer-throw returns like your axe — dodge sideways twice, the return arc curves.
  3. Parry his blue-ring attacks with the shield bash; red-ring means MOVE.
  4. The fight is scripted to be survivable, not winnable — absorb the lesson: dodge direction matters more than distance.
  5. Afterward, the Quest for Týr begins: recruit the war god from Svartalfheim's mines.
🎁 The opening duel of the generation
▶

The Quest for Týr — SvartalfheimMedium

📍 Svartalfheim, the dwarven realm · 🎁 Týr freed, story spine begins
  1. Nidavellir's water-wheel puzzles: freeze geysers with the axe to raise platforms; recall to drop them.
  2. Explore the Bay of Bounty ASAP — the boat opens optional islands with two Nornir chests and the Radsvinn's Rig favor chain.
  3. The Hateful miniboss teaches elite tells: unblockable grabs come after her scream, dodge backward twice.
  4. Find Týr in the deep mine: the 'largest prisoner cell' misdirect is story-critical later — remember what you see.
  5. Return via the Mystic Gateway network; Svartalfheim reopens for full exploration after the chapter.
🎁 Týr freed, story spine begins
▶

Atreus in Ironwood & the Jötunheim shrine trailEasy-Medium

📍 Ironwood (Jötunheim), Vanaheim, Alfheim · 🎁 Angrboda, the shrine prophecies, the mask mystery
  1. Ironwood: play Atreus's sections earnestly — the celestial paint sequences plant the endgame's biggest reveals.
  2. Angrboda becomes Atreus's anchor; her jungle traversal chapters alternate with Kratos's realm work.
  3. Odin's mask chain (Atreus undercover in Asgard) shows Asgard from inside — steal every detail; the mask's rift purpose pays off in the finale.
  4. The Atreus transformation beat lands mid-game: the father-son fight afterward is the story's emotional hinge.
  5. Alternate protagonist chapters unlock realm access for later cleanup — note locked paths for post-story favors.
🎁 Angrboda, the shrine prophecies, the mask mystery
▶

Forging Ragnarök — Brok, Sindri & the Draupnir SpearHard

📍 Vanaheim → Alfheim → the Forge · 🎁 The Draupnir Spear, the alliance, the cost
  1. The Draupnir Spear chapter (Forging Destiny): journey with Brok to birth the spear — environmental breaks.
  2. The spear opens sealed climbing points and golden spear-socket walls across every realm — massive backtrack value.
  3. The Heimdall confrontation: his foresight dodges everything EXCEPT the Draupnir's detonations — the fight is the spear's tutorial and a masterclass in hateable villains.
  4. The betrayal beat at Sindri's house lands here. Watch the dwarves. The story's grief is theirs.
  5. Muster the realms: Freya's reconciliation quest (Freya's Missing Peace) and the Vanaheim alliance chapters gate the finale.
🎁 The Draupnir Spear, the alliance, the cost
▶

Ragnarök — the assault on AsgardHard

📍 Asgard, the final chapter · 🎁 The ending, post-game unlock
  1. Point of no return is clearly flagged (Creatures of Prophecy) — side content stays available post-story, for higher difficulties.
  2. Thor rematch: everything from the opening duel, faster — Draupnir detonations break his charge stance; parry rhythm from hours of practice pays off here.
  3. Odin: he weaponizes all three elements — swap weapons per his phase (Bifröst attacks need cleansing via burst damage).
  4. His Ravens empower him mid-fight; the trio finale (Kratos, Freya, Atreus) rotates control pressure.
  5. The ending scenes and final mural land the series' thesis. Post-game: Niflheim's endgame zone, the Berserkers, and the true final favor open up.
🎁 The ending, post-game unlock

📜 Favors (Side Quests)

▶

The Weight of Chains — the lyngbakrEasy

📍 Bay of Bounty, Svartalfheim · 🎁 Whale-sized guilt, XP, Bay unlocks
  1. Start at Althjof's Rig in the Bay of Bounty (boat required).
  2. Cross the mining rigs, releasing each of the three chain anchors (crane puzzles: freeze, rotate, recall).
  3. The lyngbakr surfaces piece by piece as chains fall — the scale reveal is worth the detour alone.
  4. Combat is light: raider camps guard each rig.
  5. The closing scene — Kratos, Mimir, and a creature too broken to leave — is the game's quiet masterpiece. Do this favor early.
🎁 Whale-sized guilt, XP, Bay unlocks
▶

For Vanaheim! — the Crater & the wildsMedium-Hard

📍 Vanaheim's hidden Crater (post-Forging Destiny) · 🎁 An entire secret open zone, dragon fights, best favors
  1. Unlock via the favor Scent of Survival after the spear chapter — follow the trail into the Crater.
  2. Find the Celestial Altar: toggling day/night changes enemies, puzzles and open paths — every area has two versions.
  3. Favor chain: Return of the River (dam release floods the plains for boat access), Trail of the Dead, Animal Instincts.
  4. The Crater holds three dragon fights including the Crimson Dread — Vanaheim's apex hunt.
  5. Completing the full Crater set is the best XP-to-hour content in the game and gates several armor finals.
🎁 An entire secret open zone, dragon fights, best favors
▶

Freya's Missing Peace & the Vanir reconciliationMedium

📍 Vanaheim, Freyr's camp · 🎁 Freya as permanent companion, her story resolution
  1. Story-adjacent: after Vanaheim's main chapter, her favor chain unlocks at Freyr's Camp.
  2. Break Odin's binding on her at the ritual site fight to restore her Valkyrie power.
  3. Her companion arrows and abilities upgrade through these favors — she becomes the best co-op AI in the series.
  4. Her scenes with Kratos across these quests carry the reconciliation the whole series was building.
  5. Required emotionally, near-required mechanically before the finale.
🎁 Freya as permanent companion, her story resolution
▶

The Broken Prison — the true final favorMedium

📍 Niflheim, post-story · 🎁 The last story scene the game holds back
  1. Complete the main story, then return to Niflheim via the Mystic Gateway.
  2. Fight through the prison wreck levels (Einherjar remnants).
  3. The cell at the bottom resolves a hanging mystery from Svartalfheim's mines.
  4. The favor's final scene — the mural — recontextualizes Kratos's whole journey. It is the actual ending.
  5. Do it last, after every other favor, the way the game intends.
🎁 The last story scene the game holds back

💎 Collectibles

▶

The Ravens of Odin (48)Medium

📍 All nine realms — green glowing birds · 🎁 The Raven Tree chests (armor: the Raven set pieces), trophy
  1. Listen: ravens emit a distinct whisper-caw; the green glow shows through foliage and fog.
  2. Axe throw handles most; some fly patrol loops — lead the throw or wait at the loop's pause point.
  3. Svartalfheim holds 10, Vanaheim 13 (6 in the Crater), Alfheim 10, Midgard 6, the rest scattered in story realms.
  4. The Raven Tree in Niflheim opens one chest per milestone tier — the final chest needs all 48.
  5. Post-story cleanup with all traversal tools is painless; the in-game labors menu tracks per-region counts.
🎁 The Raven Tree chests (armor: the Raven set pieces), trophy
▶

Nornir Chests (rune puzzles)Medium

📍 Every region · 🎁 Idunn Apples (+HP) & Horns of Blood Mead (+Rage)
  1. Three rune mechanisms hide near each chest: torches (Blades ignite), bells (axe ring, sometimes timed), spin with throws), and paint pots (spear detonate).
  2. Timed bell chests: plan the throw route before ringing the first.
  3. Several need late tools (spear sockets, sigil arrows) — flagged puzzles you can't solve yet are tool-locked, not you-locked.
  4. Six apples/horns per full bar upgrade; max both by endgame for GMGOW headroom.
  5. The labors menu lists chests found/remaining per region — trust it during cleanup.
🎁 Idunn Apples (+HP) & Horns of Blood Mead (+Rage)
▶

Draugr Holes (6 Hel-tears)Hard

📍 Midgard, Svartalfheim, Vanaheim · 🎁 Hel-tear rewards and a hidden boss
  1. Locations: The Oarsmen and Alberich Hollow (Svartalfheim), the Derelict Outpost, and the Plains, plus Midgard's Lake of Nine edge.
  2. Each spawns a Hateful variant: her delayed grab punishes panic rolls — dodge on her scream, not her lunge.
  3. Fight arenas are tight; clear adds first where present.
  4. All six closed unlocks the final Draugr Hole confrontation and top-tier weapon attachments.
  5. Rage management wins these: bank Rage for her second-phase pressure.
🎁 Hel-tear rewards and a hidden boss
▶

Artifacts, Lore Markers & Kvasir's PoemsEasy

📍 All realms · 🎁 XP, Mimir stories, the best satire Santa Monica ever wrote
  1. Kvasir's Poems (14): each parodies a PlayStation exclusive (''Horizon Forbidden West' becomes 'The Dawn Machine' style riffs) — collect them all just to read them.
  2. Artifact sets (each realm has themed sets) sell for hacksilver after Mimir's commentary.
  3. Lore markers trigger Mimir/companion conversations — the actual worldbuilding channel; never skip the boat stories.
  4. Everything feeds the labors XP; artifacts show as golden glints.
  5. Post-story, use shop upgrades before artifact-hunting trips for route efficiency.
🎁 XP, Mimir stories, the best satire Santa Monica ever wrote

⚔ Unique Gear & Weapons

▶

Draupnir SpearStory

📍 Story unlock — Forging Destiny · 🎁 Third weapon: infinite spears, detonation combos
  1. Unlocked in the Forging Destiny chapter; socket walls) and combat pacing.
  2. Core loop: embed 3-4 spears in an elite, detonate (triangle) for the stagger burst.
  3. Its ranged pressure fills the Blades/Axe gap: fast multi-hit at distance without element commitment.
  4. Skill priorities: the charge finisher and the detonation radius upgrades.
  5. Against Heimdall and shielded Einherjar, spear detonations break what nothing else touches.
🎁 Third weapon: infinite spears, detonation combos
▶

Steinbjörn Armor (the tank set)Medium

📍 Midgard relic hunt — the Mystical Heirloom + troll statues · 🎁 Best defensive set in the game, life-steal on damage
  1. Get the Mystical Heirloom relic in Midgard, frozen in ice between Tyr's Temple and the tower — spear the ice wall.
  2. Use the relic at the four dormant troll statues: Derelict Outpost (Midgard), Noatun's Garden (Vanaheim), Alberich Island (Svartalfheim), and Vanaheim's plains statue.
  3. Each awakened troll is an elite fight; they drop Slumber Stones.
  4. Craft the Steinbjörn set at the dwarves' shop with the stones.
  5. The set's damage-triggered healing carries aggressive GMGOW builds — tank the hit, heal on the punish.
🎁 Best defensive set in the game, life-steal on damage
▶

Mjölnir's answer: the best Axe & Blades buildsMedium

📍 Skill trees + attachment hunting · 🎁 Endgame loadouts
  1. Axe (Permafrost build): the Banahogg-style heavy runics; permafrost meter turns every swing into bonus damage.
  2. Blades (Immolation): keep the burn meter full with fast light strings and the Rampage of the Furies runic.
  3. Swap weapons per enemy elemental resistances: blue shields break to Blades, red to Axe (the game color-codes the answer).
  4. Runic attacks are your burst economy: slot one crowd runic and one boss runic per weapon.
  5. Relic choice matters on GMGOW: blocks Valkyrie-tier attacks) is the safety button.
🎁 Endgame loadouts
▶

Atreus & Freya's arrow synergiesEasy

📍 Companion skill trees · 🎁 Crowd control multiplication
  1. Atreus's sigil arrows stack elemental sigils on enemies: with any element to detonate chains.
  2. Command discipline: fire arrows on cooldown at whatever YOU'RE hitting, not spread targets.
  3. Freya (post-favors) adds binding arrows — her hold effect sets up free heavy runics.
  4. Upgrade the companion trees early; their stun contribution doubles crowd tempo.
  5. On GMGOW, companion pressure buys the spacing errors the difficulty otherwise punishes.
🎁 Crowd control multiplication

📋 Boss Strategies

▶

King Hrólf Kraki (Berserker King)Extreme

📍 King's Grave, Midgard — after all 12 Berserkers · 🎁 The final Berserker gear — hardest fight in the game
  1. Prerequisite: defeat all 12 Berserker gravestone fights across the realms (each teaches one of his moves — they ARE the tutorial).
  2. His Hel-fire projectiles and scything dashes rotate constantly; there is no safe rhythm, only read-and-answer.
  3. His red combo ends in a leaping slam — dodge INTO and past him, punishing the landing.
  4. When he summons the spectral wolves, kill them instantly — they heal him on hit.
  5. Full apples/horns, best-rolled gear, Hilt of Skofnung for panic stops. Beating him on GMGOW is the game's true completion badge.
🎁 The final Berserker gear — hardest fight in the game
▶

Thor (both duels)Hard

📍 Opening lake / Asgard finale · 🎁 Story progress, series-best duel design
  1. Mjölnir's throw arcs curve on return — dodge lateral both directions, never straight back.
  2. His blue overhead crash is parryable and grants the biggest punish window; demands distance.
  3. Duel 2 adds lightning phases: he charges the arena floor in grids — watch his hammer-raise for the pattern's origin.
  4. Spear detonations interrupt his charge stance; Blades reach punishes his recovery.
  5. Between phases his dialogue tracks the story's real fight — a father measuring what he became. Listen while you dodge.
🎁 Story progress, series-best duel design
▶

HeimdallMedium-Hard

📍 The Vanaheim shore chapter · 🎁 Story progress (and catharsis)
  1. Phase 1: nothing lands. Intended. Embed spears and DETONATE — foresight can't dodge an explosion already inside your guard.
  2. Gulltoppr (his summoned beast) phases sandwich the duel — standard beast rules, watch the charge lanes.
  3. Once staggered by detonations, he becomes hittable and furious — his Bifröst attacks apply the shatter debuff; burst it off by attacking.
  4. His taunts adapt to your play patterns. The game wants you to hate him. Comply.
  5. The finish is a story beat with consequences — Kratos knows it too. Watch Mimir after.
🎁 Story progress (and catharsis)
▶

Gná, Queen of the ValkyriesExtreme

📍 The Crucible, Muspelheim (post-story) · 🎁 Hardest optional duel outside the Berserker King
  1. Unlocks post-story at Muspelheim's Crucible; complete the arena challenge chains to face her.
  2. Her Bifröst infliction means every hit taken threatens shatter damage — attack to purge, never turtle.
  3. Her aerial dive strings end predictably: dodge the final dive, punish the ground pause.
  4. Her weapon mixups include one unblockable identical in windup to a parryable — the difference is the wing flare. Learn it or die to it forty times.
  5. Fully upgraded everything is assumed. She and Hrólf are the game's twin skill ceilings.
🎁 Hardest optional duel outside the Berserker King

💡 Pro Tips & Secrets

▶

Weapon-swap mid-combo (the real combat system)Medium

📍 Training: any raider camp · 🎁 Double damage tempo
  1. Axe heavy → weapon swap → Blades light chain continues the combo counter and stacks both elements.
  2. Triangle-hold recalls the axe THROUGH enemies mid-Blades-string — free damage line.
  3. Spear quick-throws weave into any string as ranged punctuation.
  4. Elemental status (frost + burn together) multiplies stagger.
  5. Set L1+swap muscle memory early; endgame fights assume it.
🎁 Double damage tempo
▶

Do favors as they appear (power curve secret)Easy

📍 All realms · 🎁 Over-leveled for every story wall
  1. Each region's favors on arrival, before advancing the story chapter out.
  2. XP feeds skill trees; hacksilver feeds armor levels — both trivialize the next story spike.
  3. Companion skill points from favors upgrade arrows — see the synergy entry.
  4. Exception: tool-locked favors (spear/sigil requirements) — note and return.
  5. The Crater (Vanaheim) alone out-levels the entire back third of the story.
🎁 Over-leveled for every story wall
▶

Muspelheim & Niflheim endgame arenasHard

📍 The optional challenge realms · 🎁 Best gear grind, challenge mastery
  1. Muspelheim: paired sword challenges (kill orders, no-damage windows, weapon locks) — clearing pairs opens the Crucible chains toward Gná.
  2. Niflheim post-story adds the raider king arena rotations.
  3. Both pay the crafting currencies for final armor tiers.
  4. Practice GMGOW mechanics here before the Berserker gravestones.
  5. The arena modifiers teach matchup answers the story never forces — worth it even off the trophy path.
🎁 Best gear grind, challenge mastery
▶

Listen to the boat, the sled, and MimirEasy

📍 All traversal · 🎁 The actual lore payload
  1. Boat and sled rides trigger long-form Mimir stories — let them finish before docking; they resume but fragment.
  2. Companion banter changes per story state — re-travel old regions post-reveals for new lines.
  3. Mimir's reaction to the Broken Prison favor is the series' best delivered line. No spoilers.
  4. Idle at Sindri's house between chapters — the dwarves' scenes advance silently.
  5. The photo mode spot: Crater dragon flyovers at sunset. You'll know it when you see it.
🎁 The actual lore payload
