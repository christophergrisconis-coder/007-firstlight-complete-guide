Senua's Saga: Hellblade II — 100% Completion

Senua crosses the sea to Iceland in chains, hunting the slavers who destroyed her home — and finds giants walking the land. Ninja Theory's sequel is the most photorealistic game ever shipped: a binaural-audio psychological saga where every fight is a duel and every shadow speaks. This guide covers all six chapters, every Lorestangir pillar and hidden face, the giant encounters, and how the Furies' whispers actually help you fight.

⏱ 7-10 hrs · Accessible (auto-scaling) · ⭐ 8.8/10

⚔ Chapters

▶

Chapter I: The Shore — wrecked in chainsEasy

📍 Iceland's black-sand coast · 🎁 The opening statement
  1. Survive the wreck: follow the Furies' voices through the surf — the audio IS the navigation; play with headphones from minute one.
  2. The beach crawl teaches the sequel's pace: slower, heavier, closer than the first game.
  3. First duel against Thórgestr on the cliffs: learn the core loop — block, perfect-dodge, punish; there is no camera cut away from Senua, ever.
  4. Spare and bind Thórgestr — he becomes the first companion and translator of the saga.
  5. Chapter's hidden lore begins immediately: the first Lorestangir pole stands off-path near the wreck line (listen for the whisper shift).
🎁 The opening statement
▶

Chapter II: the raided coast & the first giant's shadowMedium

📍 Burned villages inland · 🎁 The saga's true premise
  1. Track the raiders with Thórgestr; hidden-faces collectibles in the scorched timber patterns.
  2. The draugar ambushes introduce sequential combat: enemies attack one at a time cinematically — trust the rhythm, punish every whiff.
  3. Focus (the slow-time meter) charges through perfect play — whenever pressured.
  4. The chapter's climax reveals the first giant at distance — the scale shot the whole game builds on.
  5. Meet Fargrímr and the hiddenfolk — the chapter ends recruiting the next companion toward the giant problem.
🎁 The saga's true premise
▶

Chapter III: The Sea Giant — SjávarrisiMedium

📍 The drowned coast · 🎁 The set piece of 2024
  1. The approach: tidal caves and the drowned village — the water sections are timed to the giant's breathing; move when the Furies say move.
  2. The chase-and-evade structure replaces a health bar: survive the giant's phases by reading the environmental cues (birds scatter before each strike).
  3. The torch-line sequence: keep the flame moving; darkness here is mechanical, not decorative.
  4. The resolution recontextualizes what the giants ARE — no spoilers, but the horror pivots to grief.
  5. Post-chapter, the party grows; themes — don't skip travel talk.
🎁 The set piece of 2024
▶

Chapter IV-V: the hiddenfolk caves & the mountain giantMedium

📍 The highlands and under them · 🎁 The puzzle-and-descent chapters
  1. The cave descent leans on perception-shift puzzles: align the rune shapes in the environment (the sequel's version of the first game's gates).
  2. The dark passages: torch mechanics gate progress — the Furies whisper warmer/colder honestly.
  3. The fire giant sequence trades the sea's dread for fury — the lava field walk is the sequel's boldest visual.
  4. Draugr elite duels stack here: the shielded variants demand dodge-into-attack timing rather than block.
  5. The chapters close on the saga's central choice forming: what Ástríðr's people demand versus what the giants are.
🎁 The puzzle-and-descent chapters
▶

Chapter VI: The Rite — the final ascentMedium

📍 The volcano's final ridge · 🎁 The ending
  1. The final gauntlet chains the saga's duel design: minimal HUD — pure read-and-answer combat.
  2. The confrontation with the slaver king resolves the human plot; the giant rite resolves the mythic one.
  3. The last sequence asks Senua to hold both truths at once — the game's portrayal of Senua's mind completes its arc from curse toward companion.
  4. Stay through credits for the closing narration.
  5. Full completion unlocks the hidden narration threads on replay — a second run at ~7 hours is the intended director's commentary.
🎁 The ending

📜 Optional Depth

▶

The Furies as gameplay (listen or die)Easy

📍 Senua's mind, permanently · 🎁 The whispers are mechanics
  1. In combat, the Furies call incoming off-screen attacks ('Behind!') — binaural audio makes the warning directional; react without looking.
  2. Puzzle-stuck? The whispers shift tone near solutions — warmer, faster, overlapping.
  3. Their commentary tracks Senua's confidence arc: early doubt, late defiance — the same fight sounds different by chapter six.
  4. Headphones are not optional. The entire audio design assumes them.
  5. There are no difficulty spikes the Furies don't telegraph. Trust the voices. That sentence is the whole game.
🎁 The whispers are mechanics
▶

Companion stories between chaptersEasy

📍 Travel segments · 🎁 The saga's human core
  1. Walk, don't sprint, during travel dialogue — sprinting clips conversation branches.
  2. Thórgestr's arc (slaver's son to witness) mirrors the player's own reframing of Senua.
  3. Fargrímr's hiddenfolk faith versus Ástríðr's survivor pragmatism stakes out the giant debate — the finale weighs what you heard.
  4. Idle pauses at vistas trigger optional reflections (the game marks nothing; patience is the button).
  5. There are no side quests in the open-world sense. The saga IS the side quest. Surrender to the pacing.
🎁 The saga's human core

  1. Unlocked from the start via the pause menu; free camera within a generous radius.

  3. Senua's face (Melina Juergens' performance capture) holds up at ANY zoom — the pore-level detail is the tech demo.

  2. Replay chapters via select; the ~7 hour runtime makes full replays honest propositions.
  3. Second-run reading: track how early Fury lines foreshadow the giant reveals — the script is airtight.

  5. This is a saga designed for two viewings. The first is the storm; the second is the map of the storm.

💎 Collectibles

▶

Lorestangir (lore poles) — the rune-carved pillarsMedium

📍 All six chapters, off the main path · 🎁 The Icelandic myth-threads + achievement
  1. Audio tell: an low chanting hum rises off-path near each pole — follow the sound gradient.
  2. Chapter distribution is roughly even; detours are short (the game's linearity keeps searches minutes, not hours).
  3. Classic hiding spots: behind waterfalls, inside ruined crofts, atop side-ridge viewpoints after climbs.
  4. Each pole's Fury narration adds a giant-myth thread that pays off against the chapters' reveals.
  5. Chapter select shows per-chapter counts — mop-up is painless post-completion.
🎁 The Icelandic myth-threads + achievement
▶

Hidden Faces (in trees and rock)Medium

📍 Perception spots across chapters · 🎁 Perception unlocks + achievement
  1. The tell: a focus prompt appears when a face alignment is near; the Furies murmur recognition.
  2. Move until the scattered features align into the face, then hold focus on it.
  3. Faces favor thresholds: cave mouths, gate ruins, shorelines at chapter seams.
  4. They tie into the hiddenfolk theme — each found face deepens Fargrímr's thread on replay.
  5. None are missable-forever: chapter select covers gaps. Sweep slow; the game rewards the unhurried eye.
🎁 Perception unlocks + achievement
▶

The perception puzzles (rune gates)Easy-Medium

📍 Progress gates, all chapters · 🎁 Progress + the signature mechanic
  1. Focus on the gate to burn the target rune into view, then scan the environment for matching shapes.
  2. Runes hide in negative space: crossed beams, shadow angles, water reflections — rotate positions, not just the camera.
  3. The Furies' pitch rises as your view approaches an alignment.
  4. Later chapters layer multiple runes per gate — solve nearest-first; solved runes stay lit.
  5. If truly stuck: stand where the gate is visible and walk a slow circle. The designers always sight-line the answer.
🎁 Progress + the signature mechanic

📋 Duels & Giants

▶

Sjávarrisi, the Sea GiantMedium

📍 Chapter III · 🎁 The sequence of the generation
  1. Phases follow the tide: move on the Furies' calls, shelter on their warnings.
  2. The bird-scatter cue precedes every strike — when the flocks lift, commit to cover.
  3. The torch passage: the flame gates progress; relight points chain if you move without hesitation.
  4. Nothing here punishes reflexes — it punishes panic. The sequence is authored to be survivable at walking pace if you read it.
  5. What it costs Senua matters more than what it drops. Watch her hands after.
🎁 The sequence of the generation
▶

The draugr duels (the combat spine)Medium

📍 Every chapter · 🎁 The fight system itself
  1. Core loop: block light hits, unblockable windups), punish with two-hit strings.
  2. The Focus meter (built by perfect play) slows time — spend it on shielded and dual-wield variants.
  3. Off-screen attackers are ALWAYS called by the Furies — the 'Behind!' warning gives a full dodge window.
  4. Enemy variety peaks with the heavy draugr: feint-heavy movesets that punish early dodges — wait for the Furies' 'Now!'.
  5. Death rewinds generously. The permadeath rumor from the first game remains myth here. Fight relaxed.
🎁 The fight system itself
▶

Illtauga, the Fire GiantMedium

📍 Chapter IV-V arc · 🎁 The saga's turn
  1. The lava-field approach: safe ground reads as dark rock — the Furies call the heat before it blooms.
  2. Her eruption cycles gate movement windows; count the rhythm — three beats of stillness, then move.
  3. The chapter's duels run DURING her phases: finish opponents fast or the field resets your position.
  4. Her resolution lands the game's thesis about what the settlers made of their grief. Again: not a health bar. A reckoning.
  5. The post-sequence quiet — Senua's breathing, the Furies gone soft — is the game's best sound design moment.
🎁 The saga's turn

💡 Pro Tips

▶

Headphones. Binaural. Non-negotiable.Easy

📍 Your ears · 🎁 The intended game
  1. Any stereo headphones work; the binaural mix is baked in, no special hardware needed.
  2. TV speakers lose the directional combat warnings — the game becomes measurably harder and emotionally thinner.
  3. Voice direction = threat direction in combat; puzzle proximity = whisper intensity.
  4. Play alone, at night, screen large. This is a 7-hour session game — one or two sittings, as authored.
  5. If the voices unsettle you: that's the empathy working. with lived-experience consultants for exactly that.
🎁 The intended game
▶

Settings for the cinematic (or against it)Easy

📍 Options menu · 🎁 Your preferred saga
  1. The cinematic black bars can be disabled in settings if you want full-frame play.
  2. Motion blur and film grain sliders tame the filmic look for clarity purists.
  3. Combat has accessibility tuning (timing windows, auto-aids) — the game scales invisibly by default.
  4. Enable the Fury subtitles for the full script; some whispers mix beneath hearing on speakers.
  5. Brightness: calibrate honestly — the dark chapters assume true blacks; crushed shadows hide rune solutions.
🎁 Your preferred saga
▶

Walk. The game is paced in breaths.Easy

📍 All chapters · 🎁 The intended rhythm
  1. Travel conversations complete only at walking pace — sprint saves minutes and costs the script.
  2. Vista pauses trigger optional Fury reflections; stillness is an input.
  3. The 7-9 hour runtime is honest — there is no padding to skip past.
  4. Collectible detours reward the wanderer's pace anyway.
  5. Treat it like the film it half-is: one evening, one saga, phone in another room.
🎁 The intended rhythm
