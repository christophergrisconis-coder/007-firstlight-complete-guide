007 First Light — 100% Completion

Complete all 11 missions (18 sub-chapters), find all 92 collectibles (36 Cards, 23 Intel, 14 Mementos, 10 Postcards, 9 Legacy Items), and unlock all trophies.

Collectibles: 36 Playing Cards · 23 MI6 Intel Files · 14 Mementos · 10 Postcards · 9 Legacy Items. Use Chapter Select to replay missions for missed collectibles. No collectible is permanently missable after story completion.

🔫 Missions

All 11 missions with step-by-step walkthrough, collectible locations, Pacifist and Ghost challenge notes.

▶

Mission: Against the Odds · 1

Chapter: Prologue · Sub-missions: Against the Odds, In His Majesty's Secret Service
  1. Start in the military camp — you play as a Navy Aircrewman, not yet 007.
  2. Complete the opening combat sequence — eliminate the hostile forces in the camp.
  3. Reach the extraction point while avoiding reinforcements.
  4. Complete the sub-mission 'In His Majesty's Secret Service' — follow M's contact through the facility.
  5. Interact with all required objectives and collectibles: 4 Playing Cards hidden in the camp area.
  6. Complete the Pacifist Challenge: reach the extraction without killing anyone.
  7. Complete the Ghost Challenge: reach the extraction without being detected.
  8. The prologue ends with Bond being recruited — Chapter 1 begins.
🏆 Against the Odds (Bronze)
▶

Mission: A Night Out · 1

Chapter: Mission 1 · Sub-missions: The Needle's Eye, The Heart of the Matter, A New Home
  1. Arrive at the mission location — a high-society event.
  2. Complete 'The Needle's Eye' — navigate the event using social stealth (eavesdrop, bluff, pickpocket).
  3. Use the Q-Watch to identify NPC qualities — avoid Watchers who prevent bluffing.
  4. Complete 'The Heart of the Matter' — locate the target and extract information.
  5. Complete 'A New Home' — escape the event after the situation escalates.
  6. Collectibles: 2 Playing Cards, 1 Postcard, 1 Intel file hidden in the venue.
  7. Pacifist Challenge: complete the mission without killing anyone.
  8. Ghost Challenge: complete without being detected.

Chapter: Mission 2 · Sub-missions: A Night Out, A Matter of Considerable Delicacy
  1. Travel to the new mission location — a different city.

  3. Use eavesdropping to gather intel before approaching the target.
  4. Complete 'A Matter of Considerable Delicacy' — a tense negotiation/confrontation.

Mission: All the Time in the World · 1

Chapter: Mission 3 · Sub-missions: All the Time in the World, Out of the Ashes
  1. Arrive at the sprawling hotel level — the largest mission in the game.
  2. Complete 'All the Time in the World' — 27 challenges are available in this mission.
  3. Explore all floors of the hotel to find collectibles and complete challenges.
  4. Complete 'Out of the Ashes' — escape the hotel after the situation deteriorates.
  5. Collectibles: 5 Playing Cards, 2 Intel files, 1 Memento, 1 Postcard.
  6. Pacifist Challenge: complete the hotel section without kills.
  7. Ghost Challenge: navigate the hotel without detection.
  8. This is the longest mission — allow 60-90 minutes for full completion.
🏆 All the Time in the World (Silver)

🃏 Collectibles

All 92 collectibles across all 11 missions. Use Chapter Select to replay any mission. None are permanently missable — all accessible via replay.

▶

Playing Cards · 36 total

Collect all 36 for the Card Shark Bronze Trophy. Cards are hidden in corners, on desks, and in side rooms. Use Bond's instinct ability to highlight them.
▶

MI6 Intel Files · 23 total

Collect all 23 for the Intel Analyst Bronze Trophy. Files are in side rooms, on desks, and in secure areas. Use Bond's instinct ability to highlight them.
▶

Mementos · 14 total

Collect all 14 for the Sentimental Bronze Trophy. Personal items tied to Bond's backstory — found in private quarters and personal areas.
▶

Postcards · 10 total

Collect all 10 for the Worldly Bronze Trophy. Found in tourist areas, hotel lobbies, and public spaces within mission environments.
▶

Legacy Items · 9 total

Collect all 9 for the Legacy Silver Trophy. Iconic Bond items — one per mission, usually in secure or hidden areas. Require exploration off the main path.
