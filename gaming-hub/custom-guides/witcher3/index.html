The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — 100% Completion

All 67 main quests across Velen, Novigrad, Skellige, Kaer Morhen, and both DLCs. All 6 Witcher Gear sets (36 diagrams), 30 Places of Power, and all 30 trophies including Platinum.

⚠️ Missable: It Takes Three to Tango trophy requires romancing both Triss AND Yennefer in the same playthrough — do not choose one before completing both romance quests. Aerondight sword (Blood and Wine) requires completing all 5 chivalric virtues in There Can Be Only One.

📖 Main Quests (67)

All main story quests across the base game and both DLCs, grouped by region. Includes Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine.

📍 Prologue — White Orchard▶
▶

The Witcher's Forge

  1. Speak to Vesemir at the inn in White Orchard.
  2. Follow the trail of the Wild Hunt using your Witcher Senses.
  3. Defeat the Griffin that has been terrorizing the village.
  4. Use Aard sign to knock the Griffin out of the air during its dive.
  5. Collect the Griffin trophy and return to the Nilfgaardian commander.
▶

Lilac and Gooseberries

  1. Follow Vesemir through the forest toward White Orchard village.
  2. Investigate the abandoned village using Witcher Senses.
  3. Find Yennefer's trail — follow the scent of lilac and gooseberries.
  4. Fight through the Nilfgaardian patrol blocking the road.
  5. Reach the White Orchard inn and speak to the locals about Yennefer.
▶

The Beast of White Orchard

  1. Brew the Griffin Pheromone potion using the ingredients from the herbalist.
  2. Set the trap at the Griffin's nest on the hilltop.
  3. Use the pheromone to lure the Griffin to the trap location.
  4. Fight the Griffin — use Aard to knock it down and Quen for defense.
  5. Collect the Griffin trophy and deliver it to the Nilfgaardian commander.
▶

Imperial Audience

  1. Travel to Vizima for the audience with Emperor Emhyr.
  2. Speak to the Emperor about Ciri's whereabouts.
  3. Receive the mission to find Ciri before the Wild Hunt does.
  4. Meet Yennefer in the palace courtyard.
  5. Depart for Velen with Yennefer.
📍 Velen — No Man's Land▶
▶

Hunting a Witch

  1. Travel to Midcopse village and ask the locals about the witch.
  2. Follow the children's directions to Keira Metz's hut.
  3. Speak to Keira about the Crones and Ciri's trail.
  4. Accept Keira's help in exchange for completing her tasks.
  5. Receive the magic lamp from Keira to investigate the elven ruins.
▶

Wandering in the Dark

  1. Enter the elven ruins beneath Velen with Keira Metz.
  2. Use the magic lamp to reveal the ghosts of elves.
  3. Navigate through the underground tunnels — use Witcher Senses for the path.
  4. Defeat the Golem guardian blocking the inner chamber.
  5. Find the notes left by the mage and escape the ruins.
▶

Ladies of the Wood

  1. Travel to the Crones' shrine in the Crookback Bog.
  2. Speak to the spirit of the Crones to learn about Ciri.
  3. Complete the Crones' task — find the missing children.
  4. Return to the Crones with the information they requested.
  5. Receive the Crones' knowledge of Ciri's location.
▶

Bloody Baron

  1. Travel to Crow's Perch and speak to the Bloody Baron.
  2. Agree to find the Baron's missing wife and daughter in exchange for information about Ciri.
  3. Investigate the Baron's estate using Witcher Senses.
  4. Follow the trail to the abandoned village.
  5. Discover what happened to the Baron's family.
▶

Family Matters

  1. Continue the investigation into the Baron's missing family.
  2. Travel to the Crones' shrine and speak to the spirit.
  3. Find Anna and Tamara — they are in different locations.
  4. Choose how to resolve the Baron's family situation.
  5. Return to the Baron with your findings.
▶

Ciri's Story: The King of the Wolves

  1. Play as Ciri in this flashback sequence.
  2. Escape from the King of the Wolves through the forest.
  3. Use Ciri's blink ability to teleport past obstacles.
  4. Defeat the wolves pursuing you.
  5. Reach safety and end the flashback.
▶

Ciri's Story: The Race

  1. Play as Ciri in this flashback.
  2. Win the horse race through the Velen countryside.
  3. Use Ciri's speed boost ability to stay ahead.
  4. Reach the finish line before your opponent.
  5. End the flashback and return to Geralt's perspective.
▶

A Towerful of Mice

  1. Speak to Keira Metz about the haunted tower on Fyke Isle.
  2. Travel to Fyke Isle and enter the tower.
  3. Use the magic lamp to communicate with the ghost of Annabelle.
  4. Discover the truth about what happened on the island.
  5. Choose how to resolve Annabelle's situation — free her or bind her.
▶

The Whispering Hillock

  1. Travel to the Ancient Oak in Velen.
  2. Speak to the spirit trapped within the tree.
  3. Decide whether to free the spirit or destroy it.
  4. Freeing the spirit leads to the children being saved but Anna cursed.
  5. Destroying the spirit leads to Anna surviving but the children lost.
▶

Return to Crookback Bog

  1. Return to the Crones' shrine after resolving the Whispering Hillock.
  2. Confront the Crones about Ciri's fate.
  3. Fight through the bog creatures defending the shrine.
  4. Rescue Anna from the Crones' curse (if applicable).
  5. Receive the final information about Ciri's location.
📍 Novigrad▶
▶

Novigrad, Closed City

  1. Enter Novigrad through the main gate.
  2. Avoid the witch hunters patrolling the streets.
  3. Make contact with Triss Merigold at her hiding place.
  4. Learn about the Eternal Fire's persecution of mages.
  5. Begin the search for Ciri in Novigrad's underworld.
▶

Broken Flowers

  1. Speak to Dandelion's associates to find his whereabouts.
  2. Visit Molly, Elihal, Rosa var Attre, and Marabella.
  3. Each contact gives a clue about Dandelion's last known location.
  4. Follow the trail to the Rosemary and Thyme tavern.
  5. Discover that Dandelion has been arrested by the witch hunters.
▶

Get Junior

  1. Investigate Whoreson Junior's operations in Novigrad.
  2. Infiltrate the arena to find information about Junior.
  3. Raid Junior's casino and his hideout.
  4. Confront Whoreson Junior — he knows where Ciri is.
  5. Choose to kill Junior or spare him — both advance the quest.
▶

Pyres of Novigrad

  1. Help Triss rescue mages being persecuted by the Eternal Fire.
  2. Infiltrate the witch hunter headquarters.
  3. Free the captured mages from the dungeons.
  4. Escort the mages to the docks for evacuation.
  5. Defeat the witch hunter captain blocking the escape route.
▶

Novigrad Dreaming

  1. Investigate the haunted house in Novigrad.
  2. Use Witcher Senses to find the source of the haunting.
  3. Discover the spirit of a child trapped in the house.
  4. Resolve the haunting by finding the child's toy.
  5. Return to the quest giver with your findings.
▶

A Poet Under Pressure

  1. Locate Dandelion in the Witch Hunter's prison.
  2. Infiltrate the prison using a disguise or bribery.
  3. Free Dandelion from his cell.
  4. Escape the prison with Dandelion.
  5. Speak to Dandelion about Ciri — he knows where she went.
▶

Count Reuven's Treasure

  1. Speak to Dijkstra at the bathhouse.
  2. Help Dijkstra recover his stolen treasury.
  3. Investigate the theft using Witcher Senses.
  4. Track the thieves to their hideout.
  5. Recover the treasury and receive Dijkstra's information about Ciri.
▶

The Play's the Thing

  1. Help Dandelion stage a play at the Rosemary and Thyme.
  2. Recruit actors and gather props for the performance.
  3. Perform the play — choose dialogue options carefully.
  4. Use the play as a distraction to make contact with Priscilla.
  5. Receive information about Ciri's next location.
▶

A Favor for Radovid

  1. Meet King Radovid at his ship in the harbor.
  2. Agree to spy on Philippa Eilhart in exchange for information.
  3. Track Philippa through Novigrad.
  4. Report your findings to Radovid.
  5. Receive Radovid's intelligence about the Wild Hunt.
▶

Destination: Skellige

  1. Arrange passage to Skellige with a ship captain.
  2. Pay the captain's fee or complete a task for him.
  3. Board the ship and depart for Skellige.
  4. Survive the storm at sea.
  5. Arrive in Skellige and begin the search for Ciri.

  5. Free the jarl from the Hym's possession.

  4. Confront the creature responsible.

The Battle of Kaer Morhen

  1. Prepare for the Wild Hunt's assault on Kaer Morhen.
  2. Recruit allies — Triss, Keira, Zoltan, Roche, Ves, and others.
  3. Defend Kaer Morhen against the Wild Hunt's attack.
  4. Protect the keep's gates from being breached.

Blood on the Battlefield

  1. Speak to Ciri after the battle.
  2. Choose how to comfort Ciri — this affects the ending.
  3. Visit Vesemir's grave with Ciri.
  4. Decide whether to take Ciri to the Emperor or to Avallac'h.
  5. Prepare for the final confrontation with the Wild Hunt.

Final Preparations

  5. Escape the island with Ciri.

The Sunstone

  1. Help Yennefer and the Lodge find the Sunstone.
  2. Travel to the underwater ruins.
  3. Retrieve the Sunstone from the ruins.
  4. Return to Yennefer with the Sunstone.
  5. Prepare to use the Sunstone to lure the Wild Hunt.
▶

Child of the Elder Blood

  1. Help Ciri access her Elder Blood powers.
  2. Travel with Ciri to the ritual site.
  3. Protect Ciri during the ritual.
  4. Witness Ciri's transformation.
  5. Prepare for the final battle.
▶

Tedd Deireadh, The Final Age

  1. Confront the Wild Hunt at the ritual site.
  2. Fight through the Wild Hunt's forces.
  3. Defeat Caranthir, the navigator of the Wild Hunt.
  4. Reach Eredin for the final confrontation.
  5. Defeat Eredin, King of the Wild Hunt.
▶

Something Ends, Something Begins

  1. Witness the ending based on your choices throughout the game.
  2. If Ciri survives: she either becomes a Witcher or the Empress.
  3. If Ciri dies: Geralt retrieves her medallion from the White Frost.
  4. Speak to your allies and companions.
  5. The game's main story concludes.
