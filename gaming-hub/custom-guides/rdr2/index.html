Red Dead Redemption 2 — 100% Completion

Complete all 83 main missions across 8 chapters/epilogues, 14 side missions, all stranger encounters, collectibles, and trophies. Honor affects the Chapter 6 ending.

🦴 Collectibles

All collectible categories for 100% completion.

Dinosaur Bones (30): Scattered across the map — complete "A Test of Faith" stranger mission to track them.
Rock Carvings (10): Found on cliff faces and rock formations — complete the "Geology for Beginners" stranger mission.
Dream Catchers (20): Hanging in trees across the map — complete the "Paying Respects" stranger mission.
Legendary Animals (16): One per region — hunt all for the Trapper's legendary gear sets.
Legendary Fish (13): One per body of water — complete the "Fisher of Fish" stranger mission.
Cigarette Cards (144): Found in cigarette packs — complete the full set for a unique reward.
Grave Sites (9): Visit all graves of fallen gang members for the "Paying Respects" trophy.
