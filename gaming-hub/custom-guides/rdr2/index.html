Red Dead Redemption 2 — 100% Completion

Complete all 83 main missions across 8 chapters/epilogues, 14 side missions, all stranger encounters, collectibles, and trophies. Honor affects the Chapter 6 ending.

⚠ Honor Warning: High Honor is required for the best ending in Chapter 6 and unlocks exclusive stranger missions. Maintain High Honor throughout Arthur's story. | Progress saves automatically to your browser.

🤠 Main Story Missions

All 83 missions across Prologue, Chapters 1–6, and both Epilogue parts. Complete all for 100%.

▶

Prologue · 3

  1. After the opening cutscene, follow Dutch and Micah through the snow to Colter.
  2. Eliminate the O'Driscoll Boys in the abandoned town — use cover and Dead Eye.
  3. Search the building for supplies: food, medicine, and ammunition.
  4. Follow Dutch back to the gang's camp to complete the mission.
  5. This mission introduces the core shooting mechanics.
🥇 Gold Medal: No Gold Medal objectives for this mission.
🏆 Gang camp established
  1. Follow Dutch and Charles through the snow on horseback.
  2. Hunt a deer to provide food for the gang — use your bow for a clean kill.
  3. Skin the deer and return to camp with the meat.
  4. Speak with Hosea and other gang members to begin building relationships.
  5. This mission introduces hunting and camp mechanics.
🥇 Gold Medal: No Gold Medal objectives for this mission.
🏆 Hunting introduced
  1. Ride with Dutch, Arthur, and Micah to intercept the O'Driscoll Boys.
  2. Eliminate the O'Driscoll ambush party — use cover on the hillside.
  3. Chase down the fleeing O'Driscolls on horseback.
  4. Interrogate the captured O'Driscoll for information about Colm.
  5. Return to camp to complete the mission.
🥇 Gold Medal Requirements:
  • Take no damage during the shootout at Adler Ranch
  • Loot 6 or more items from Adler Ranch
  • Complete with at least 80% accuracy
🏆 Colm O'Driscoll introduced

🦴 Collectibles

All collectible categories for 100% completion. None are missable in free roam after the story. Cigarette Cards can be purchased from fences if you miss them in the world.

▶

Dinosaur Bones · 30

Collect all 30 for the Jawbone Knife (unique weapon). Part of the "A Test of Faith" stranger mission with Deborah MacGuiness. Bones glow faintly at night.
▶

Rock Carvings · 10

Part of the "Geology for Beginners" stranger mission with Francis Sinclair. Collect all 10 rock carvings and return to his cabin for the reward. Note: The game lists 10 carvings for the stranger mission; however there are additional petroglyphs in the world. All 10 required carvings are listed below.
▶

Dream Catchers · 20

Part of the "Paying Respects" stranger mission. Dream catchers hang in trees. Interact with all 20 to trigger a vision and receive the reward.
▶

Legendary Animals · 16

Hunt all 16 for the Poacher trophy and to craft all Trapper legendary gear sets. Use Eagle Eye to track them. Each animal has a unique pelt that can only be sold to the Trapper.
▶

Legendary Fish · 13

Part of the "Fisher of Fish" stranger mission with Jeremy Gill. Use Special Lures (purchased from Bait Shop) to catch each one. Mail the fish to Jeremy after catching.
▶

Cigarette Cards · 144 (12 sets of 12)

Found in cigarette packs throughout the world, or purchased from fences. Complete all 12 sets of 12 cards each. Mail complete sets to the collector for rewards. Required for 100% completion.
▶

Treasure Maps · 6 hunts

Treasure maps are found on enemies, in chests, or purchased. Each leads to a buried cache of cash and valuables. Complete all for the Treasure Hunter challenge rank.
