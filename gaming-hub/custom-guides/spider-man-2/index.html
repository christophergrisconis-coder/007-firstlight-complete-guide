Marvel's Spider-Man 2 — 100% Completion

Two Spider-Men, one hunter, and a symbiote that wants everything. Insomniac's sequel doubles New York across the East River and hands you Peter and Miles in a single seamless story. This guide covers the full mission chain, every collectible district sweep, the suit tech worth your parts, and the boss fights — including what to do when the black suit stops being fun.

⏱ 25-40 hrs · Accessible (scales to Ultimate) · ⭐ 9.1/10

⚔ Main Missions

▶

Surface Tension — Sandman over ManhattanEasy

📍 Midtown / Financial District · 🎁 The wildest opening in superhero games
  1. Play the swinging tutorial honestly — the new Web Wings and wind tunnels are core traversal, not gimmicks.
  2. Alternate between Peter and Miles as scripted; learn each one's dodge/parry rhythm now (parry is NEW and mandatory later).
  3. Chase the sand plumes up Marko's body, clearing crystallized weak points.
  4. The finale QTE cascade sets the story's tone — Marko's arrest fills the first Marko's Memories collectible hook.
  5. Post-mission, with Brooklyn and Queens added to Manhattan. Sweep a district before advancing.
🎁 The wildest opening in superhero games
▶

The Hunter arrives: Kraven's Great HuntMedium

📍 Citywide, escalating · 🎁 The story's spine — Kraven's bases and hunters
  1. Hunter bases and patrols spawn across districts as the story advances — clear them for tech parts and XP.
  2. Story missions escalate through Kraven's hit list: watch the villain cameos fall one by one — the stakes are real.
  3. Learn parry (L1 timing) against hunter brutes; their red-flash attacks CANNOT be dodged, only parried.
  4. The Lizard hunt chapter (Dr. Connors) is the mid-game showpiece: chase, fight, survive — one of Insomniac's best sequences.
  5. Kraven's interrogation scenes reposition him from joke to genuine threat. He is not the final boss. It gets worse.
🎁 The story's spine — Kraven's bases and hunters
▶

Black Suit Peter: the symbiote arcMedium

📍 Peter's missions, mid-game · 🎁 Symbiote powers — and their price
  1. Symbiote abilities replace Peter's arms tech: tendril barrages, slam AoEs, and the Surge mode that melts crowds.
  2. Notice the writing shift: Peter's quips sharpen into cruelty; MJ and Miles dialogue tracks the descent.
  3. Combat encourages brutality — the suit rewards aggression with healing, making YOU complicit in the power fantasy.
  4. The intervention beat and the bell tower sequence are unmissable story peaks — let them breathe.
  5. Post-symbiote, Anti-Venom powers replace the black suit kit for the endgame. The arc is the game's thesis: play it slow.
🎁 Symbiote powers — and their price
▶

Miles rising: Be Greater, TogetherMedium

📍 Miles's mission chain · 🎁 Evolved venom powers, the Brooklyn Visions arc
  1. Miles's bioelectric venom tree expands: chain venom strikes, venom dash-punches, and the endgame evolution.
  2. The Mr. — the memory realm sequence is a highlight.
  3. Brooklyn Visions side content (see Side Quests) feeds Miles's story directly — do it alongside the main chain.
  4. Miles's cultural beats (the museum mission, Hailey's mission where you PLAY as Hailey) are the game's soul.
  5. By the finale Miles claims the mantle equally — his final power unlock makes him many players' preferred Spider.
🎁 Evolved venom powers, the Brooklyn Visions arc
▶

Venom: New York's last nightHard

📍 Endgame — the city under siege · 🎁 The finale gauntlet
  1. Venom's rampage chapter (playable Venom included) pivots the map: symbiote nests replace hunter bases citywide.
  2. Clear nests with the new sonic-tech abilities — both Spiders get anti-symbiote kits.
  3. The Kraven/Venom collision resolves the Great Hunt with brutal finality.
  4. Final gauntlet: both Spiders, all powers, — swap-combat sequences chain both heroes' abilities in one fight.
  5. Stay through the ending scenes: the family dinner and EVERY teaser. Insomniac plants three sequels' worth of seeds.
🎁 The finale gauntlet

📜 Side Missions

▶

The Flame questline (Yuri returns)Medium

📍 Fire cult sites, citywide chain · 🎁 The best side story — and a legendary post-credit sting
  1. Chain unlocks in waves after story chapters — check the FNSM app between main missions.
  2. Investigate cult sites with Wraith; the tone is a horror-thriller detour from the main story's bombast.
  3. The tanker finale and the cult's true purpose land a genuine gut-punch reveal.
  4. The final scene's reveal about the Flame's identity is essential Spider-Man lore — do NOT skip this chain.
  5. Complete it before the endgame for maximum thematic echo with the symbiote arc.
🎁 The best side story — and a legendary post-credit sting
▶

Brooklyn Visions requestsEasy

📍 Miles's school district · 🎁 Miles's best writing, a homecoming payoff
  1. Requests unlock through Miles's story — answer them promptly; several are timed to story beats emotionally, not mechanically.
  2. The missing student, the bodega cat cameo, and the music teacher chains each resolve in the finale block.
  3. These missions are mostly combat-light: investigation, traversal, and dialogue.
  4. Finishing all requests unlocks the school ceremony scene — bring tissues.
  5. Hailey's solo mission (you play as her, sound design muted to her experience) is one of the boldest sequences in AAA games.
🎁 Miles's best writing, a homecoming payoff
▶

Mysteriums (Mysterio's arcades)Hard (gold ranks)

📍 Queens & Brooklyn · 🎁 Combat mastery challenges, tokens
  1. Each Mysterium is a themed combat puzzle: time attacks, stealth gauntlets, undersea illusions.
  2. Gold ranks demand ability rotation: into finishers, no downtime.
  3. Beck's between-round dialogue quietly runs its own redemption story.
  4. Rewards feed rare tech parts — gold all of them before the endgame gear checks.
  5. The final Mysterium resolves Beck's arc with an unexpected sincerity. Worth every retry.
🎁 Combat mastery challenges, tokens
▶

EMF Experiments, drones & Howard's pigeonsEasy

📍 Citywide oddities · 🎁 Tech parts, upgrades, and the best 5 minutes in the game
  1. EMF Experiments (Peter): Harry's foundation tech puzzles — clean energy, plant drones, neighborhood science. They feed the story's Emily-May Foundation theme.
  2. Unidentified Targets: chase the mysterious drones for a hidden safehouse payoff.
  3. Howard returns (from SM1's pigeon quest) with a quiet, devastating little finale on a rooftop. Play it. Feel things.
  4. Photo ops and science trophies — each district's misc markers take minutes and pay tech parts.
  5. None of this is filler; Insomniac's side content hit rate is absurd.
🎁 Tech parts, upgrades, and the best 5 minutes in the game

💎 Collectibles

▶

Spider-Bots (42)Easy

📍 All districts — glowing backpack-sized drones · 🎁 Tech parts + a multiversal payoff scene
  1. They appear on the map per district after unlocking district scans (clear any activity there).
  2. Each bot is a variant homage — Spider-Punk, Bombastic Bag-Man, the works. Collect visibly styled favorites first, why not.
  3. Grab them with a web-pull; no combat involved.
  4. District sweep strategy: clear bots while traveling between story missions — never fast-travel past collectibles early.
  5. Return all 42 to the delivery point (marked after completion) for a delightful multiverse cameo scene.
🎁 Tech parts + a multiversal payoff scene
▶

Marko's Memories (14 sand crystals)Easy

📍 Sand deposits, citywide · 🎁 Sandman's story, tech parts, a father's apology
  1. Unlocked after Surface Tension; sand whirlwind markers show on the map per district.
  2. Each crystal triggers a Marko voice memory and a small sand-enemy wave.
  3. The chain maps Marko's breakdown geographically — toward the final sites.
  4. Collect all 14 and return to the marked finale site for the resolution scene with Flint.
  5. One of the sequel's quietest, best stories, hidden in a collectible chain.
🎁 Sandman's story, tech parts, a father's apology
▶

Prowler Stashes (9)Easy

📍 Aaron Davis's caches, citywide · 🎁 Tech parts + Miles and Aaron's reconciliation
  1. Unlocked via Miles's story; stash markers appear on district maps.
  2. Each stash is a mini-puzzle: trace the power conduits, trigger the hidden mechanisms, open the cache.
  3. Aaron's recorded messages track his arc from criminal to uncle trying to be better.
  4. Stashes pay rare tech parts — Miles's suit tech eats these.
  5. The final stash conversation between Miles and Aaron closes the Prowler chapter with grace.
🎁 Tech parts + Miles and Aaron's reconciliation
▶

Tech Parts, — the upgrade economyEasy

📍 All activities · 🎁 Suits, gadgets, suit tech tiers
  1. Suit tech upgrades (Health, Damage, Focus, Traversal trees) eat Tech Parts — prioritize Health and Focus early on higher difficulties.
  2. Gadget upgrades transform crowd control: the Web Grabber's black-hole pull is the best purchase in the game.
  3. Hero Tokens gate the premium suits — — clear each district's full activity set for its token payout.
  4. 68 suits exist across both Spiders with color variants; suits are cosmetic only — wear whatever brings joy.
  5. By endgame you'll cap everything naturally; don't hoard, spend on sight.
🎁 Suits, gadgets, suit tech tiers

⚔ Suits & Abilities

▶

Anti-Venom Suit (Peter)Story

📍 Endgame story unlock · 🎁 The symbiote kit, redeemed
  1. Unlocks automatically in the endgame chapters after the bell tower arc.
  2. Anti-Venom abilities mirror the black suit's kit with anti-symbiote bonuses — nest clearing becomes trivial.
  3. Rotation: open with the AoE lift, chain into Focus finishers.
  4. Against symbiote enemies, their tendril attacks.
  5. The suit itself (white symbiote look) is one of the best designs in the game. Function met fashion.
🎁 The symbiote kit, redeemed
▶

Evolved Venom Suit (Miles)Story

📍 Miles's final story unlock · 🎁 Peak Miles — his own identity, powers included
  1. Unlocks with Miles's final story beats.
  2. His venom chains hit harder and jump further between enemies — crowd fights melt.
  3. The associated suit (his self-designed look) sparked discourse; his community-designed alternates satisfy purists.
  4. Rotation: venom dash opener, thunderburst mid-crowd, web-throw the stragglers.
  5. Miles's Focus generation outpaces Peter's — he sustains through Ultimate difficulty more comfortably.
🎁 Peak Miles — his own identity, powers included
▶

Web Wings & the Wind TunnelsEasy

📍 Traversal system, always on · 🎁 The fastest traversal in the series
  1. Trigger wings mid-air with the dedicated button and chain into wind tunnels (green current lines across the map).
  2. Rivers stop being dead zones — the East River crossings use dedicated high-speed currents.
  3. Upgrade the traversal tech tree first if you love movement: slingshot launches and dive speed transform flow.
  4. Wing-dive-wing rhythm (dive to build speed, deploy to convert) beats pure swinging for distance.
  5. The Slingshot launch points (district devices) yeet you at highway speeds. Fast travel exists but you will never use it. That's the point.
🎁 The fastest traversal in the series
▶

Gadget mastery: the Web Grabber metaMedium

📍 Gadget wheel · 🎁 Crowd control that trivializes Ultimate difficulty
  1. WEB GRABBER: a web vortex that yanks whole crowds into one webbed clump — upgrade it fully, immediately.
  2. Clumped enemies take shared damage: one heavy attack into the clump staggers everyone.
  3. Upgraded Web Shooters still rule single-target: webbed-to-wall enemies are OUT of the fight permanently.
  4. Concussion Burst clears breathing room; Ricochet Web handles ranged rows.
  5. Gadget + parry rhythm is the Ultimate difficulty survival kit: Grabber pull → AoE ability → parry the brute → repeat.
🎁 Crowd control that trivializes Ultimate difficulty

📋 Boss Fights

▶

Kraven KravinoffHard

📍 Story confrontations (multiple) · 🎁 Story progress — and respect
  1. Kraven's red-flash attacks are parry-only — the fights teach the parry system by force.
  2. His knife combos chain 3-4 hits: parry the LAST hit for the punish window, dodging the rest.
  3. Ranged phases (rifle, traps) demand web-zip approaches through cover — straight lines eat bullets.
  4. The greenhouse duel against symbiote Peter is the fight of the game: aggression meets aggression, and the suit's healing lets you play greedy exactly once.
  5. Watch his tells, not his body: shoulder dip = lunge, blade flip = combo string. He duels like a fighting game character.
🎁 Story progress — and respect
▶

The LizardMedium

📍 Upper West Side → the chase · 🎁 Story progress, Connors's humanity
  1. Phase 1 apartment: dodge his tail sweeps, punish the wall-lunge recovery — his face when he pins you (QTE).
  2. The chase sequence: trust the traversal, with dive momentum, and don't grind walls.
  3. Sewer phase: he stalks YOU in the dark — horror rules; keep repositioning.
  4. Final phase targets his regeneration: burst damage during his stagger windows, gadgets on cooldown.
  5. Connors mid-fight dialogue matters to the symbiote arc later — Peter promised Harry. Remember that.
🎁 Story progress, Connors's humanity
▶

VenomHard

📍 Endgame · 🎁 The finale
  1. Phase rotation: Venom swaps aggro between Peter and Miles — the game hot-swaps you; keep both ability wheels warm mentally.
  2. His tendril slam grid covers the arena floor — verticality is safety; reset height between exchanges.
  3. Symbiote adds spawn at health thresholds: his pressure.
  4. The unblockable grabs (red flash + roar) need pre-emptive distance, not reaction dodges.
  5. Final phase strips it to raw combat rhythm — parry, punish, ability dump. Let the story land.
🎁 The finale
▶

Sandman (opening kaiju)Easy

📍 Surface Tension, Midtown · 🎁 Tutorial mastery
  1. Sand tornado phases: web-wing through the wind tunnels his storm creates — the game teaches wind riding here.
  2. Crystallized amber weak points glow on his limbs: zip to them, burst them, ride the collapse.
  3. Mini-Sandmen waves between phases teach crowd basics: web-throw sand constructs into each other, they shatter.
  4. Both-Spiders swap sections preview the finale's structure.
  5. On replay (NG+), this fight is a speedrun playground — the set piece holds up on the tenth run.
🎁 Tutorial mastery

💡 Pro Tips & Secrets

▶

Parry early, parry alwaysMedium

📍 Combat settings & practice · 🎁 Survival on Spectacular+ difficulties
  1. Red flash = parry REQUIRED (undodgeable), blue flash = dodge works. Learn the color language now.
  2. Parry timing is generous; the followup window is the real skill — heavy attack immediately after.
  3. Hunter brutes and Kraven force the mechanic; practice on hunter bases before story duels.
  4. Perfect dodge still exists and feeds Focus — mix both, don't replace one with the other.
  5. Accessibility sliders can widen parry timing without shame. The game respects your settings.
🎁 Survival on Spectacular+ difficulties
▶

Swap heroes constantly (the city reacts)Easy

📍 Map menu, anywhere · 🎁 Hidden dialogue, crime variety, both upgrade tracks
  1. Swap via the map (near-instant): the other Spider is always doing something — the transition scenes are little gifts.
  2. District crimes vary by hero; both have separate ability trees fed by shared XP, so neither falls behind.
  3. Certain FNSM app requests are hero-locked — check as both.
  4. Miles's Focus sustain suits harder difficulties; Peter's gadget-heavy style suits crowds — swap per activity, not per session.
  5. Late-game city ambience changes per hero after story beats. Walk around as each. Listen.
🎁 Hidden dialogue, crime variety, both upgrade tracks
▶

New Game+ and the Ultimate LevelsHard

📍 Post-launch update features · 🎁 NG+ progression, level 60 cap, new suits
  1. NG+ carries all suits, tech and abilities into a fresh story run.
  2. Ultimate Levels (post-cap) pay cosmetic and challenge rewards.
  3. The trophy-tied gauntlets and time trials return via the update's challenge modes.
  4. Ultimate difficulty + NG+ is the intended mastery lap: parry-perfect play, full gadget rotations.
  5. Save your favorite save slot pre-finale; the endgame state locks some story-window activities.
🎁 NG+ progression, level 60 cap, new suits
▶

Accessibility & photo mode are eliteEasy

📍 Settings menu · 🎁 The game, your way — and gorgeous screenshots
  1. Game speed sliders (70/50/30%) work in ANY mode — combat puzzles become gentle or surgical at will.
  2. Screen-shake, camera wobble and motion blur all tune independently — swing-sickness is solvable.
  3. Photo mode: lighting rigs, stickers, and both heroes posable — the vista dives over the East River print like posters.
  4. Chapter select post-completion replays any mission with your full kit.
  5. The bodega cat suit exists. It rides in a backpack. it is visible in cutscenes. Priorities.
🎁 The game, your way — and gorgeous screenshots
