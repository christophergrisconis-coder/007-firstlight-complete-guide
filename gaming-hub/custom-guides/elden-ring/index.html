Elden Ring — 100% Completion

FromSoftware's open-world masterpiece. Rise, Tarnished, and journey through the Lands Between to restore the Elden Ring and become Elden Lord. This guide covers the critical path, the most important questlines, every Golden Seed route, legendary armaments and how to bring down the hardest bosses in gaming.

⏱ 60-120 hrs · Very Hard · ⭐ 9.6/10

⚔ Main Quests

▶

Defeat Margit, the Fell OmenHard

📍 Stormveil Castle approach, Limgrave · 🎁 12,000 Runes, Talisman Pouch
  1. Level to at least 20-25 before attempting. Farm the Highroad Cave and clear Gatefront Ruins for the Whetstone Knife and map fragment.
  2. Buy Margit's Shackle from Patches (found in Murkwater Cave) — it pins Margit in place twice during phase one.
  3. Summon Sorcerer Rogier at the fog gate for extra damage and aggro relief.
  4. Stay behind him and punish his slow hammer slam. When he pulls out the golden hammer in phase 2, back off and only attack after his 3-hit combos.
  5. Spirit Ash Jellyfish or Wolves keep him distracted — cast them BEFORE crossing the fog gate.
🎁 12,000 Runes, Talisman Pouch
▶

Defeat Godrick the GraftedHard

📍 Stormveil Castle throne room · 🎁 Godrick's Great Rune, Remembrance of the Grafted
  1. Take the side entrance past the Rampart Tower grace (talk to Gostoc to open the main gate or sneak the side path).
  2. Summon Nepheli Loux just before the arena — she survives most of the fight.
  3. Phase 1: dodge INTO his spin attacks and punish after the ground slam.
  4. Phase 2 begins at 50% HP: he grafts a dragon head. Stay on his left leg, away from the flame arm. Roll through the fire sweep and get 2-3 hits.
  5. Activate his Great Rune at the Divine Tower of Limgrave (across the bridge east of Stormveil) to actually use it.
🎁 Godrick's Great Rune, Remembrance of the Grafted
▶

Defeat Rennala, Queen of the Full MoonMedium

📍 Raya Lucaria Academy, Liurnia · 🎁 Rennala's Great Rune, Remembrance of the Full Moon Queen, Respec unlock
  1. Get the Academy Glintstone Key from the sleeping dragon Smarag west of the academy (sneak, grab it behind him).
  2. Phase 1: three students glow gold and chant — hit the glowing ones to drop Rennala's barrier, then unload on her. Repeat 2-3 times.
  3. Phase 2: she fights with full moon sorceries. Rush her immediately — she is slow to start casting.
  4. Dodge the Rennala's Full Moon spell by sprinting sideways, not rolling.
  5. Her summons (dragon, giant) can be outrun; stay aggressive and she rarely completes them.
🎁 Rennala's Great Rune, Remembrance of the Full Moon Queen, Respec unlock
▶

Defeat Starscourge RadahnVery Hard

📍 Redmane Castle, Caelid (Radahn Festival) · 🎁 Radahn's Great Rune, Remembrance of the Starscourge, unlocks Nokron
  1. Trigger the Radahn Festival by reaching Altus Plateau OR progressing Ranni's quest, then teleport via the Redmane Castle grace.
  2. Use every gold summon sign repeatedly — they respawn after dying. Keep 3+ allies on him at all times.
  3. Opening: hide behind debris to avoid his sniper arrows, or spur Torrent in a serpentine sprint toward him.
  4. Fight on horseback. Hit-and-run his legs; resummon allies from the signs scattered around the arena.
  5. At 50% HP he flies into space and crashes down as a meteor — sprint away or perpendicular on Torrent to dodge the impact, then resume hit-and-run.
🎁 Radahn's Great Rune, Remembrance of the Starscourge, unlocks Nokron
▶

Defeat Morgott, the Omen KingHard

📍 Elden Throne, Leyndell Royal Capital · 🎁 Morgott's Great Rune, Remembrance of the Omen King
  1. Reach Leyndell via the Grand Lift of Dectus (both Dectus Medallion halves: Fort Haight, Limgrave + Fort Faroth, Dragonbarrow) or through the Ruin-Strewn Precipice.
  2. Summon Melina at the fog gate (her sign is on the stairs) plus your Spirit Ash — Mimic Tear excels here.
  3. His holy dagger rain is dodged by sprinting toward him diagonally.
  4. Phase 2: he ignites his sword — his 'Cursed Blade' lunge has huge range, roll INTO it late.
  5. Punish after his triple-slash into spear-throw combo; that's your biggest window.
🎁 Morgott's Great Rune, Remembrance of the Omen King
▶

The Forge of the Giants & Crumbling Farum AzulaVery Hard

📍 Mountaintops of the Giants → Farum Azula · 🎁 Access to endgame, Remembrance of the Fire Giant & Black Blade
  1. Get the Rold Medallion from Melina after Morgott, then take the Grand Lift of Rold to the Mountaintops.
  2. Fire Giant: fight on Torrent. Phase 1 target the left ankle brace. Phase 2 he tears off his own leg — stay behind his hands and avoid the rolling attack.
  3. At the Forge, rest and choose to be burned — you awake in Crumbling Farum Azula.
  4. In Farum Azula, don't miss the Dragon Temple Altar grace; Maliketh's fog awaits past the Godskin Duo (use Sleep pots — both Godskins are sleep-vulnerable!).
  5. Maliketh phase 2 inflicts Destined Death (max HP reduction). Stay point-blank under him; most of his aerial arcs whiff at hugging distance. Blasphemous Blade or Mimic Tear trivializes it.
🎁 Access to endgame, Remembrance of the Fire Giant & Black Blade
▶

Become Elden Lord — Radagon & the Elden BeastExtreme

📍 Fractured Marika, Leyndell, Ashen Capital · 🎁 Elden Remembrance, choice of endings
  1. Radagon is weak to physical and fire; he is HEAVILY resistant to holy. Swap off any holy weapons now.
  2. Dodge his teleport slam by rolling the moment golden light flashes at your feet.
  3. Save your Spirit Ash (Mimic Tear) for the start of Radagon so it carries into Elden Beast.
  4. Elden Beast: sprint to it when it swims away — chase on foot along its glowing side. Patch 1.12+ lets you summon Torrent here: use him.
  5. During Elden Stars (golden rain), run TOWARD the beast's tail and keep circling. Punish after its triple ring attack. Choose your ending at Fractured Marika.
🎁 Elden Remembrance, choice of endings

📜 Side Quests

▶

Ranni the Witch (Age of Stars ending)Long / Complex

📍 Starts at Ranni's Rise, Three Sisters, Liurnia · 🎁 Dark Moon Greatsword, Age of Stars ending
  1. Meet Ranni at Ranni's Rise (northwest Liurnia, behind Caria Manor). Pledge service to her.
  2. Speak to Blaidd, Seluvis, and Iji in the tower, then defeat Radahn to open Nokron.
  3. In Nokron, get the Fingerslayer Blade from the chest in the Night's Sacred Ground and bring it to Ranni.
  4. Use the Carian Inverted Statue she gives you in the Carian Study Hall, cross to the Divine Tower of Liurnia, and collect the Cursemark of Death.
  5. Travel through Renna's Rise portal → Ainsel River, find the miniature Ranni doll, talk to it at graces 3 times, kill the Baleful Shadow, and receive the Discarded Palace Key.
  6. Unlock the chest in Rennala's arena for the Dark Moon Ring. Descend Lake of Rot → beat Astel, Naturalborn of the Void → place the ring at the Moonlight Altar's cathedral. Kiss the finger, get the Dark Moon Greatsword outside.
🎁 Dark Moon Greatsword, Age of Stars ending
▶

Millicent's PilgrimageLong

📍 Starts at Church of the Plague, Caelid · 🎁 Rotten Winged Sword Insignia OR Unalloyed Gold Needle (Malenia's flower)
  1. Talk to Gowry in Sellia. Retrieve the Unalloyed Gold Needle from Commander O'Neil (swamp of Aeonia, Caelid).
  2. Give the needle to Gowry, rest, then speak to Millicent at the Church of the Plague and exhaust dialogue.
  3. Meet her again at Erdtree-Gazing Hill (Altus), then atop the Shaded Castle after giving her the Valkyrie's Prosthesis (found in the castle).
  4. She appears at Windmill Village (Dominula) after you beat the Godskin Apostle there.
  5. Final choice at the Haligtree, Elphael: invade her (Rotten Winged Sword Insignia — huge attack buff) or aid her against her sisters (get the needle to cure Malenia's rot and unlock Miquella's Needle in the DLC path).
🎁 Rotten Winged Sword Insignia OR Unalloyed Gold Needle (Malenia's flower)
▶

Fia, Deathbed CompanionMedium

📍 Roundtable Hold · 🎁 Fia's Mist, Duelist ending (Age of Duskborn)
  1. Talk to Fia in Roundtable Hold, let her hold you, receive Baldachin's Blessing (using it removes the HP debuff).
  2. After getting a Great Rune, she gives you the Weathered Dagger — give it to D, Hunter of the Dead.
  3. After Radahn's fall, D is found dead; take his twinned armor. Fia disappears to Deeproot Depths (reach via Nokron's Siofra Aqueduct after beating Valiant Gargoyles).
  4. In Deeproot Depths, defeat Fia's champions at the deathbed. Give her the Cursemark of Death (from Ranni's quest, Divine Tower of Liurnia).
  5. Sleep, enter her dream, defeat Lichdragon Fortissax. Wake and collect the Mending Rune of the Death-Prince for the Duskborn ending.
🎁 Fia's Mist, Duelist ending (Age of Duskborn)
▶

Iron Fist AlexanderMedium

📍 First met stuck in a hole, Stormhill · 🎁 Alexander's Innards, Shard of Alexander (best skill-damage talisman)
  1. Free him near Stormhill by hitting his back (charged heavy works). Exhaust dialogue.
  2. Meet him at the Radahn Festival and summon him for the fight.
  3. Find him stuck again near the Carian Study Hall lakeshore (Liurnia east bluff) — coat him with Oil Pot to free him (craft with the Nomadic Warrior's Cookbook 17 from Seluvis's Rise area).
  4. He bathes in lava at Mt. Gelmir near Fort Laiedd — talk to him there.
  5. Final duel: Dragon Temple, Crumbling Farum Azula. Defeat him honorably for the Shard of Alexander (+15% skill damage) and his innards for Jar-Bairn in Jarburg.
🎁 Alexander's Innards, Shard of Alexander (best skill-damage talisman)

💎 Collectibles

▶

Golden Seeds (30 total, need ~26)Easy-Medium

📍 Golden saplings across all regions · 🎁 +1 Sacred Flask charge each tier
  1. Limgrave quick 3: Third Church of Marika (east), Stormhill road fork before Margit, Fort Haight west road.
  2. Liurnia: outside the Academy's south gate, Church of Vows garden, road north of Caria Manor.
  3. Altus has the densest cluster: the Sealed Tunnel area, outer Leyndell wall sapling (2 seeds), Windmill Village road.
  4. Boss-drop seeds: Ulcerated Tree Spirits in Mt. Gelmir's Minor Erdtree cave and Leyndell sewers each drop one.
  5. Endgame: Miquella's Haligtree has a 3-seed sapling at the Prayer Room. You need 26 seeds for all 12 flask charges — you can miss a few.
🎁 +1 Sacred Flask charge each tier
▶

Sacred Tears (12 total)Easy

📍 Statues of Marika in ruined churches · 🎁 + Flask healing potency per tear
  1. Limgrave: Third Church of Marika (northeast), Fourth Church (Weeping Peninsula west), Church of Pilgrimage (Peninsula north).
  2. Liurnia: Church of Vows (east), Bellum Church (north road), Church of Inhibition (northeast, Frenzy Tower area).
  3. Caelid: Church of the Plague (Millicent's spot). Altus: Stormcaller Church, Second Church of Marika.
  4. Mt. Gelmir: First Church of Marika (northwest lava field).
  5. Mountaintops: Church of Repose (east), First Church of Marika (Consecrated Snowfield, portal from the Yelough Anix area).
🎁 + Flask healing potency per tear
▶

Memory Stones (8 total)Medium

📍 Rises (wizard towers) and key bosses · 🎁 +1 spell slot each
  1. Buy one outright from Twin Maiden Husks at Roundtable Hold (3,000 runes).
  2. Oridys's Rise (Weeping Peninsula) and Testu's Rise (Liurnia): solve the 'seek three wise beasts' turtle puzzles.
  3. Converted Tower (Liurnia lake) puzzle: perform the 'Erudition' gesture in front of the statue while wearing a Glintstone Crown. Chest at top.
  4. Boss drops: Red Wolf of the Champion (Gelmir Hero's Grave) and Demi-Human Queen Maggie (Hermit Village).
  5. Lenne's Rise and Chelona's Rise (Ranni's quest area) hold the last two — Chelona's requires the Ranni questline turtle hunt around Moonlight Altar.
🎁 +1 spell slot each
▶

Larval Tears (18 total)Medium

📍 Disguised enemies & merchants · 🎁 One full character respec each (at Rennala)
  1. Agheel Lake south (Limgrave): kill the lone Giant Lobster-looking runebear disguised as a wandering noble — it drops one.
  2. Village of the Albinaurics (Liurnia): the docile wolf on the ledge is a mimic — one tear.
  3. Buy from the Nomadic Merchant in Siofra River (2 in stock, 3,000 each) and Pidia in Caria Manor.
  4. Caelid: the giant crow near the Caelem Ruins disguised soldier drops one.
  5. Nokron, Eternal City has 3 in the mimic silver-tear pools near the Mimic Tear boss arena.
🎁 One full character respec each (at Rennala)
▶

Legendary Talismans (8 total)Hard

📍 Hidden catacombs, towers and quest rewards · 🎁 Trophy/Achievement + best-in-slot builds
  1. Radagon Icon (faster casting): chest atop the Debate Parlor rafters, Raya Lucaria — jump from the moving lift.
  2. Erdtree's Favor +2 (HP/Stamina/Equip): Leyndell Ashen Capital, on the tree branch near the Forbidden Lands ladder.
  3. Godfrey Icon (charged attack +15%): beat Godefroy the Grafted in the Golden Lineage Evergaol (Altus, needs Stonesword Key).
  4. Marika's Soreseal (+5 to 4 stats): Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree, in a room behind a Stonesword Key imp statue.
  5. Old Lord's Talisman (spell duration): Crumbling Farum Azula chest; Dragoncrest Greatshield +2 (physical -20%): Elphael balcony; Moon of Nokstella (+2 memory): Ainsel River chest under the throne; Ancestral Spirit's Horn (FP on kill): trade Regal Ancestor Remembrance.
🎁 Trophy/Achievement + best-in-slot builds

⚔ Unique Items & Weapons

▶

Mimic Tear Ashes (best Spirit Summon)Medium

📍 Night's Sacred Ground, Nokron · 🎁 Summons a clone of your full build
  1. Defeat Radahn to open the path to Nokron (crater south of Fort Haight).
  2. Drop through the crater's floating ruins carefully — follow the graces to Nokron's main street.
  3. From the Ancestral Woods grace, ride the ledge path south past the spirit deer to Night's Sacred Ground.
  4. The ashes sit in a chest behind an imp statue door (1 Stonesword Key) beneath the gazebo.
  5. Upgrade it with Ghost Glovewort at Roundtable Hold — at +10 it's a second you. Equip your best gear BEFORE summoning; it copies your current equipment.
🎁 Summons a clone of your full build
▶

Blasphemous BladeHard

📍 Trade Rykard's Remembrance · 🎁 Faith greatsword that heals on every kill and with its skill
  1. Reach Volcano Manor (Altus/Mt. Gelmir) — join the Manor and do the first two assassination contracts, or climb Mt. Gelmir manually.
  2. Take the Manor drawing-room door that opens after contracts (or the hidden wall in the Prison Town Church) to reach the Temple of Eiglay.
  3. Defeat the God-Devouring Serpent / Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy — use the provided Serpent-Hunter greatspear lying at the fog gate.
  4. Take the Remembrance of the Blasphemous to Enia at Roundtable Hold.
  5. Trade it for the Blasphemous Blade. Needs 22 STR / 15 DEX / 21 FAI. Upgrade with Somber stones.
🎁 Faith greatsword that heals on every kill and with its skill
▶

Moonveil KatanaMedium

📍 Gael Tunnel, Caelid · 🎁 INT katana with a screen-clearing quickdraw skill
  1. Enter Gael Tunnel from the west Caelid cliff entrance (border of Limgrave/Caelid ravine).
  2. Fight through the miners — they resist physical, so use magic or fire.
  3. Boss: Magma Wyrm. Stay at its side, punish after the lava spew, and watch the tail sweep.
  4. It drops the Moonveil directly. Requires 12 STR / 18 DEX / 23 INT.
  5. Weapon skill: L2 then R1 (light) or R2 (heavy) — the heavy version does massive posture damage to bosses.
🎁 INT katana with a screen-clearing quickdraw skill
▶

Bolt of GransaxMedium

📍 Leyndell, Royal Capital · 🎁 Legendary lightning greatspear
  1. In Leyndell, from the Avenue Balcony grace, drop to the main avenue and head toward the giant dragon-spear monument.
  2. Climb the wing of the massive fallen dragon statue (Gransax) from the West Capital Rampart side.
  3. Walk carefully up the wing onto the spear itself.
  4. The Bolt of Gransax sits at the spear's hilt as a glowing pickup.
  5. GRAB IT BEFORE BURNING THE ERDTREE. Requires 20 STR / 40 DEX. Its Ancient Lightning Spear skill melts dragon and Farum Azula enemies.
🎁 Legendary lightning greatspear
▶

Rivers of BloodHard

📍 Church of Repose, Mountaintops of the Giants · 🎁 Bleed katana with the Corpse Piler skill
  1. Progress to the Mountaintops of the Giants via the Grand Lift of Rold.
  2. Head east from the Zamor Ruins toward the Church of Repose across the snow bridge.
  3. Bloody Finger Okina invades as you near the church — he uses the very katana he drops and can two-combo you.
  4. Fight him near the church pillars to block his reach; he's parryable and staggers to jump attacks.
  5. Kill him for Rivers of Blood (12 STR / 18 DEX / 20 ARC). Corpse Piler (L2) stacks blood loss absurdly fast. Grab the Sacred Tear inside the church too.
🎁 Bleed katana with the Corpse Piler skill

📋 Boss Strategies

▶

Malenia, Blade of MiquellaExtreme

📍 Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree · 🎁 Remembrance of the Rot Goddess (Hand of Malenia / Scarlet Aeonia)
  1. Reach her via the Haligtree Secret Medallion halves (Village of the Albinaurics + Castle Sol) → Consecrated Snowfield → Ordina puzzle → Miquella's Haligtree → all of Elphael.
  2. Rule 1: never trade hits. Her lifesteal punishes greed. Use a +10 Mimic Tear but expect her to heal off it.
  3. Waterfowl Dance (the flurry): at range, SPRINT away from the first flurry, dodge INTO the second, roll through the third. At point-blank, run behind her first wind-up. Practice this — it's the whole fight.
  4. Phase 2 (Goddess of Rot): she opens with Scarlet Aeonia — sprint backward immediately, the flower AoE inflicts instant rot. Keep Preserving Boluses hotbarred.
  5. Frost + bleed weapons interrupt her heal rhythm. Prosthesis-Wearer Heirloom + jump attacks stagger her reliably. Her stance breaks fast — go for ripostes.
🎁 Remembrance of the Rot Goddess (Hand of Malenia / Scarlet Aeonia)
▶

Mohg, Lord of BloodVery Hard

📍 Mohgwyn Palace (underground) · 🎁 Remembrance of the Blood Lord, Mohg's Great Rune
  1. Reach Mohgwyn via Varré's quest reward, the Pureblood Knight's Medal, or the Sending Gate in the Consecrated Snowfield.
  2. Get the Purifying Crystal Tear by defeating Eleonora, Violet Bloody Finger at the Second Church of Marika (Altus, Yura's quest). Mix it in your physick.
  3. His 'Nihil! Nihil! Nihil!' ritual at phase change deals 3 massive blood bursts — drinking the Purifying Crystal physick negates the entire ritual damage.
  4. Phase 1: stay on his left, punish the trident slam. His bloodflame trails linger — reposition constantly.
  5. Phase 2 he flies: use ranged or wait for his landing slam. Bleed does NOT work on him; frost and physical do.
🎁 Remembrance of the Blood Lord, Mohg's Great Rune
▶

Dragonlord PlacidusaxVery Hard

📍 Hidden arena, Crumbling Farum Azula · 🎁 Remembrance of the Dragonlord
  1. From the Beside the Great Bridge grace in Farum Azula, ride the lift down, exit to the ledge where dragons circle.
  2. Find the missing-floor ledge south of the church ruin — 'lie down' at the glowing spot on the protruding platform to warp to his era.
  3. Stay under his chest between the two necks — most breath attacks miss there.
  4. When he vanishes into lightning, SPRINT — he teleports behind you with a claw dive. Listen for the crack.
  5. His golden lightning nova requires two well-timed rolls. Melt his feet; his posture breaks for a massive crown riposte.
🎁 Remembrance of the Dragonlord
▶

Godskin DuoVery Hard (or trivial with sleep)

📍 Dragon Temple, Farum Azula · 🎁 Ash of War: Black Flame Tornado, Smithing-Stone Bell Bearing
  1. Craft Sleep Pots with the Fevor's Cookbook 2 (Academy of Raya Lucaria, hanging corpse); Trina's Lily grows all over Liurnia.
  2. Both Godskins are extremely sleep-vulnerable: 2 pots put one down. Sleep the Noble (fat one) first, burst the Apostle.
  3. Use the pillars — the Noble's rolling attack breaks on them, buying free windows.
  4. Keep your Mimic Tear alive; it splits aggro perfectly here.
  5. When one dies, a replacement spawns after ~30s. Re-sleep the survivor and finish the shared HP bar before getting sandwiched.
🎁 Ash of War: Black Flame Tornado, Smithing-Stone Bell Bearing

💡 Pro Tips & Secrets

▶

Rune farming: 40k/minute at MohgwynEasy

📍 Mohgwyn Palace approach · 🎁 Fast levels
  1. Unlock the Palace Approach Ledge-Road grace in Mohgwyn Palace.
  2. From the grace, turn around and shoot the giant crow near the cliff edge with one arrow.
  3. It charges, falls off the cliff, and awards ~11,000 runes (with Gold Scarab talisman: ~13,000). Reset at grace, repeat.
  4. Alternatively AoE the sleeping albinauric field ahead for ~35-50k per 2-minute loop.
  5. Equip Gold Scarab (Abandoned Cave, Caelid) and use Gold-Pickled Fowl Foot to stack +50% rune gain.
🎁 Fast levels
▶

Get Torrent's double-jump routes earlyEasy

📍 Global · 🎁 Sequence breaks
  1. You can ride to the Grand Lift of Dectus and beeline Altus at level 1 if you grab both medallion halves (Fort Haight + Fort Faroth).
  2. Spiritspring jumps (wind pillars) often bypass entire dungeon gates — look for them near cliffs.
  3. The Fourth Church of Marika (Weeping Peninsula) has a portal straight to Mohgwyn's region entrance — great for early rune farming.
  4. Dragon-Burnt Ruins cellar chest in Limgrave is a TRAP that teleports you to Sellia Crystal Tunnel in Caelid — but it's a legitimate early route to grab Somber stones and the Moonveil area.
  5. Never fight what you can outrun. Grab loot on horseback and go.
🎁 Sequence breaks
▶

Stock Stonesword Keys — never skip an imp statueEasy

📍 Global · 🎁 Hidden dungeons & best talismans
  1. Buy 3 early from the Twin Maiden Husks (Roundtable) and 1 from Kalé in the Church of Elleh.
  2. Priority doors: Fringefolk Hero's Grave (2 keys, tutorial area — Dragon Communion Seal + Erdtree's Favor), Night's Sacred Ground (Mimic Tear), Summonwater Ruins (Green Turtle).
  3. The Roundtable Hold's own imp door hides the Crepus's Vial and Assassin items.
  4. Keys are finite-ish per playthrough region but there are ~50 total for ~30 doors — you can afford most, just not blindly.
  5. Check behind every waterfall and prayerful corpse pile — keys love hiding there.
🎁 Hidden dungeons & best talismans
▶

The Great-Jar's secret & colosseumsHard

📍 Caelid north / Limgrave · 🎁 Great-Jar's Arsenal talisman (+19% equip load)
  1. Ride north through Caelid's Dragonbarrow to the giant living jar at the ravine's end (near Divine Tower of Caelid).
  2. Three red summon signs appear before him — each summons a phantom based on real player builds.
  3. Fight them ONE at a time; rest at the grace between duels to reset flasks (progress keeps).
  4. Cheese: fight them at the ravine edge — phantoms happily walk off cliffs.
  5. Reward talisman lets you wear heavy armor and still medium-roll — core for strength builds.
🎁 Great-Jar's Arsenal talisman (+19% equip load)
