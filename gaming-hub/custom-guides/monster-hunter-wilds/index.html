Monster Hunter Wilds — 100% Completion

The Forbidden Lands open before you: living deserts, storm-wracked plains, and the white ghost Arkveld at the heart of it all. Capcom's biggest hunt yet pairs a seamless open world with the Seikret mount, Focus Mode wound combat, and dual-weapon loadouts. This guide covers the full story hunt chain, High Rank progression, endemic collectibles, Artian crafting and the apex monsters that will humble you.

⏱ 60-200 hrs · Medium-Hard · ⭐ 9.0/10

⚔ Main Story Hunts

▶

Chapter 1: Windward Plains — Chatacabra & Rey DauEasy → Hard

📍 Windward Plains, Kunafa Village · 🎁 Base kit, Seikret mastery, first apex kill
  1. Tutorial hunt Chatacabra: a frog bruiser — practice Focus Mode (L2): aim strikes at glowing wounds for burst damage and knockdowns.
  2. Hunt Quematrice and Lala Barina next; Barina's paralysis armor is one of the best early crafts.
  3. Learn Seikret flow: auto-run to targets, swap between your TWO equipped weapons mid-hunt from the saddle, and gather while mounted.
  4. Rey Dau arrives with the Sandtide: fight it in the dunes, sheltering behind rock arches when it charges its railgun horn blast — the beam one-shots low-rank armor.
  5. Break its horns in Focus Mode for bonus parts; its lightning armor set carries you through most of Low Rank.
🎁 Base kit, Seikret mastery, first apex kill
▶

Chapter 2: Scarlet Forest — Uth Duna & the DownpourMedium

📍 Scarlet Forest, the Wudwud hideout · 🎁 Water apex clear, forest camp network
  1. Story hunts: Congalala (stench control — just dodge the gas), Balahara sand-eels, and Rompopolo's toxic swamp bog.
  2. Befriend the Wudwud tribe to unlock forest side quests and their hidden village vendor.
  3. Uth Duna rides the flood: it cloaks in a water veil that dampens damage — strip the veil by attacking the draped folds (they wound fast in Focus Mode).
  4. Its belly-flop wave attack floods low platforms; fight from the elevated root shelves when it surges.
  5. Break the veil, mount it, your weapon into the exposed back wounds. Its armor's water resistance sets up the next chapter.
🎁 Water apex clear, forest camp network
▶

Chapter 3: Oilwell Basin — Black Flame & Nu UdraMedium-Hard

📍 Oilwell Basin, Azuz the Everforge · 🎁 Fire apex clear, smithing story unlocks
  1. Chapter hunts: Rathalos returns as the sky tyrant, plus Ajarakan the flame ape and the horror-adjacent Nerscylla in the Dunes crossover hunts.
  2. Nu Udra ignites the basin: fight phases alternate between lit and unlit oil — when the Firespring erupts, retreat from the tar pools before they catch.
  3. Sever its tentacles individually — each severed limb is a carve and permanently weakens its reach.
  4. Its cephalopod body squeezes through tunnels between zones; use your Seikret to keep pace or set traps at the tunnel mouths.
  5. Extinguish its flame cloak with water ammo or environmental mud, then unload on the head core. Azuz's forge story afterward unlocks key smithing upgrades.
🎁 Fire apex clear, smithing story unlocks
▶

Chapter 4: Iceshard Cliffs — Jin Dahaad's AvalancheHard

📍 Iceshard Cliffs, the Keepers' village · 🎁 Ice apex clear, endgame region access
  1. Chapter hunts include Hirabami ice-flyers and the returning Gore Magala — the frenzy virus fight is a Low Rank skill check; cure frenzy by dealing damage while infected.
  2. Jin Dahaad is a siege: it coils through a multi-level ice cavern, triggering avalanche slides between floors.
  3. ITS NOVA IS LETHAL: when it charges the absolute-zero burst, sprint to the ice boulders and HIDE BEHIND them (the prompt appears) — nothing survives it in the open.
  4. Target the glowing frost sacs on its underside; wounds there cause massive topples.
  5. After the kill, the story pivots to the Wyveria mystery and the truth about Arkveld and Nata's people.
🎁 Ice apex clear, endgame region access
▶

Chapter 5: Wyveria & Arkveld — the White WraithHard

📍 Ruins of Wyveria · 🎁 Story climax, credits, High Rank unlock
  1. Wyveria's Guardian monsters (artificial Rathalos, Ebony Odogaron and kin) hit harder than their wild cousins — upgrade to a full apex-material set first.
  2. Guardian Arkveld duel: its chain-blade wings whip in wide crescents — stay diagonal to its shoulders, never directly ahead or behind.
  3. Wound its wingblades in Focus Mode; broken chains shorten its reach dramatically.
  4. It siphons vitality through chain strikes to heal — interrupt the red-glow drain lunge with a well-timed offset attack or flash pod.
  5. Zoh Shia, the true final story fight, waits beneath: a dragon-energy colossus whose crystallized zones detonate — break the crystals before its supernova channel. Credits roll; High Rank begins immediately.
🎁 Story climax, credits, High Rank unlock
▶

High Rank & Tempered Arkveld (HR 41+)Very Hard

📍 All regions, Suja · 🎁 Endgame loop, Artian weapons, 8-star hunts
  1. HR progression re-hunts apexes in High Rank with new movesets; unlock every region's HR camps as you go.
  2. HR 21-40 assignments culminate in wild Arkveld — faster and angrier than the Guardian version.
  3. Tempered monsters (purple outline) drop Artian parts; 5-star tempered Gore Magala and Arkveld are the gear checks of the base endgame.
  4. Zoh Shia returns at HR 50+ as a repeatable hunt with top-tier crafts — its set skills define meta builds.
  5. Title updates added Mizutsune, Lagiacrus, Seregios and Arch-tempered variants — the event quest board is your weekly ritual now.
🎁 Endgame loop, Artian weapons, 8-star hunts

📜 Side Quests & Optional Hunts

▶

Village support quests (all regions)Easy

📍 Kunafa, Wudwud hideout, Azuz, the Keepers, Suja · 🎁 Ingredients, gear recipes, Palico gadgets, meal buffs
  1. Kunafa (Plains): the local cuisine chain upgrades meal ingredients — do every cooking quest, meal buffs are enormous.
  2. Wudwud hideout (Forest): fetch-and-hunt quests unlock their trader's rare decorations stock.
  3. Azuz (Basin): sharpening materials and smithing shortcuts.
  4. The Keepers (Cliffs): quests unlock cold-region gathering boosts and story lore on the Guardians.
  5. Suja / Grand Hub: post-game side quests gate arena challenges and the melding pot expansions. Talk to EVERYONE with a blue marker after each chapter.
🎁 Ingredients, gear recipes, Palico gadgets, meal buffs
▶

Optional monster hunts & crownsMedium

📍 Quest board, all regions · 🎁 Rare materials, crown achievements
  1. Optional hunts unlock as you encounter monsters in the wild — check the board after every field session.
  2. Gold crowns: giant and miniature monsters spawn in the open world; inspect via binoculars — the size variance shows before engaging.
  3. Rare spawn windows: environmental cycles (Inclemency, Plenty) change which monsters roam — Plenty periods spawn rarer herds.
  4. Field surveys can be saved as investigations to re-run good size rolls.
  5. The crown grind is optional but the arena quests offer fixed-size targets for several achievements.
🎁 Rare materials, crown achievements
▶

The Gathering Hub arena & challenge questsHard

📍 Grand Hub · 🎁 Arena coins, unique layered armor, leaderboard glory
  1. Arena quests hand you preset builds — learn weapons outside your main; coin rewards forge exclusive gear.
  2. Challenge quests rotate via title updates with leaderboard timers.
  3. Coin sets (arena exclusives) require A-rank clears; co-op halves the pain.
  4. Practice offset attacks and perfect guards here — the fixed builds punish panic rolling.
  5. Event-limited layered armor and gestures rotate through this board — check weekly.
🎁 Arena coins, unique layered armor, leaderboard glory
▶

Palico & support crew questsEasy

📍 Base camps, story interludes · 🎁 Palico gadgets, Seikret cosmetics, NPC hunters
  1. Palico quests unlock gadget tiers: the vigorwasp heal, shieldspire taunt, and the plunderblade (steal materials mid-hunt — best-in-slot for farming).
  2. Complete the Palico-culture side stories in each region for cosmetic sets.
  3. NPC support hunters can join solo hunts via SOS-equivalent — their quest chains improve their loadouts.
  4. Seikret customization (saddles, through traversal challenges and vendor quests.
  5. The fishing side quests deserve special mention: the giant fish trophies hang in your tent, and the rod minigame is genuinely good.
🎁 Palico gadgets, Seikret cosmetics, NPC hunters

💎 Collectibles

▶

Endemic life (capture log)Medium

📍 All regions, all weather cycles · 🎁 Trophy log completion, pets for your tent
  1. Carry the capture net on your radial menu always; sneak (crouch) toward skittish species.
  2. Rare endemics spawn under specific weather: check each region during Inclemency storms and Plenty blooms.
  3. Downpour-only and Sandtide-only species are the usual log blockers — camp at a pop-up tent and rest to cycle weather.
  4. Aquatic rares need the fishing rod, not the net.
  5. Captured pets can be placed in your tent's terrarium — yes, you can decorate with beetles.
🎁 Trophy log completion, pets for your tent
▶

Relic records & Wyverian ruinsMedium

📍 Hidden carvings in every region · 🎁 Lore entries, map completion, achievement
  1. Glowing blue carvings emit a faint chime — listen while exploring caves and cliff ledges.
  2. The Windward Plains ruins cluster around the oasis and the ancient aqueduct bones.
  3. Scarlet Forest records hide behind vine curtains (cut them) and above the canopy root-paths.
  4. Oilwell Basin's are tucked in cooled lava tubes; Iceshard's behind breakable ice walls.
  5. Wyveria itself is one giant record — sweep it post-story with no monsters aggroed for the final entries.
🎁 Lore entries, map completion, achievement
▶

Camp locations (pop-up network)Easy

📍 Every region, marked and hidden · 🎁 Fast travel web, weather resets, restock points
  1. Scout camp-suitable flat spots (the map hints candidate zones); confirm with Alma to establish.
  2. Camps can be DESTROYED by roaming monsters — rebuild via the support ship supplies when notified.
  3. Priority: one camp per apex arena zone — respawn-adjacent restocking wins hunts.
  4. Hidden candidate spots sit behind vine walls, up Seikret-only ledges, and inside cave hollows.
  5. Rest at camps to pass time and cycle weather/spawns — the whole endgame farm loop leans on your camp web.
🎁 Fast travel web, weather resets, restock points
▶

Artian parts & Rarity 8 dreamsHard

📍 Tempered hunts, endgame · 🎁 Custom-forged endgame weapons
  1. Tempered hunts (purple map outline) drop Artian parts by rarity tier; 8-star hunts drop Rarity 8.
  2. Collect matching part types (blade, tube, device...) — three parts forge one weapon; element and stats roll from the parts used.
  3. Reinforce rolls are random: attack, affinity, sharpness — farm spare parts and re-roll bad weapons mercilessly.
  4. Match part elements for a pure-element weapon; mixed parts dilute.
  5. The dream: a perfectly reinforced Artian weapon of your element. The grind: eternal. Welcome to Monster Hunter.
🎁 Custom-forged endgame weapons

⚔ Unique Gear & Builds

▶

Gore Magala set (Frenzy meta)Hard

📍 Gore Magala hunts, Iceshard Cliffs / HR investigations · 🎁 Black Eclipse skills — the base-game meta armor
  1. Hunt HR Gore Magala (unlocks in High Rank region rotations; tempered at 6-star).
  2. Break the feelers when enraged for the rarest part drops.
  3. The set bonus triggers frenzy infection on you — OVERCOME it by attacking to gain massive affinity.
  4. Pair with sustain skills to survive the pre-overcome window.
  5. Its weapons carry innate frenzy synergy; the full package outperforms most apex gear until Zoh Shia sets arrive.
🎁 Black Eclipse skills — the base-game meta armor
▶

Rey Dau lightning weaponsMedium

📍 Windward Plains apex farm · 🎁 Top-tier thunder element line
  1. Farm HR Rey Dau during Sandtide windows (it always spawns with the storm).
  2. Focus horn breaks: the horn parts gate the final weapon upgrades.
  3. Its heavy bowgun and greatsword are standout crafts; the layered look alone justifies the farm.
  4. Pair thunder weapons vs Uth Duna, Jin Dahaad and the water/ice roster.
  5. Tempered Rey Dau investigations with gold-tier rewards are the efficient route — save good ones and re-run.
🎁 Top-tier thunder element line
▶

The dual-loadout system (two weapons, one hunt)Easy

📍 Seikret saddle, always · 🎁 Build flexibility no previous MH allowed
  1. Equip a primary and secondary at the tent; swap anytime from the saddle mid-hunt.
  2. Classic pairings: greatsword + light bowgun (tempo control), lance + hammer (part-break coverage), your main + a status weapon (paralysis/sleep setup).
  3. Element-swap pairing: match both weapons to the target's two weaknesses and swap per phase.
  4. Sharpness saver: swap out instead of sheathing to whetstone mid-fight.
  5. Solo hunters: a sleep weapon secondary + barrel bombs recreates co-op burst windows alone.
🎁 Build flexibility no previous MH allowed
▶

Mantles & specialized toolsEasy

📍 Story and side quest rewards · 🎁 Situational superpowers
  1. Ghillie Mantle (early story): invisibility for repositioning and sneak attacks — the farming favorite.
  2. Rocksteady and Temporal equivalents come from late assignments: flinch immunity vs. auto-evade — the classic endgame debate.
  3. Assign mantles to the radial menu; their cooldowns reset at camp rests.
  4. Use Ghillie + capture net for skittish endemic rares.
  5. Check side quest logs in each village — several mantle upgrades hide behind unassuming NPC chains.
🎁 Situational superpowers

📋 Apex & Endgame Monsters

▶

Tempered Arkveld (the endgame wall)Extreme

📍 8-star tempered investigations · 🎁 Best Artian parts, amulet rolls
  1. Its chainblade crescents now combo twice — dodge the first arc and KEEP moving; the second follows your roll.
  2. The vitality drain field (red glow) heals it massively — flash pod or offset-attack the channel every time.
  3. Wound management is the fight: its wings wound fast, and Focus Strikes on wing wounds cause topples — cycle wounds for openings.
  4. Bring max thunder or dragon element; its head and wingblades take the bonus.
  5. Its chain attacks chip through guards — Guard Up or perfect-guard timing for lance/shield mains.
🎁 Best Artian parts, amulet rolls
▶

Zoh Shia (true final boss, repeatable at HR 50+)Extreme

📍 Wyveria depths · 🎁 Endgame armor set with unique skills
  1. Phase 1: it seeds the arena with dragon-energy crystals — destroy them; they detonate during its big channels otherwise.
  2. Its slam chains stagger the whole arena floor — stay on its flanks, never under the chest.
  3. The supernova: when it rears and channels white light, either break the charged crystals to interrupt or sprint to the arena rim.
  4. Wounds on its chest core are the damage windows — save Focus Strikes for post-nova staggers.
  5. Its HR armor set skills anchor several meta builds — expect many farming laps.
🎁 Endgame armor set with unique skills
▶

Gore Magala & the Frenzy checkHard

📍 Iceshard Cliffs / HR rotations · 🎁 Meta armor, antivirus mastery
  1. Frenzy infection: a purple meter fills when hit by its virus — reduced recovery when it completes.
  2. Its enraged state unveils the hidden feelers — horn breaks only count while enraged.
  3. The wing-arm slam grid covers huge ground: superman-dive the wide sweeps, position at its hind legs.
  4. Nulberries and wide-range support help teams, but aggression is the true cure.
  5. Its tempered version at 6-star gates the mid-endgame — learn it well; the arch-tempered event version demands it.
🎁 Meta armor, antivirus mastery
▶

Title update legends: Mizutsune, Lagiacrus & friendsVery Hard

📍 Event assignments, post-launch updates · 🎁 New weapon trees, layered armor, Arch-tempered challenges
  1. Mizutsune (TU1): bubble blight fight — bubbles inflict slippery status; consume nulberry or embrace the evasion buff bubbles grant.
  2. Lagiacrus (TU2): the sea emperor returns with semi-aquatic phases — thunder sacs on its back are the break priority.
  3. Arch-tempered apex reruns (Rey Dau onward) are the hardest content in the game — fixed event windows, best layered rewards.
  4. Seregios and later additions bring bleed mechanics — crouch or eat rations to staunch bleeding.
  5. Check the event quest board weekly; limited-time hunts rotate and their exclusive cosmetics do expire.
🎁 New weapon trees, layered armor, Arch-tempered challenges

💡 Pro Tips & Secrets

▶

Focus Mode is not optionalEasy

📍 Every hunt · 🎁 Twice the damage, half the hunt time
  1. Hold Focus Mode (L2/LT) to aim attacks precisely — your combos steer mid-swing now.
  2. Repeated hits on one body part open red WOUNDS; Focus Strikes (R1/RB in Focus Mode) on wounds burst for huge damage and resources.
  3. Wound bursts stagger — chain them across body parts to keep monsters permanently off-balance.
  4. Weapon Focus Strikes differ wildly (greatsword's is a cinematic cleave; dual blades shred) — learn yours.
  5. Tempered monsters' old wounds give bonus rewards when burst. Aim at the glow. Always the glow.
🎁 Twice the damage, half the hunt time
▶

Eat, drink, and buff like you mean itEasy

📍 Camp & saddle · 🎁 30-50% effective power
  1. Cook at camp before every serious hunt: ingredient combos trigger meal skills (defense, elemental resist, moxie-style survival).
  2. The portable BBQ works from the Seikret saddle — buff en route to the monster.
  3. Standard stack: Demondrug + Might Seed + Dash Juice for stamina weapons.
  4. Hunt during Plenty weather for bonus gathering en route; Inclemency spawns the apexes.
  5. Your Palico's vigorwasp station covers healing gaps — gadget choice is a build slot, treat it seriously.
🎁 30-50% effective power
▶

Offset attacks & perfect guards (the new counters)Medium

📍 Training area → muscle memory · 🎁 Turn monster aggression into openings
  1. Offset attacks (certain rising/charged moves) CLASH with monster attacks when timed right, staggering them massively.
  2. Guard weapons get perfect guards: block at the moment of impact for zero chip and instant counter windows.
  3. Practice in the training area against the dummy's timed attacks.
  4. Offset timing beats roll-spam against apex charge attacks — Rey Dau's railgun and Arkveld's chains both offset beautifully.
  5. SOS flares bring NPC or player backup instantly — there is no shame mid-learning-curve.
🎁 Turn monster aggression into openings
▶

Investigation banking (the smart farm)Easy

📍 Map menu, Alma · 🎁 Choose your rewards before you hunt
  1. Field-spotted monsters generate investigations with visible reward pools — save ones with gold-tier rewards via Alma.
  2. Bank tempered Arkveld/Gore investigations with Artian part bonuses; re-run them back-to-back.
  3. Size crowns: banked investigations preserve the spawn's size — inspect before saving.
  4. Reward multipliers stack during environmental Plenty.
  5. Rest at camp to refresh field spawns when the map runs dry. The desert always provides.
🎁 Choose your rewards before you hunt
