Ghost of Yōtei — 100% Completion

Complete all 27 Main Tales, 58 Side Tales across 6 regions, 6 Mythic Tales, and all trophies. Set in Ezo (Hokkaido) in 1603.

Progress saves automatically to your browser and persists between sessions. Click ▼ How to complete on any quest to see step-by-step instructions.

⚔ Main Tales

All 27 story missions across Prologue, Chapter I, II, and III. Complete all to finish Atsu's story.

▶

Prologue · 1

  1. Travel to the Yotei Grasslands and follow the path to the drunkard's camp.
  2. Confront The Snake — this is the first of the Yotei Six. Defeat him in combat.
  3. After the fight, ride your horse through Yotei Grasslands toward Atsu's childhood home.
  4. Complete the flashback combat tutorial to learn basic sword techniques.
  5. Completing the prologue unlocks Chapter I: The Lone Wolf.
🏆 Unlocks Chapter I
▶

Chapter I: The Lone Wolf · 18

  1. Travel to the inn in Yotei Grasslands — it appears as a gold quest marker on the map.
  2. Speak with the innkeeper to gather information about the Yotei Six.
  3. Unlock merchants: Armorer, Bounty Board, Cartographer, and Vendors at the inn.
  4. Learn Onryo's Howl ability from the innkeeper's contact.
  5. Complete the inn objectives to unlock the open world and begin hunting the Yotei Six.
🏆 Onryo's Howl ability, Merchants unlocked
  1. Find the weapon master in Yotei Grasslands — follow the gold marker.
  2. Complete the dual katana training sequence — defeat the training dummies.
  3. Learn the dual katana stance and special attacks.
  4. Defeat the weapon master's test challenge.
  5. Unlock Dual Katana as a permanent weapon option.
🏆 Dual Katana weapon
  1. Travel to Lord Saito's training fortress in Tokachi Range.
  2. Scout the fortress from the ridge — identify guard patrol patterns.
  3. Infiltrate the fortress using stealth — avoid alerting guards.
  4. Locate the intelligence inside the fortress about the Yotei Six.
  5. Escape the fortress without triggering a full alarm.
🏆 Intel on Yotei Six, Odachi weapon path unlocked
  1. Speak with contacts in Tokachi Range to gather leads on the Oni, Kitsune, or Saito Brothers.
  2. Choose which Yotei Six member to hunt first — Oni (Ishikari Plain), Kitsune (Teshio Ridge), or Saito Brothers (Oshima Coast).
  3. Follow the chosen lead's trail to their region.
  4. Complete the introductory objectives for your chosen target.
  5. This mission branches — each target unlocks different weapons and abilities.
🏆 Unlocks three hunt paths
  1. Travel south from Tokachi Range toward Ishikari Plain.
  2. Follow the trail of Oni Raiders burning villages along the road.
  3. Defeat the Oni Raiders at the roadblock checkpoint.
  4. Speak with survivors to learn about the Oni's base of operations.
  5. Reach Ishikari Plain to unlock the Oni hunt missions.
🏆 Unlocks Oni hunt in Ishikari Plain
  1. Follow the Oni's trail through Ishikari Plain — the land is burning.
  2. Defeat the Oni Raiders guarding the approach to the castle construction site.
  3. Infiltrate the outer perimeter of the Oni's fortified camp.
  4. Confront the Oni's lieutenant and extract information about the Oni's location.
  5. Escape the camp and regroup to plan the assault on the Oni.
🏆 Oni's location revealed
  1. Assault the Oni's main camp in Ishikari Plain.
  2. Fight through waves of Oni Raiders using the Yari spear if unlocked.
  3. Locate the Oni inside the camp's inner sanctum.
  4. Defeat the Oni in a major boss fight — use fire resistance if available.
  5. Collect the Mercenary Attire and Twin Wolves sword kit from the Oni's chambers.
🏆 Mercenary Attire, Twin Wolves sword kit, 3000 Coin, Horse Charge
  1. After defeating the Oni, deal with the aftermath of the battle in Ishikari Plain.
  2. Help survivors escape the burning camp.
  3. Confront the Oni's remaining forces who are still burning the land.
  4. Complete the cleanup objectives in Ishikari Plain.
  5. Return to the inn to report your progress and collect rewards.
🏆 Charm of Fearful Strike (Gift Altar)
  1. Travel to the location revealed by the Oni's intelligence.
  2. Meet the contact who has information about the Twin Wolves mercenary group.
  3. Infiltrate the Twin Wolves' camp and gather intelligence.
  4. Defeat the Twin Wolves leader in combat.
  5. Collect the Oni's Flame quickfire weapon and Charm of Hachiman.
🏆 Charm of Hachiman, Oni's Flame quickfire weapon
  1. Travel to the final Oni stronghold in Ishikari Plain.
  2. Fight through the Oni's elite guard — use Ghost Weapons to thin their numbers.
  3. Confront the Oni in his throne room for the final confrontation.
  4. Defeat the Oni in an extended boss fight — he uses fire attacks.
  5. Completing this tale crosses the Oni's name off Atsu's sash.
🏆 Onryo Armor Upgrade, 5 Precious Metals
  1. Travel north to Teshio Ridge — the frozen wasteland where the Kitsune operates.
  2. Follow the trail of the Kitsune's shinobi through the snow.
  3. Solve the first puzzle to enter the Kitsune's hidden network of tunnels.
  4. Defeat the shinobi guards using stealth — they are highly alert.
  5. Reach the Kitsune's outer base to unlock the next mission.
🏆 Unlocks Kitsune hunt in Teshio Ridge
  1. Follow the Kitsune's trail deeper into Teshio Ridge.
  2. Rescue the Ainu villagers being held captive by the Kitsune's shinobi.
  3. Defeat the shinobi patrol leader and collect the Kusarigama weapon.
  4. Pursue the Kitsune through the frozen forest.
  5. The Kitsune escapes — gather clues about his true base location.
🏆 Kusarigama weapon
  1. Find the shamisen teacher in Teshio Ridge — follow rumors at the local inn.
  2. Learn the shamisen playing mechanic — this is used to find collectibles.
  3. Complete the shamisen performance sequence.
  4. The teacher reveals a secret about the Kitsune's hideout location.
  5. Unlock the Shamisen as a tool for tracking Mythic Tales and collectibles.
🏆 Shamisen ability unlocked
  1. Investigate the poisoned village in Teshio Ridge — the Kitsune's work.
  2. Gather evidence of the Kitsune's poison operation.
  3. Track the poison supply chain back to the Kitsune's laboratory.
  4. Infiltrate the laboratory and destroy the poison stockpile.
  5. Escape before the Kitsune's shinobi close in.
🏆 Kitsune's laboratory location revealed
  1. Travel to the Kitsune's hidden base deep in Teshio Ridge.
  2. Solve the puzzle gates protecting the Kitsune's inner sanctum.
  3. Defeat the Kitsune's elite shinobi guards.
  4. Confront the Kitsune — he uses illusions and poison attacks.
  5. Defeat the Kitsune in the boss fight to cross his name off the sash.
🏆 Hunter's Ear ability, Smoke Bomb weapon
  1. Deal with the aftermath of the Kitsune's defeat in Teshio Ridge.
  2. Help the surviving Ainu people reclaim their village.
  3. Complete the final objectives in Teshio Ridge.
  4. Collect the Onryo Armor Upgrade from the Gift Altar.
  5. Return to Yotei Grasslands to prepare for Chapter II.
🏆 Onryo Armor Upgrade, 5 Precious Metals, 4 Shinobi Steel
  1. Find the Odachi weapon master in Tokachi Range.
  2. Complete the Odachi training sequence — learn heavy attacks and counters.
  3. Defeat the weapon master's final test.
  4. Unlock the Odachi as a permanent weapon.
  5. This mission is required before Ghosts of the Past can begin.
🏆 Odachi weapon
  1. Return to Atsu's childhood home in Yotei Grasslands.

  3. Speak with a key character about the past and the remaining Yotei Six.

  5. Chapter II: The Onryo unlocks after this mission.

Chapter II: The Onryo · 5

  1. Travel to Oshima Coast — the cherry blossom region controlled by the Saito Brothers.
  2. Fight through the Saito Brothers' soldiers at the coastal checkpoint.
  3. Reach the Oshima Coast inn and speak with the local resistance.
  4. Gather intelligence on The Dragon and The Spider's locations.
  5. Unlock the Tanegashima rifle from the local blacksmith.
🏆 Tanegashima rifle, Unlocks Oshima Coast
  1. Follow the lead on The Dragon — he controls the Saito Brothers' military forces.
  2. Infiltrate the Saito Brothers' coastal fortress.
  3. Defeat The Dragon's lieutenant and gather intelligence.
  4. Escape the fortress with the intelligence.
  5. The Dragon's location is revealed for the next mission.
🏆 Dragon's location revealed
  1. Travel to The Dragon's command post on Oshima Coast.
  2. Fight through the Saito Brothers' elite soldiers.
  3. Confront The Dragon in his command post.
  4. Defeat The Dragon in combat — he uses firearms and heavy armor.
  5. Collect the Charm of Fearful Mastery and Twin Wolves Mask.
🏆 Charm of Fearful Mastery, Twin Wolves Mask
  1. Track The Spider — the Saito Brothers' master assassin.
  2. Follow the web of clues across Oshima Coast.
  3. Infiltrate The Spider's hidden compound.
  4. Defeat The Spider's assassin guards — they use ambush tactics.
  5. Confront The Spider and defeat him to unlock Ghost Stance.
🏆 Ghost Stance ability, Onryo Mask, Onryo Armor Upgrade
  1. Gather your allies before the final assault on Lord Saito.
  2. Complete the alliance-building objectives across Ezo.
  3. Speak with key characters to confirm their support.
  4. Prepare for the assault on Lord Saito's fortress.
  5. This mission sets up the final chapter.
