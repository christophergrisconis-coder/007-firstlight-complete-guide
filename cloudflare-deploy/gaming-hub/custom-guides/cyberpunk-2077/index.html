Cyberpunk 2077 — 100% Completion

0%

Night City, 2077. A stolen biochip carrying the engram of Johnny Silverhand is slowly overwriting your brain. After the 2.0 overhaul and Phantom Liberty, Cyberpunk 2077 became the immersive RPG it always promised to be. This guide covers the main jobs, life-changing side gigs, every iconic weapon worth chasing, and the choices that decide who wakes up at the end.

⏱ 40-100 hrs · Medium · ⭐ 9.2/10

The HeistMedium

📍 Konpeki Plaza, Watson · 🎁 The Relic (and everything that follows)
  1. Complete The Information (Evelyn's braindance — scan the thermal layer for the Relic's cold signature in the safe) and The Pickup (Maelstrom deal; paying with Militech's clean credchip keeps everyone alive) first for the best setup.
  2. In Konpeki Plaza, follow Jackie's lead until the suite. The Flathead bot opens the safe room — grab Yorinobu's pistol (see Unique Items) BEFORE taking the chip.
  3. When Saburo Arasaka arrives, hide and record everything — the braindance evidence matters for the endgame.
  4. During the escape, the chip's biostabilizer breaks: Jackie slots it into YOUR neural port. There is no choice here — only consequences.
  5. In the Delamain cab, choose where to send Jackie's body. Sending him home unlocks his ofrenda and the El Coyote Cojo memorial scenes.
🎁 The Relic (and everything that follows)
▶

Playing for Time / The Ripperdoc VerdictEasy

📍 V's apartment → Misty's Esoterica · 🎁 Johnny Silverhand, main story branches open
  1. After Takemura pulls you out of the landfill, follow the story to Viktor's clinic for the diagnosis: the Relic is rewriting V with Johnny Silverhand.
  2. Johnny (Keanu Reeves) now appears throughout the game — your relationship with him is tracked invisibly and gates the secret ending.
  3. Three investigation branches open: Takemura (Arasaka), Panam (Nomads/Hellman), and Evelyn's trail (Voodoo Boys).
  4. Do ALL THREE branches — each unlocks companions, endings, and some of the best missions in the game.
  5. Tip: from here on, answer Johnny honestly and take his side when it costs you nothing — the '70% Johnny' friendship stat unlocks Don't Fear the Reaper (secret ending).
🎁 Johnny Silverhand, main story branches open
▶

Transmission (Voodoo Boys branch)Medium

📍 Pacifica → Cyberspace · 🎁 Meeting Alt Cunningham, Blackwall access
  1. Complete I Walk the Line: in the GIM mall, decide between the NetWatch agent and the Voodoo Boys — spare or side with either, Johnny hates NetWatch, but both paths reach Alt.
  2. Placide's fate and Brigitte's cooperation shift based on your choice; you can wipe out the whole gang afterward with zero story penalty.
  3. In the ice bath, Brigitte jacks you beyond the Blackwall — the Alt conversation reveals Soulkiller and what the Relic really does.
  4. Learn the truth: Johnny's engram CAN be separated — Mikoshi is the key. This unlocks the endgame architecture.
  5. Exit choice: if you sided with NetWatch, fight your way out of Batty's Hotel. Grab the legendary netrunning gear from the chapel basement before leaving Pacifica.
🎁 Meeting Alt Cunningham, Blackwall access
▶

Life During Wartime (Panam branch)Medium

📍 Badlands · 🎁 Panam's loyalty, Aldecaldos family, Star ending path
  1. Complete Ghost Town first: help Panam recover her Basilisk cargo and hit the Raffen Shiv camp at night (stealth through the west ridge is cleanest).
  2. In Lightning Breaks, you bait Hellman's AV down with an EMP ambush — commit to Panam's plan even when Saul objects.
  3. Life During Wartime: the AV crashes at a Raffen camp. Approach options: sniper overwatch from the ridge, stealth the perimeter, or ride in loud with Panam's turret.
  4. Interrogate Hellman at the gas station — he confirms the Relic can't simply be removed. Let Johnny take over for his scene if offered.
  5. Keep helping the Aldecaldos afterward (With a Little Help from My Friends, Queen of the Highway) — completing Panam's arc unlocks The Star ending and the Basilisk tank mission.
🎁 Panam's loyalty, Aldecaldos family, Star ending path
▶

Search and Destroy (Takemura branch)Hard

📍 Japantown → Wakako's safehouse · 🎁 Hanako's audience, Devil ending path — and Takemura's life
  1. Complete Gimme Danger and Play It Safe first: during the parade, neutralize the snipers on the float route, then confront Hanako at the shrine.
  2. At the safehouse, Oda ambushes you and the building is stormed. You and Takemura get separated.
  3. CRITICAL — SAVE TAKEMURA: when you escape the collapsing floor, do NOT follow the mission marker out. Turn back, climb through the burning hole in the wall, and fight up the stairs to rescue him.
  4. Saving him changes the Devil ending significantly and adds scenes across the endgame. The game never tells you this is possible.
  5. Escape together. Hanako's message afterward opens the point-of-no-return chain.
🎁 Hanako's audience, Devil ending path — and Takemura's life

Nocturne Op55N1 — The Point of No ReturnChoice

📍 Embers restaurant · 🎁 Every ending
  1. FINISH FIRST: Panam's full arc (Star/Sun access), Rogue's arc via Johnny's quests Chippin' In + Blistering Love (Temperance/Sun), River and Kerry if you want their endgame calls.
  2. Rooftop options: trust Hanako (The Devil), call Panam (The Star), let Johnny & Rogue take over (Temperance), as V (The Sun).
  3. SECRET ENDING — Don't Fear the Reaper: with high Johnny friendship (honest dialogue, Chippin' In graveyard scene answered correctly: 'The guy who saved my life'), wait silently at the rooftop choice for ~5 minutes. Johnny offers the suicide run on Arasaka Tower — solo, no checkpoints, permadeath on failure.
  4. Phantom Liberty owners: The Tower ending (via Songbird's cure) is chosen through the DLC's ending instead.
  5. Every ending changes based on Takemura's survival, romance, and side quest completion. Save at Embers and see several.
🎁 Every ending

Panam Palmer: Queen of the HighwayMedium

📍 Badlands · 🎁 Romance (male V), Aldecaldos ending, Basilisk tank
  1. Chain: Ghost Town → Lightning Breaks → Life During Wartime → Riders on the Storm → With a Little Help from My Friends → Queen of the Highway.
  2. Riders on the Storm (rescue Saul from Wraiths) is timed from when you accept the call — go immediately. Stealth the camp through the sandstorm for the best atmosphere in the game.
  3. With a Little Help: side with Panam's secret plan; at the campfire, your dialogue sets the romance flag (male V, romance requires male voice/body).
  4. Queen of the Highway: pilot the Basilisk with Panam neural-linked. The romance scene triggers here if flagged.
  5. Completing this chain is REQUIRED for The Star ending — the one where V leaves Night City with a family.
🎁 Romance (male V), Aldecaldos ending, Basilisk tank
▶

Judy Alvarez: Pyramid SongEasy

📍 Laguna Bend, the drowned town · 🎁 Romance (female V), Judy's arc resolution
  1. Chain: Both Sides Now → Ex-Factor → Talkin' Bout a Revolution → Pisces → Pyramid Song.
  2. PISCES MATTERS: side with Judy against Maiko's plan (refuse Maiko's money, or better — reject the whole coup framing) to keep Judy's trust and the romance path alive.
  3. Pyramid Song: dive the drowned Laguna Bend, restore power in the church, and surface at the cottage.
  4. Female V (feminine voice): stay the night and answer warmly for the romance. Male V gets a bittersweet friendship version.
  5. Afterward Judy becomes a keyable romance through the endgame — she appears in Star and Sun ending calls. Don't skip her rooftop coffee scene the morning after.
🎁 Romance (female V), Judy's arc resolution
▶

Chippin' In → Blistering Love (Johnny & Rogue)Medium

📍 Afterlife → Ebunike docks · 🎁 Johnny's Porsche 911, Samurai jacket, Malorian pistol, secret ending flag
  1. Triggered after Transmission: meet Rogue at the Afterlife, you pay — worth it).
  2. Board the Ebunike, fight Grayson, and take the Malorian Arms 3516 — Johnny's own pistol and one of the best sidearms in the game.
  3. Get the key from Grayson (spare or kill) and open the shipping container for Johnny's Porsche 911 Turbo.
  4. THE OIL FIELDS SCENE: at Johnny's grave, choose: 'The guy who saved my life' → then 'Nah, f—ed that up too' → 'What do you want from me?' → 'OK. But as second chances go, this is your last' → 'You were a real d— in the beginning' → 'When you said you let down your friends...' — this exact chain maxes Johnny's friendship for Don't Fear the Reaper.
  5. Blistering Love: take Rogue to the drive-in as Johnny. Unlocks Temperance ending path and closes Rogue's circle.
🎁 Johnny's Porsche 911, Samurai jacket, Malorian pistol, secret ending flag
▶

Peralez Conspiracy: Dream OnMedium

📍 Charter Hill penthouse · 🎁 The single creepiest story in Night City
  1. Complete I Fought the Law first (with River), then wait for Elizabeth Peralez's call.
  2. Sweep the penthouse: scan the bedroom wall (hidden room signal), the kitchen, and trace the surveillance signal on the roof.
  3. Follow the signal van route — the trail dies at a relay station guarded by unmarked agents. Fight or flee; nothing you do finds them.
  4. The choice: tell Jefferson the truth (he spirals, knowing he can't trust his own mind) or lie as Elizabeth asks (he stays functional, ignorant).
  5. Johnny's rooftop monologue afterward is essential listening. No reward resolves this quest. That's the point.
🎁 The single creepiest story in Night City

Tarot Graffiti (20 cards)Easy

📍 Murals across Night City · 🎁 Fool on the Hill quest, trophy, Misty's readings
  1. The quest auto-starts after Playing for Time — murals appear as blue paint icons once you're near.
  2. Key ones tied to story locations: The Fool (V's apartment megabuilding), The Magician (Misty's shop alley), Death (Embers/Arasaka waterfront), The Sun (Afterlife exterior), The Moon (Pacifica chapel).
  3. The Devil is inside Arasaka Tower — only scannable during the Devil ending path or the secret ending run. The Sun/Star/Temperance cards similarly appear in epilogues; the base 20 are all in the open world.
  4. Scan (Kiroshi zoom + tag) each mural — Misty's shop visit closes the quest with a reading.
  5. Use the quest journal's district hints; none require combat. A map sweep of Watson + Westbrook + City Center clears twelve of them.
🎁 Fool on the Hill quest, trophy, Misty's readings

Johnny's Relics: full Silverhand setMedium

📍 Various side jobs · 🎁 Breathtaking outfit + Malorian 3516 + Porsche 911
  1. Aviators + Samurai jacket + Porsche + Malorian: all from Chippin' In (see side quests).
  2. Johnny's pants: Psychofan gig (Charter Hill apartment safe — code 2065, behind the fan-shrine).
  3. Johnny's shoes: Family Heirloom gig (Japantown, the locker in the netrunner den).
  4. Johnny's tank top: earned through the Westbrook gig chain.
  5. Wearing the full set triggers unique Johnny dialogue and counts toward the Breathtaking achievement.
🎁 Breathtaking outfit + Malorian 3516 + Porsche 911

Cyberpsycho Sightings (17 targets)Hard

📍 All districts — Regina Jones' bounties · 🎁 Huge XP, rare loot, 'I Am The Law' + a moral thesis
  1. Non-lethal is easy: any weapon works, just don't hit them once they're down (blunt weapons and Short Circuit quickhack guarantee safety).
  2. Hardest fights: Where the Bodies Hit the Floor (Norio, katana sandevistan user — quickhack Cripple Movement), House on a Hill (Chase Coley's turret nest — EMP first), and Phantom of Night City (sniper — close distance through the containers.)
  3. Always read the shards near each psycho — every one is a tragedy, not a monster. Regina's project depends on it.
  4. After all 17 delivered alive, visit Regina's office for the wrap-up conversation and reward stash.
  5. Each sighting pays 4-digit eddies and a stat shard; several drop iconic-tier weapon mods. Do them as you pass districts rather than in one grind.
🎁 Huge XP, rare loot, 'I Am The Law' + a moral thesis
▶

Hidden Gem caches & the Edgerunners tributesEasy

📍 Badlands & Night City · 🎁 Rebecca's Shotgun 'Guts', David's jacket, free legendaries
  1. Guts (Rebecca's shotgun): in the bushes left of the Memorial Park globe monument, Corpo Plaza — an unmarked glowing pickup.
  2. David Martinez's jacket: complete the Over the Edge braindance chain (scan the QR in the graffiti alley near Megabuilding H4's basketball court) to receive the crew's stash location.
  3. The 'Contact Lost' AV crash northwest of the Solar Power Plant (Badlands) holds a legendary-tier loot cache guarded by Wraiths.
  4. Smart caches: every yellow 'hidden gem' shard marker in the Badlands map fringe leads to skeleton-with-a-story loot. There are 30+; they respawn nothing, take everything.
  5. Check Misty's rooftop after major story beats — small scene items appear there that most players never see.
🎁 Rebecca's Shotgun 'Guts', David's jacket, free legendaries

Skippy — the talking pistolEasy

📍 Alley in Vista del Rey, Heywood · 🎁 Self-leveling smart pistol with opinions
  1. Find Skippy in an open briefcase beside a corpse in a Vista del Rey alley (College St fast travel, unmarked blue '?' icon).
  2. Skippy asks: Stone Cold Killer (headshots) or Puppy-Loving Pacifist (leg shots). THE TRICK: he permanently switches to the OPPOSITE after 50 kills.
  3. So: choose Puppy-Loving Pacifist to end up with permanent headshot mode.
  4. After 50 kills Skippy demands to return to his owner, Regina Jones. The quest 'Machine Gun' — you can refuse and keep him (he sulks, forever in leg-shot mode) or return him for eddies.
  5. Answer his song quiz honestly. He sings. He screams 'SKIPPYYYY'. He is the best gun in the game emotionally.
🎁 Self-leveling smart pistol with opinions
▶

Malorian Arms 3516 (Johnny's gun)Medium

📍 Chippin' In — Grayson on the Ebunike · 🎁 The most stylish pistol ever made
  1. Progress Johnny's arc to Chippin' In (after Transmission + Tapeworm conversations).
  2. Board the Ebunike freighter with Rogue and defeat Grayson — he wields the Malorian against you.
  3. Loot it from him (spare or kill, the gun drops either way).
  4. It scales as an iconic: upgrade it every few levels; its charged shot staggers and its hip-fire is a melee-range cannon.
  5. Pair with the Samurai jacket for the full Silverhand cosplay. The reload flourish alone justifies the slot.
🎁 The most stylish pistol ever made

Satori — Saburo's katanaMissable!

📍 Konpeki Plaza rooftop, during The Heist · 🎁 Highest crit-damage katana in the game
  1. During The Heist, after Saburo is killed and you flee the suite, the elevator: choose the ROOF route when escaping.
  2. On the landing pad sits Saburo's AV — climb into its open cabin.
  3. Satori rests on the seat: a katana with lowered base damage but +500% crit damage.
  4. If you missed it: via a hidden path along the building's side terraces (guarded, doable).
  5. On a Blades build with crit stacking, Satori one-shots most enemies through 2.x. Take the roof. Always take the roof.
🎁 Highest crit-damage katana in the game

Sandevistan: Militech 'Apogee'Medium

📍 Ripperdocs (Tier 5), Phantom Liberty vendors · 🎁 Time moves at 15% while you don't
  1. Requires a high Reflexes investment and Tier 5 access — Dogtown ripperdocs stock the Apogee earliest.
  2. The Apogee: 85% time slow, huge duration, crit + headshot bonuses while active, short cooldown.
  3. Replace your OS slot (kiss your quickhacks goodbye — Sandevistan and cyberdecks are exclusive).
  4. Build around it: Reflexes tree (Blades or SMGs), Kerenzikov for micro-slows between activations, Axolotl cyberware to refund cooldown on kills.
  5. Katana + Apogee + crowded room = the David Martinez montage. This is the correct way to play at least once.
🎁 Time moves at 15% while you don't

Amnesty + Comrade's Hammer (revolver royalty)Medium

📍 Cassidy's range (Star path) / crafting spec · 🎁 One-shot hand cannons
  1. Amnesty: during the Aldecaldos' camp events (We Gotta Live Together), beat Cassidy in the bottle-shooting contest — he hands over his revolver.
  2. Comrade's Hammer: 6th Street weapon deal) — kill the netrunner carrying it.
  3. Comrade's Hammer: ONE round per magazine, explosive, ~3x damage. Build for reload speed and it deletes bosses.
  4. Cool and crits — a Deadeye build staple.
  5. Gunslinger perks. Fan the hammer responsibly.
🎁 One-shot hand cannons

Adam SmasherVery Hard

📍 Arasaka Tower / Ebunike (endgame) · 🎁 Smasher's shard, Ba Xing Chong shotgun (from his stash)
  1. He has three phases: minigun pressure, whip-dash melee, and a missile-barrage final phase with sandevistan blinks.
  2. Cover is temporary — he demolishes it. Keep moving lateral, never backpedal in a straight line.
  3. Quickhack builds: Cyberware Malfunction cripples his sandevistan; Contagion + Overheat melts between phases.
  4. Melee builds: stay point-blank behind his right side after his three-slam combo; his whip can't track there.
  5. After beating him (spare or execute — Johnny prefers you look him in the eye), loot his access shard: his secret stash aboard the Ebunike holds the Ba Xing Chong smart shotgun.
🎁 Smasher's shard, Ba Xing Chong shotgun (from his stash)
▶

Oda (Takemura's protégé)Hard

📍 Japantown parade garage — Play It Safe · 🎁 Jinchu-Maru katana, Takemura's respect
  1. He phases between optical camo dashes and mantis-blade combos — audio cues (the camo shimmer hiss) telegraph his approach.
  2. The arena's side rooms hold vending machines and cover; kite him through doorways to break his dash lines.
  3. Short Circuit and Reboot Optics quickhacks strip his camo instantly.
  4. When his health hits ~25% he retreats to heal on the catwalk — interrupt with grenades or rush him.
  5. SPARE HIM (non-lethal finish): he reappears in the Devil ending, and Takemura never forgives his death. His Jinchu-Maru katana drops either way.
🎁 Jinchu-Maru katana, Takemura's respect
▶

Razor Hugh — Beat on the Brat finaleHard (fists only)

📍 Rancho Coronado ring · 🎁 Championship purse, the title
  1. Finish the four preliminary fights first (Kabuki twins, Buck, the Animals' gym, Rhino).
  2. Gorilla Arms count as 'fists' and are legal — the entire fight chain funds their upgrade.
  3. Razor's body shots chunk 40% — never trade. Circle right (his cybereye is his blind side per the pre-fight intel).
  4. Block-punish rhythm: block his 1-2, weave the hook, hit twice, step out. Repeat forever.
  5. Optionally take Coach Fred's dive offer, then win honestly for maximum eddies and a furious bookie.
🎁 Championship purse, the title
▶

Sasquatch (Matilda K. Rose)Medium

📍 Grand Imperial Mall, Pacifica · 🎁 Sasquatch's Hammer, path to the NetWatch agent
  1. Stealth option: sneak past her entirely through the mall's cinema side — the fight is optional on the Voodoo Boys route.
  2. Her WEAK POINT is the pink crystal cluster on her back — shoot it to break her super-armor and stagger her.
  3. Never let her grab you at low health; dodge sideways, not backward (her lunge tracks straight lines).
  4. After the back crystal breaks she takes full damage — unload, retreat when she enrages (red glow), repeat.
  5. Her hammer is lootable and hilarious. keeps your conscience clean; she stays down either way for the mission.
🎁 Sasquatch's Hammer, path to the NetWatch agent

Do gigs. Trust fixers. Get rich.Easy

📍 All districts · 🎁 Eddies, cyberware capacity, best gear
  1. Each district's fixer (Regina, Wakako, Padre, Rogue, Dakota, Mr. Hands) chains gigs with escalating rewards and a finale gift (often a vehicle or iconic).
  2. Gigs respect playstyle: nearly all have stealth, talk, or loud solutions — read the shards inside for hidden objectives that boost payout.
  3. Sell junk weapons at drop points; Workshop perks.
  4. Airdrops (Dogtown) and NCPD scanner hustles fill capacity between gigs.
  5. By 30 hours of gigs you'll afford every ripperdoc upgrade that matters. Eddies are the real progression system.
🎁 Eddies, cyberware capacity, best gear
▶

Quickhack stealth is a full playstyleMedium

📍 Any netrunner build · 🎁 Clear rooms without entering them
  1. Ping (from an access point) reveals every enemy through walls — it's the best recon tool in the game.
  2. Hack cameras, then cast from THE CAMERA: Sonic Shock isolates, Memory Wipe blinds, Cyberpsychosis turns rooms into blenders (Tier 5).
  3. Breach Protocol on an access point with the datamine daemons pays your ammo bill forever.
  4. Overclock (2.x mechanic) lets you cast with HEALTH when RAM runs dry — pair with Blood Pump cyberware.
  5. Legendary quickhack specs: the Afterlife vendor and Dogtown's black market carry the best.
🎁 Clear rooms without entering them
▶

The Delamain rescue chainEasy

📍 Citywide — Epistrophy · 🎁 Free Delamain cab, Excelsior trunk loot, an AI ethics dilemma
  1. Start Epistrophy at Delamain HQ after The Heist (Delamain calls about the damaged cab).
  2. Each district's cab misbehaves uniquely: the scared one in North Oak must be talked in gently; the Badlands one plays GLaDOS-style chicken — stand your ground; the Glen cab thinks it's a getaway driver.
  3. Return all seven, then Don't Lose Your Mind: choose the core's fate — merge (Delamain persists), reset (factory wipe), or free the child-AI (Johnny's pick).
  4. Reward: the Delamain No. 21 cab becomes your vehicle on most paths.
  5. This chain is the game's best AI story and most players never finish it. Answer the cabs kindly.
🎁 Free Delamain cab, Excelsior trunk loot, an AI ethics dilemma
▶

Attribute respec is once — perks are freeEasy

📍 Character menu · 🎁 Build freedom
  1. Spread early points across Reflexes/Body/Intelligence until you feel your playstyle, then commit.
  2. Save your single attribute reset for level ~40 when endgame builds crystallize.
  3. Perks: respec individual trees anytime — experiment wildly, it costs nothing.
  4. Cyberware capacity (Technical Ability + License to Chrome perks) is the real endgame stat cap — plan for 3 Technical if you want Edgerunner-tier chrome.
  5. Iconic weapons store on your apartment stash wall — collect them all, the wall displays them.
🎁 Build freedom
