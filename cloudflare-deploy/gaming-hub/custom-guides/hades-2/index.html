Hades II — 100% Completion

Melinoë, Princess of the Underworld and witch of the crossroads, hunts the Titan of Time himself. Supergiant's sequel doubles the original: two full routes (down to Tartarus, up to Olympus), a deep Arcana system, and Chronos waiting at the bottom of it all. This guide covers both routes, every boss, Arcana priorities and the weapon aspects worth your Ash.

⏱ 50-100+ hrs · Hard (scaling Fear system) · ⭐ 9.4/10

⚔ Main Route Progression

▶

The Underworld Route: reach ChronosHard

📍 Erebus → Oceanus → Fields of Mourning → Tartarus · 🎁 The story's first spine — defeating Time itself
  1. Region 1 Erebus: learn the sprint-cast rhythm; Hecate's trial gates your early runs (see Bosses).
  2. Region 2 Oceanus: use the water hazards against sirens; Scylla and her band await.
  3. Region 3 Fields of Mourning: shade-infested wetlands; the Infernal Beast Cerberus (corrupted) guards the gate.
  4. Region 4 Tartarus: elite everything. Take defensive boons before entering; Chronos's door only opens once per run.
  5. Chronos falls multiple times before the story resolves — each defeat advances the plot. Keep going; the true ending needs both routes progressed and key incantations completed.
🎁 The story's first spine — defeating Time itself
▶

The Surface Route: the road to OlympusVery Hard

📍 City of Ephyra → Rift of Thessaly → Mount Olympus · 🎁 Second full campaign spine, Prometheus & Typhon
  1. Unlock with the 'Unraveling a Fateful Bond' incantation at the Cauldron (requires gathered reagents and story progress).
  2. The surface DRAINS your life steadily until you complete the later incantation that stabilizes it — early surface runs are on a literal timer.
  3. Ephyra: clear the siege city, the shade commander's post, and face Polyphemus the Cyclops.
  4. Rift of Thessaly: naval-wreck islands; Eris, Strife incarnate, with your own stolen power.
  5. Mount Olympus gates: the final ascent through Typhon's storm — the two-route structure converges in the endgame chapters.
🎁 Second full campaign spine, Prometheus & Typhon
▶

The Cauldron & IncantationsProgression

📍 The Crossroads hub · 🎁 Every major system unlock in the game
  1. Priority incantations: crafting), the surface route unlock, Permeation of Witching-Wards (removes surface damage-over-time later), and the fishing rod.
  2. Reagents come from gathering tools (see Collectibles) — always carry a tool per run.
  3. Check the cauldron after EVERY run; new incantations appear as the story advances.
  4. Story-critical: incantations involving Hecate's training, the family in the House of Hades, and the Olympian alliance gate the true ending.
  5. Hoard nothing. Ash, Psyche, and Moly spend themselves — banked resources are wasted power.
🎁 Every major system unlock in the game
▶

Defeat Chronos (and keep defeating him)Extreme

📍 House of Hades, deepest Tartarus · 🎁 Story progression, massive Ash/Psyche payouts
  1. Phase 1: his scythe combos end with a delayed time-burst — dodge the swing, then dodge AGAIN on the echo.
  2. His clock-face arena attack freezes zones of the floor; stand in the gaps between the hour-hand sweeps.
  3. Phase 2 he stops time and repositions — hold your dash until time resumes, he always follows with the triple-slam.
  4. Bring a charged cast build or Hestia's scorch — damage-over-time keeps ticking through his defensive phases.
  5. Death Defiance count matters more here than any fight: the Fates' cards granting extra defiance are worth their whole grasp cost.
🎁 Story progression, massive Ash/Psyche payouts

📜 Side Quests & Relationships

▶

Hecate's ApprenticeshipEasy

📍 The Crossroads · 🎁 Story depth, keepsake, training scenes
  1. Gift Nectar early — her keepsake grants a random boon rarity upgrade.
  2. Talk to her after every run — her dialogue tracks your Chronos progress and unlocks training incantations.
  3. Beating her without taking damage, with different weapons, and at high Fear all trigger unique lines.
  4. Her questline interweaves with the true ending — complete her incantations as they appear.
  5. Do not skip the Crossroads conversations generally: Supergiant gates real content behind 'just talking'.
🎁 Story depth, keepsake, training scenes
▶

Nectar, Bath Salts & KeepsakesEasy

📍 All NPCs, everywhere · 🎁 Keepsakes from every character
  1. Priority keepsakes: Hecate's (boon rarity), Athena's aegis-adjacent options as they unlock, Moros's (doom protection), and the family keepsakes from House of Hades NPCs.
  2. Gift each NEW character one Nectar immediately — first-gift keepsakes are always worth it.
  3. Bath Salts unlock via incantation; use them to trigger hangout scenes with Crossroads regulars (Eris included, chaotically).
  4. Odysseus, Nemesis, Moros, Dora, the tutorial skeleton — everyone has an arc. Nemesis's rivalry quest affects runs directly (she invades and duels you for a prize).
  5. Max bonds pay off in the epilogue chapters. The witch remembers kindness.
🎁 Keepsakes from every character
▶

Arachne's Silken ArmorMedium

📍 Hidden chamber, Erebus (spider door) · 🎁 Run-long armor buffs + her questline
  1. Find her spider-marked door in Erebus (it appears semi-randomly among room rewards).
  2. Choose an outfit: each gives armor (a second health bar) plus a passive — take the armor+cast or armor+speed options early.
  3. Her story advances per visit; bring Nectar when the option appears.
  4. Outfit armor synergizes with defensive Arcana cards — a tanky Melinoë shrugs off Tartarus elites.
  5. Her questline resolution is one of the sequel's best-kept secrets. Keep visiting.
🎁 Run-long armor buffs + her questline
▶

Narcissus, Echo & the surface shadesEasy

📍 Oceanus & surface regions · 🎁 Resources, boons, and myth-perfect writing
  1. Narcissus (Oceanus): offers you a choice of gifts — he keeps the better one, obviously. His quest pays off via incantations.
  2. Echo (surface): duplicates one of your resources or boons — one of the best mid-run encounters; prioritize duplicating rare boons.
  3. Medea appears in Ephyra with curse-flavored deals — her prices are steep but her poisons are real.
  4. Icarus (surface): offers armaments and explosive support; his arc ties to Daedalus lore.
  5. Treat every named shade as a questline. They all resolve if you keep showing up.
🎁 Resources, boons, and myth-perfect writing

💎 Collectibles & Resources

▶

Arcana Cards (25 to awaken)Progression

📍 Altar of Ashes, the Crossroads · 🎁 Permanent run buffs — the sequel's mirror system
  1. Early priorities: slow time while casting), Death Defiance-adjacent survivability), Eternity (Death Defiance), the attack/speed economy cards.
  2. Grasp is limited: cheap 1-cost cards often beat expensive ones per point — build wide first.
  3. Some cards awaken ONLY by meeting hidden conditions (activating surrounding cards in patterns) — hidden cards telegraph via the spread layout.
  4. Spend Ash freely; it flows steadily from runs.
  5. Re-spec costs nothing — retune the spread per weapon and route.
🎁 Permanent run buffs — the sequel's mirror system
▶

Gathering Tools & ReagentsEasy

📍 Tool rack at the Crossroads · 🎁 Silver, herbs, fish, souls — the crafting economy
  1. Unlock the Crescent Pick (Silver ore), roots), captured souls), and Fishing Rod via early incantations.
  2. Match tool to need: Silver bottlenecks early (weapons + incantations) — pick the pick until you own every weapon.
  3. Fishing: cast at glowing pools, on the bob — region fish feed big incantations.
  4. Psyche extraction (Tablet) fuels the Grasp expansions — switch to it once Silver flows.
  5. The resource-doubling Arcana and Echo's duplication turn one run into a warehouse.
🎁 Silver, herbs, fish, souls — the crafting economy
▶

Weapon Aspects (Nocturnal Arms)Progression

📍 Silver Pool, the Crossroads · 🎁 Every weapon's alternate forms
  1. Unlock order suggestion: Witch's Staff (starter, excellent), Sister Blades (melee burst), rotating projectiles), Moonstone Axe (heavy AoE), blast-shell rushdown), Black Coat (endgame unlock).
  2. Aspects unlock with resources at the Silver Pool; each has a Melinoë aspect plus mythic alternates (Medea, Pan, Momus, Charon and stranger names).
  3. Hidden aspects reveal after using a weapon enough and progressing story — check the pool after boss milestones.
  4. Daedalus Hammers in-run modify the weapon further; aspect + hammer combos define the meta (Pan blades' seeking knives, experiment).
  5. Pick one weapon per session and learn its Omega moves — mastery beats variety in Fear climbing.
🎁 Every weapon's alternate forms
▶

Chaos Trials & the Fated ListHard

📍 Chaos gates / Crossroads ledger · 🎁 Trial rewards, completion tracking
  1. Chaos gates cost health to enter; their boons trade a temporary curse for scaling power — take them when your sustain is good.
  2. Chaos's named trials remix encounters with mutators for premium rewards.
  3. The Fated List equivalent (prophecies) pays resources for milestones you'd hit anyway — check it to aim your next unlock.
  4. Fear scales through the Vow system: raise vows selectively — enemy speed and boss vows pay best per difficulty point.
  5. 32 Fear clears on both routes is the long-haul completionist flex. Pace yourself, witch.
🎁 Trial rewards, completion tracking

📋 Boss Strategies

▶

Hecate (Erebus Guardian)Medium

📍 Erebus depths · 🎁 Route progress, first-clear resources
  1. Her burst-fire volleys punish greed — weave attacks between her cast gaps.
  2. SHEEP HEX: she polymorphs you if the spell connects — sprint the pattern's gaps; as a sheep, just run until it expires.
  3. Clone phase: hit clones once to pop them; the real one flinches differently. AoE clears the lineup fast.
  4. Her summoned cauldron zones the arena — destroy it or fight in the remaining space.
  5. She respects your growth. The fight tightens as story advances — later versions add patterns. Stay humble.
🎁 Route progress, first-clear resources
▶

Scylla and the SirensMedium-Hard

📍 Oceanus stage · 🎁 Route progress, region resources
  1. Three targets share the stage: Scylla (melee diva), the axe-wielder, and the drummer summoning shockwaves.
  2. Kill the DRUMMER first — attacks. Then the guitarist, then Scylla.
  3. Scylla enrages as bandmates fall — her spotlight beam sweeps demand pre-positioned sprints.
  4. During the 'encore' (final third), the stage floods with projectiles set to the song — the rhythm literally telegraphs the bullet waves.
  5. It is the best boss theme Supergiant has ever written. You will die to it dancing. Acceptable.
🎁 Route progress, region resources
▶

Eris, Strife IncarnateVery Hard

📍 Rift of Thessaly (surface) · 🎁 Surface route progress
  1. She wields rapid-fire barrages and lobbed explosives simultaneously — treat it as a bullet-hell duel, not a brawl.
  2. Her self-buff stacks damage over the fight: burst her early or out-sustain the scaling.
  3. Close range shuts down her gun arcs; she counters with radial bursts — dash THROUGH her, hit, dash out.
  4. Mid-fight she detonates the arena edges: the safe zone shrinks — hold center control.
  5. Post-fight she keeps meddling at the Crossroads with strife mechanics. Strife incarnate, as advertised.
🎁 Surface route progress
▶

Prometheus & the Olympus gauntletExtreme

📍 Mount Olympus approach · 🎁 Endgame progression
  1. Fire everywhere: his arena stacks flame zones — mobility Arcana and Hermes boons are near-mandatory.
  2. His flame-titan patterns chain: dodge the thrown fire, then the ground eruption follows your feet — keep moving post-dodge.
  3. He phases with invulnerable monologues — reposition and top up your power during them.
  4. Bring damage-over-time or high burst; mid-tempo builds get ground down by the arena chip.
  5. Beyond him: the Typhon confrontation crowns the surface route. Full build, full Death Defiance, no shame in lowering Vows for the first clear.
🎁 Endgame progression

💡 Pro Tips & Secrets

▶

Learn the Omega moves — they're the gameMedium

📍 Every run · 🎁 Doubled damage ceilings
  1. Hold attack/special/cast to channel the Omega version — boons scale Omegas separately and spectacularly.
  2. The binding circle cast is your crowd-control anchor: drop it, sprint, channel Omegas while enemies stand rooted.
  3. Arcana (The Sorceress, regen options) turn Omega spam into a playstyle.
  4. Sprint-casting: many boons trigger effects on sprint — Melinoë wants to MOVE, not turret.
  5. If you're playing it like Hades I (mash attack, dash-strike), you're playing at half power. Channel the witch.
🎁 Doubled damage ceilings
▶

Boon duo & legendary huntingMedium

📍 Olympian boons · 🎁 Run-defining power spikes
  1. Check requirements in the boon UI — legendaries list exact prerequisite boons.
  2. New gods (Apollo, Hestia, Hephaestus, Selene) pair with returners — Hestia's scorch + Hephaestus blast is a bruiser classic; Apollo's daze stacks safety.
  3. Selene's Hexes are a separate moon-magic system: her selection screen upgrades hexes down a talent path — one hex per run, choose for your build.
  4. Reroll charms from the reroll Arcana convert bad luck into consistency — worth Grasp in every spread.
  5. God keepsakes force first-boon gods: open with your build's core god, swap keepsakes between regions.
🎁 Run-defining power spikes
▶

Resource loop: run with purposeEasy

📍 The Crossroads planning · 🎁 3x faster progression
  1. Before departing, check: which incantation is blocked, on what reagent, from which region and tool?
  2. Take the matching tool and route — a deliberate farming run beats three aimless ones.
  3. Raise Fear on your comfortable weapon for better rewards once clears feel safe.
  4. Alternate routes between sessions: surface and underworld feed different materials.
  5. The animal familiars (Frinos the frog, Toula the cat, etc.) gather bonus resources — raise them via gifts and bring them along.
🎁 3x faster progression
▶

Death is dialogueEasy

📍 The Crossroads · 🎁 The actual story
  1. After every run, full lap of the Crossroads: Hecate, Nemesis, Moros, Dora, Odysseus, every present NPC.
  2. Story beats (Chronos taunts, family revelations) unlock incantations and route changes — dialogue IS the quest tracker.
  3. The true ending is many, many runs deep. There is no 'one final boss kill and credits' — the narrative braids both routes.
  4. Gift regularly; relationship scenes gate epilogue content.
  5. You are not losing. You are gathering. The moon sees everything, and so should you.
🎁 The actual story
