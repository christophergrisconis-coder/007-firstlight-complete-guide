Baldur's Gate 3 — 100% Completion

0%

Larian's Game-of-the-Year juggernaut. A mind flayer tadpole squirms behind your eye and the only cure runs through three acts of the deepest RPG ever shipped. This guide tracks the critical path, companion loyalty quests, legendary gear and the fights that end Honour Mode runs.

⏱ 75-150 hrs · Scales (Explorer → Honour Mode) · ⭐ 9.7/10

Act 1: Find a Cure / Save the GroveMedium

📍 Wilderness — Emerald Grove & Goblin Camp · 🎁 Camp allies, massive XP, companion approval
  1. After the Nautiloid crash, recruit Shadowheart (beach), Astarion (rocks south), Gale (waypoint stone), Lae'zel (trapped in a cage north).
  2. Enter the Emerald Grove, defuse the Aradin/Zevlor standoff, and meet Halsin's druids.
  3. Best route: kill the three goblin leaders at the Shattered Sanctum — Priestess Gut (lure her to her private room), or just fight), and Minthara.
  4. Free Halsin from the worg pens first (he's a bear) — he fights alongside you.
  5. Return to the grove for the tiefling party, Wyll and Karlach's approvals, and the Nature's Snare / grove vendor rewards. Rescue Volo from the goblin camp beforehand — his 'eye surgery' at camp grants permanent See Invisibility.
🎁 Camp allies, massive XP, companion approval
▶

Act 1: The Underdark → Grymforge RouteMedium-Hard

📍 Underdark, beneath the Blighted Village · 🎁 Adamantine gear, Level 5-6, best act transition
  1. Enter via the Zhentarim well in Blighted Village, the phase-spider chasm, solution: rotate until the dark circles fill the corner plates) behind Priestess Gut.
  2. In the Underdark: save the Myconid colony, expose the duergar impostors, and grab the Phalar Aluve singing sword from the stone near the Festering Cove.
  3. Take the boat from the Decrepit Village to Grymforge.
  4. In Grymforge, save the gnome Nere or take his head (Absolute allies), and clear the rubble with Runepowder or the dwarf wedge.
  5. Forge Adamantine gear: place Mithral Ore + a mould, pull the lava lever, then smash the forge crucible. The Adamantine Splint Armour and Shield make Act 2 dramatically easier. Beat Grym by luring him onto the crucible platform and hammering him when superheated.
🎁 Adamantine gear, Level 5-6, best act transition
▶

Act 2: The Shadow-Cursed Lands & Moonrise TowersHard

📍 Shadow-Cursed Lands · 🎁 Access to Moonrise, Nightsong revelation
  1. Enter with a Moon Lantern (take Kar'niss's, or the broken one fixed later) or travel with pixie blessing — free the pixie inside the lantern for the party-wide immunity blessing.
  2. Base at Last Light Inn: meet Jaheira, and DO NOT let Isobel die when Marcus attacks — her fall dooms the inn and everyone in it.
  3. Do the Gauntlet of Shar trials (Soft-Step, Self-Same, Faith-Leap) to reach the Shadowfell. Faith-Leap trial: walk the invisible path matching the floor mural — throw items to mark tiles if lost.
  4. THE choice: at the Shadowfell, either let Shadowheart kill the Nightsong (dark Shadowheart path, Selûne gear locked) or convince her to spare Dame Aylin (Aylin joins the war, best ending path).
  5. Storm Moonrise Towers, free the prisoners (tieflings + gnomes), then face Ketheric Thorm at the top and follow him down into the Mind Flayer Colony for the true fight.
🎁 Access to Moonrise, Nightsong revelation
▶

Act 2 Finale: Ketheric ThormHard

📍 Mind Flayer Colony beneath Moonrise · 🎁 Level 8-9, Act 3 access, a Netherstone
  1. Before the fight, and disable the colony reinforcements.
  2. Bring Dame Aylin (if spared) — she removes Ketheric's invincibility narrative-style and is an unkillable damage machine.
  3. Stage 1 rooftop: burst Ketheric to half; he retreats. Stage 2 in the colony: he raises undead — focus HIM, not the adds.
  4. Stage 3 Apostle of Myrkul: it eats necromites to heal — to it.
  5. Myrkul's bone-cage AoE hits hard: spread out, keep ranged DPS on the wings, and burn Aylin's misty step engagements. Loot the Nether­stone and Ketheric's Shield afterward.
🎁 Level 8-9, Act 3 access, a Netherstone
▶

Act 3: Gather Your Allies & the Two NetherstonesHard

📍 Baldur's Gate — Lower City · 🎁 Orin's and Gortash's Netherstones
  1. Gortash: attend his coronation at Wyrm's Rock first (peaceful), then disarm the Steel Watch by blowing the Steel Watch Foundry (free the gnomes, overload the runepowder core with Wulbren's bomb). Without Watchers, Gortash fights nearly alone in his audience chamber — kill him on the checkered floor and dodge his trap grid.
  2. Orin: she kidnaps a companion or camp follower (whoever you've used least — keep Yenna the kid safe). Find the Bhaal Temple beneath the Undercity via the Lower City sewers.
  3. Pass the Murder Tribunal: bring Sarevok's test or slay your way through; accepting 'Unholy Assassin' has costs — refusing is fine.
  4. Duel Orin at the Bhaal altar: she starts as Slayer form. Bless/Protection from Evil helps against her multiattack; her Bone Chill aura punishes melee — one ranged heavy hitter (Karlach throwing, Astarion archery) trivializes her adds.
  5. With three Netherstones, you're ready for the Morphic Pool. Do companion quests FIRST — the point of no return is boarding the boat.
🎁 Orin's and Gortash's Netherstones
▶

Act 3 Finale: The NetherbrainVery Hard

📍 Morphic Pool → High Hall · 🎁 The ending(s)
  1. At the Morphic Pool, dominate the brain with the stones — it resists, forcing the Netherbrain assault on the city.
  2. Choose your Orpheus/Emperor allegiance: freeing Orpheus (needs the Orphic Hammer from Raphael's House of Hope) keeps Lae'zel's best ending; someone must become illithid (Orpheus volunteers, or Karlach...).
  3. Call EVERY ally you recruited across three acts at the High Hall battle — they arrive in waves as summonable reinforcements.
  4. Climbing the brainstem: use Misty Step/Fly liberally; the Netherbrain's Imperative pulse forces saves — Gith psionics and Bless stack your odds.
  5. At the Crown: hold the platform until the portal opens, dominate the brain, and choose the fate of the Absolute. Save beforehand — there are many endings worth seeing.
🎁 The ending(s)

Karlach: The Infernal EngineMedium

📍 Act 1 riverbank → Act 3 Avernus choice · 🎁 The best girl at full power, Infernal Iron upgrades
  1. Recruit her at the Risen Road riverbank (Act 1); kill the fake paladins of Tyr hunting her (Anders' group at the toll house).
  2. Bring Infernal Iron (Blighted Village smithy basement, Zhentarim hideout, Grymforge) to Dammon the tiefling smith at the Emerald Grove.
  3. Act 2: find Dammon again at Last Light Inn with more iron for upgrade two — she can finally touch people.
  4. Act 3: Dammon (Forge of the Nine, Lower City) tells her the truth: the engine can't be fixed on this plane.
  5. Endgame choice: she burns out on the docks, returns to Avernus (best: send Wyll or go with her yourself), or takes the mind flayer path. Gortash's death is personal for her — bring her to that fight.
🎁 The best girl at full power, Infernal Iron upgrades
▶

Astarion: The Pale Elf & Cazador's RitualHard

📍 Act 1 camp bites → Act 3 Szarr Palace · 🎁 Ascended or Spawn Astarion, Bhaalist-tier gear
  1. Let him bite you at camp in Act 1 (survive the check) — honesty builds his best arc. Find Raphael at Yurgir's lair in Act 2 to decode the scars.
  2. Act 3: infiltrate the Szarr Palace via the Lower City. The dungeon beneath holds thousands of caged spawn.
  3. Free or question the Gur children and Sebastian in the cells — this decides Astarion's conscience later.
  4. Cazador fight: interrupt the ritual by pulling Astarion off the ritual frame FIRST (send a fast character up). Kill the werewolf and spawn adds while Cazador is in mist form phases.
  5. Final choice: let Astarion complete the ritual (Ascended — powerful, darker romance) or stop him (Spawn — walks into his own freedom). Sparing the 7,000 spawn with the Gur is the good route.
🎁 Ascended or Spawn Astarion, Bhaalist-tier gear
▶

Shadowheart: Daughter of DarknessMedium

📍 All three acts — Sharran trials & House of Grief · 🎁 Selûne's or Shar's champion, Spear of Night / Moonlight gear
  1. Act 1: bring her to the Owlbear cave's Selûnite altar and the Githyanki crèche for key scenes. the Nightshade flower dialogue at camp.
  2. Act 2: she must be IN the party for the Gauntlet of Shar. Complete all trials and let her make (or be talked out of) the Nightsong kill.
  3. If Aylin is spared: Shadowheart is hunted by Shar — comfort her at the Moonrise docks scene.
  4. Act 3: the House of Grief (Lower City) — Viconia DeVir and the Sharran cloister. This fight is brutal: hold the door choke point, silence the casters.
  5. Choice: no — Shar's price is their death OR her pain; sparing them costs her Shar's gifts, the truest ending) or complete her Dark Justiciar path if the Nightsong died.
🎁 Selûne's or Shar's champion, Spear of Night / Moonlight gear
▶

Save Vlaakith's Prize: Lae'zel & the CrècheMedium-Hard

📍 Rosymorn Monastery, Mountain Pass (Act 1.5) · 🎁 Githyanki gear, the truth about Vlaakith, Blood of Lathander access
  1. Take the Mountain Pass route (do the Underdark FIRST, then return — you can do both before Act 2).
  2. In Crèche Y'llek, let Lae'zel lead dialogue. The zaith'isk purification device: put LAE'ZEL in it — have her resist fully to earn the Awakened psionics buff.
  3. Refuse to hand the Astral Prism to the inquisitor. The whole crèche turns hostile — fight in the inquisitor's room, not the open halls.
  4. Vlaakith herself appears and orders you into the Prism to kill the 'enemy'. Meet the Dream Visitor's true form instead. Do NOT attack Vlaakith (she casts Power Word: Kill on the whole party).
  5. This scene defines Lae'zel's arc through Act 3 (Orpheus). Grab the Blood of Lathander before leaving (see Unique Items).
🎁 Githyanki gear, the truth about Vlaakith, Blood of Lathander access

Soul Coins (Karlach fuel)Medium

📍 Scattered across all acts · 🎁 Karlach's Infernal Fury (+1d4 fire damage rages)
  1. Act 1: one in the dank crypt's sarcophagus room (entrance beach chapel), one on the dead mule outside the Zhentarim Hideout entrance, by Grat the goblin vendor.
  2. Act 1 Underdark: Dhourn's petrified drow (shatter carefully) near the Spectator holds one.
  3. Act 2: Raphael's debtor chest in the Mason's Guild basement; one in the Gauntlet of Shar.
  4. Act 3: buy from Helsik at the Devil's Fee occult shop.
  5. Slot them into Karlach at camp — she gains bonus fire damage while raging. Save 2 for the endgame fights.
🎁 Karlach's Infernal Fury (+1d4 fire damage rages)

Infernal Iron (x4+)Easy

📍 Acts 1-2 · 🎁 Karlach's engine repairs
  1. Blighted Village: locked chest in the smithy basement (the house with the ladder under a hatch).
  2. Zhentarim Hideout: shelf in the storage cave behind the leader.
  3. Grymforge: near the Adamantine Forge dock and on a corpse by the rubble.
  4. Reithwin Mason's Guild (Act 2) holds another.
  5. You need one per upgrade (two total for her arc) — the rest sell well or serve crafting flavor.
🎁 Karlach's engine repairs

Umbral Gems (x3)Hard

📍 Gauntlet of Shar, Act 2 · 🎁 Shadowfell access + Yurgir's loot
  1. Soft-Step Trial: a stealth maze — send one sneaky character, use Minor Illusion to distract the shadows, grab the gem at the end.
  2. Self-Same Trial: fight dark mirrors of your party. Strip your party's gear BEFORE starting — the clones copy your equipment. Re-equip and stomp them.
  3. Faith-Leap Trial: invisible bridge over a void; the floor mural in the entry room maps the path. Feather Fall insurance recommended.
  4. The third gem belongs to Yurgir the Orthon (Astarion's quest ties here): fight him on the balcony or lawyer him into suicide via the Rat/Contract dialogue chain (epic Persuasion checks).
  5. Slot all three into the pedestal by the great statue and ride the platform down to the Shadowfell.
🎁 Shadowfell access + Yurgir's loot

The Owlbear CubEasy (emotionally hard)

📍 Owlbear Nest, Act 1 · 🎁 A camp owlbear (and a war mount of pure love)
  1. Find the Owlbear Nest cave west of the Blighted Village (river gorge).
  2. Fight or pacify the mother (Animal Handling / Illithid check to leave peacefully). If she dies, spare the cub.
  3. Later, the cub appears at the GOBLIN CAMP being tormented — win the 'chicken chase' or pass checks to claim it, then tell it to find your camp.
  4. Feed it at camp over several long rests. Do not let Krolla goad you into hurting it.
  5. It grows through the acts and joins the final battle as a summonable ally. Non-negotiable content.
🎁 A camp owlbear (and a war mount of pure love)

Necromancy of Thay + Silver AmuletMedium

📍 Blighted Village cellar, Act 1 · 🎁 Speak with Dead at will, Forbidden Knowledge (+1 Wisdom saves), Danse Macabre later
  1. In the Blighted Village apothecary's cellar, pull the lever behind the shelf (Perception to spot the crack) to open the hidden crypt.
  2. Grab the Necromancy of Thay from the vault (mirror puzzle: answer as a servant of the dead — '; answers: befriend the mirror by claiming to be an ally of its master and admitting the balm ingredients).
  3. You need the Dark Amethyst from the phase spider matriarch's pit (Whispering Depths well) to unlock the book.
  4. Read it and pass all three Wisdom saves for Forbidden Knowledge (permanent +1 to Wisdom saves and Speak with Dead).
  5. Keep the book: at Act 3's Sorcerous Sundries vault (Ramazith's Tower), open it with the Tharchiate Codex knowledge to gain Danse Macabre (summon 4 ghouls). Astarion has personal dialogue for the whole arc.
🎁 Speak with Dead at will, Forbidden Knowledge (+1 Wisdom saves), Danse Macabre later

Blood of Lathander (Legendary Mace)Medium

📍 Rosymorn Monastery, Mountain Pass · 🎁 Sunbeam mace, blindness aura vs undead, revive-once buff
  1. Solve the stained-glass altar puzzle on the monastery roof-level: place the Ceremonial Weapons (Battleaxe in the Guardian room, Warhammer from the eagle nest, the Dawnmaster's crest area) on the correct altars matching the windows.
  2. The Dawnmaster's Crest is the key: loot it from the secret room revealed by the puzzle.
  3. In the Inquisitor's chamber below, find the hidden door (west wall) to the Lathander vault.
  4. CRITICAL: inserting the mace triggers a solar cannon countdown that destroys the monastery. Use the Dawnmaster's Crest on the twin pedestals to disarm it BEFORE pulling the mace.
  5. The mace: +1d6 radiant, blinds undead/fiends nearby, with a Sunbeam charge. Karlach or a paladin carries it beautifully for two acts.
🎁 Sunbeam mace, blindness aura vs undead, revive-once buff
▶

Nyrulna (Legendary Trident)Medium

📍 Circus of the Last Days, Rivington — Akabi's prize · 🎁 Returning thunder trident, +3, flight zone on throw
  1. At the Circus of the Last Days (Rivington, Act 3), play Akabi's Spinning Wheel game.
  2. He cheats. Pickpocket his Djinni Ring first (high Sleight of Hand — use Astarion with guidance and invisibility backup).
  3. Spin again wearing/holding knowledge of the ring — with the ring stolen you WIN, and furious Akabi teleports you to a jungle 'prize' zone.
  4. In the jungle, survive, loot the chest for Nyrulna, and take the portal back.
  5. Nyrulna: +3 trident, 1d6 thunder, returns when thrown, AoE explosion on impact, and grants flight-like jump in its zone. The best throwing weapon for Karlach/EK throwers.
🎁 Returning thunder trident, +3, flight zone on throw

Orphic Hammer (Legendary)Very Hard

📍 Raphael's House of Hope, Act 3 · 🎁 +3 warhammer, advantage vs fiends, frees Orpheus
  1. Visit Helsik at the Devil's Fee shop; buy the ritual components (~20k gold or steal them) and draw the pentagram in the attic (place items per her diagram: skull, diamond, coins, incense, infernal marble).
  2. In the House of Hope, — grab the hammer from the archive (use Gale's or a scroll of Knock/thievery; the orb alarms regardless).
  3. Free Hope the imprisoned deva on the way (her boudoir cell, behind the illusory wall) — she's your win condition for the escape fight.
  4. Raphael ambushes you at the exit in full Archdevil form with Hope's prison pillars fueling him: destroy the four soul pillars FAST, keep Hope alive, she casts divine intervention-tier heals.
  5. Escape through the boudoir portal. The hammer breaks Orpheus's chains at the endgame — enabling the best githyanki/Lae'zel resolution. Also it's just a great +3 hammer.
🎁 +3 warhammer, advantage vs fiends, frees Orpheus
▶

Balduran's Giantslayer (Legendary Greatsword)Very Hard

📍 Wyrmway, beneath Wyrm's Rock · 🎁 +3 greatsword, doubles Strength damage bonus, advantage vs Large+
  1. Find the Wyrmway entrance in Wyrm's Rock prison: strike the two braziers with lightning damage to reveal the hidden passage.
  2. Pass the four trials: Chess (Lanceboard — destroy the enemy pieces with fire), Courage (stand in the flame circle), Self (kill your dopplegangers), Wisdom (answer with mercy-themed choices).
  3. Ansur's lair: the skeletal bronze dragon rises. Bring the Emperor's dialogue — it recontextualizes everything.
  4. Fight: his lightning breath charges a mega-nova (Stormheart Nova) — get BEHIND the pillars when he channels. Adds spawn at the walls; ignore them if your burst is high.
  5. Giantslayer rewards strength builds absurdly: everything Large or bigger with advantage. with Elixirs of Cloud Giant Strength for meme damage.
🎁 +3 greatsword, doubles Strength damage bonus, advantage vs Large+
▶

Helldusk Armour (Legendary Plate)Very Hard

📍 House of Hope, his archive · 🎁 AC 21, fire immunity-ish, Fly, no Strength requirement
  1. It's Raphael's armor: defeat him in the House of Hope escape battle (see Orphic Hammer entry).
  2. Alternative for pacifists: none. The devil must fall.
  3. Focus the four soul pillars first — Raphael heals and empowers off them; each destroyed pillar weakens him.
  4. Silence/Counterspell his Firestorm casts; Hope (freed deva) tanks his cambion adds.
  5. Helldusk Plate: 21 AC, proficiency granted to ANY wearer (Gale in full plate!), fire resistance, immune to burning, and 1/day Fly. found in the Iron Throne) for the set fantasy.
🎁 AC 21, fire immunity-ish, Fly, no Strength requirement

Raphael (House of Hope)Extreme

📍 House of Hope, Act 3 · 🎁 Helldusk Armour, 30k+ XP, Orphic Hammer escape
  1. Pre-buff EVERYTHING before touching the hammer: Aid, Heroes' Feast, Death Ward, elixirs — buffs persist into the ambush.
  2. Turn 1: split damage between the nearest two soul pillars (each gives Raphael stacking power). Haste your bruiser.
  3. Keep Hope alive at range — her Divine Intervention and heals swing the fight. boudoir hall before the fight).
  4. Raphael's Ascendant form gets legendary resistances — burn them with cheap saves (Tasha's, Hold Person won't work; use Command: Grovel via multiple casters).
  5. Kill order: pillars → cambions harassing Hope → Raphael. Loot Helldusk Armour and walk out through the boudoir portal like you own the place.
🎁 Helldusk Armour, 30k+ XP, Orphic Hammer escape
▶

Grym (Adamantine Forge)Hard (or free with the hammer)

📍 Grymforge, Act 1 · 🎁 Grymskull Helm, forge access for a second item
  1. Set the forge: mould in, mithral in, pull the lava valve so the arena floods — Grym spawns aggressive.
  2. Grym takes real damage ONLY while Superheated (standing in lava does this to him).
  3. He targets whoever hit him last: bait him onto the central crucible platform with a ranged poke.
  4. When he stands on the crucible, pull the forge hammer lever — the smash deals ~100 and staggers. Two smashes end him.
  5. Grym hits hard: keep melee out of his cleave arc, rotate the bait role. His Grymskull Helm gives fire resistance and 1/day Hunter's Mark. Forge the splint armor first, shield second if you return.
🎁 Grymskull Helm, forge access for a second item
▶

Myrkul's Apostle (Ketheric, true form)Hard

📍 Mind Flayer Colony · 🎁 Act 2 completion, Netherstone
  1. Position BEFORE dialogue ends: high ground on the side platforms, ranged spread wide, melee near the stairs.
  2. Kill every necromite on sight — his abilities.
  3. Dame Aylin (if saved) solos his aggro half the fight; heal her, don't tank yourself.
  4. His bone palm AoE targets clumps: never stack two characters. Globe of Invulnerability (scroll) trivializes his turn if things go wrong.
  5. He's undead-adjacent: Turn Undead won't land, but radiant damage rips. Shadowheart's Guardian of Faith on his hitbox does free damage every turn.
🎁 Act 2 completion, Netherstone
▶

Orin the Red (Slayer Form)Hard

📍 Bhaal Temple, Act 3 · 🎁 Netherstone, Bloodthirst dagger
  1. Rescue her hostage FIRST (whoever she kidnapped) by keeping dialogue calm — attacking early can kill the hostage.
  2. Her Unhallowed Aura projects a bone-chill zone; fight from the entrance stairs.
  3. Slayer form multiattacks 3x: Shield spells, Mirror Image, and Blade Ward keep your frontline alive.
  4. Her cultist adds are fanatics but fragile — one Fireball/Spirit Guardians sweep clears them.
  5. Stunning her (Monk) or Hold Monster (she burns legendary resistance fast) opens crit windows. She drops Bloodthirst (piercing vulnerability dagger) — give it to Astarion and watch numbers explode.
🎁 Netherstone, Bloodthirst dagger

Long rest MORE, not lessEasy

📍 Camp · 🎁 You literally get more story
  1. Major companion scenes, romance beats, and tadpole plot events trigger ONLY on long rest.
  2. Rest after every significant quest beat — if a companion 'wants to talk', rest immediately.
  3. Partial rests (no supplies) still trigger some scenes. Supplies are everywhere; 40 is a trivial cost by mid-Act 1.
  4. Some sequences warn you time is critical — only a handful truly advance timers (the grove attack won't happen just from resting).
  5. If you finished Act 1 with fewer than ~15 long rests, you missed scenes. Seriously.
🎁 You literally get more story
▶

Shove, throw, and barrels are legalEasy

📍 Every fight · 🎁 Free wins
  1. Shove is a BONUS action: chasm-adjacent enemies (Dror Ragzlin's rafters, Grymforge ledges, Moonrise walls) can just be pushed.
  2. Height gives ranged attackers a real edge: archers on roofs win fights before they start.
  3. Smokepowder barrels stack: pre-place them during stealth (out of combat), then detonate turn 1. The Steel Watch Foundry keeps a supply room of them.
  4. Throwing enemies AT enemies is real: a Strength build can throw a goblin into another goblin for double prone.
  5. Water + lightning, grease + fire, wet + cold = doubled crowd control. Carry Create Water on someone forever.
🎁 Free wins
▶

Respec is 100 gold — abuse itEasy

📍 Withers, camp · 🎁 Perfect party always
  1. Wake Withers in the Dank Crypt beyond the beach chapel — he simply walks to your camp forever.
  2. Any companion can be ANY class — try Shadowheart as a Storm Sorcerer. Point is: nothing is locked.
  3. Level 1 respec resets ability scores too — fix any build mistake for 100g.
  4. Hirelings from Withers fill gaps (a bard lockpicker, a cleric buffbot for camp casting).
  5. Camp-cast trick: a hireling casts Aid/Heroes' Feast, you swap them out — buffs persist. Degenerate and wonderful.
🎁 Perfect party always
▶

Talk to the dead, the doors, and the ratsEasy

📍 Everywhere · 🎁 Hidden quests & solutions
  1. Cast Speak with Animals daily (or eat the potion — Alchemy makes them cheap): rats, dogs, cats and squirrels carry quest hints and pure comedy.
  2. Speak with Dead works on almost every named corpse — murders in Baldur's Gate practically solve themselves.
  3. Corpses refuse to talk if they watched you kill them — kill with surprise or use a disguise.
  4. The Amulet of Lost Voices (Dank Crypt) gives free Speak with Dead forever — slot it on a bonus character.
  5. Disguise Self changes racial dialogue checks: a githyanki face opens the crèche; a drow face commands goblins.
🎁 Hidden quests & solutions
