Astro Bot — 100% Completion

0%

The Game of the Year 2024 winner and the PS5's purest joy machine. Astro's mothership — the PS5 itself — is torn apart by a galactic villain, scattering 300 bots across six galaxies. Rescue them all, ride the Dual Speeder, and collect three decades of PlayStation history. This guide covers every galaxy, the hidden exits, VIP bot locations, and the Master Challenge that will test your soul.

⏱ 12-25 hrs · Easy (with brutal optional challenges) · ⭐ 9.4/10

Crash Site & Galaxy 1: Gorilla NebulaEasy

📍 The Crash Site hub · 🎁 Hub unlock, first boss, core moveset
  1. Learn the trinity: jump, hover (laser feet damage enemies below), and the spin punch.
  2. The Crash Site hub grows with every rescue — return between levels to play with rescued bots and unearth buried hub secrets (the sand hides buttons, buried surprises).
  3. Gorilla Nebula levels teach the power-up rhythm: the power gloves, sponge, etc.).
  4. Boss: the first mothership part — punch the fists back, climb the arms in phase two.
  5. Rescue priority: VIP bots (gold sparkle, PlayStation legends) unlock hub cameos — Nathan Drake and kin are all here.
🎁 Hub unlock, first boss, core moveset

Galaxies 2-3: the middle worldsEasy-Medium

📍 Galaxy map · 🎁 Mothership parts, gadget mastery
  1. The ocean galaxy: the octopus boss and swimming levels — Astro swims with full moveset, no drowning anxiety, pure play.
  2. The sponge gadget levels (absorb water, slam to release) hide the most secret exits in the game — soak EVERYTHING.
  3. The sky galaxy: the barking rocket gadget opens dash-platforming; its levels hide secret exits.
  4. Mini-levels (single-mechanic wonders) branch off the map — none are skippable if you want full rescue counts.
  5. Boss rhythm continues: each galaxy's finale drops a mothership part (GPU, fan, SSD — the PS5 rebuilds itself piece by piece).
🎁 Mothership parts, gadget mastery

Galaxies 4-5: Twin Peaks & the villain's trailMedium

📍 Galaxy map, later worlds · 🎁 Escalating set pieces, penultimate showdowns
  1. The slow-time gadget levels are the puzzle peak — freeze waterfalls and projectiles mid-air and climb them.
  2. The casino level and the Bot of War level (full God of War homage with axe recall!) are the showcase — every big PlayStation franchise gets a themed level with a VIP crew to rescue.
  3. Track hidden exits: show incomplete markers on the map — the exits hide inside background details (posters, cracks, odd doors).
  4. Lady Venomara and the later bosses layer mechanics — hover-laser weak points glow orange, always.
  5. Nebulax's tells grow through cutscenes: he's stolen the PS5's CPU as a crown. The audacity.
🎁 Escalating set pieces, penultimate showdowns
▶

Finale: Nebulax & the rebuilt mothershipMedium

📍 The final galaxy · 🎁 Credits, post-game unlock wave
  1. The approach gauntlet remixes every gadget — quick refreshers, nothing unfair.
  2. Nebulax fights in stolen-hardware phases: his hardware weapons, punch back projectiles, laser the exposed core between phases.
  3. Your rescued bots physically join the finale — rescue counts change the ending's texture (rescue everyone first for the full moment).
  4. Post-credits: the master challenge galaxy unlocks, plus hub construction finishes.
  5. Stay through the credits minigame. Team Asobi doesn't waste a single screen.
🎁 Credits, post-game unlock wave

📍 Galaxy transit + dedicated levels · 🎁 The DualSense showcase
  1. Adaptive trigger resistance IS the throttle feedback — fly by feel, the rumble maps terrain under you.
  2. Flight levels hide bots in off-course detours: throttle down and sweep edges.
  3. Boost timing through rings feeds level completion.
  4. The speeder sections gate nothing hard — they're palate cleansers with secrets.

The Bull Gloves (punch across the world)Easy

📍 Gorilla Nebula levels onward · 🎁 Grapple-punch traversal and combat reach
  1. Punch pulls: fist anchors to grapple points, Astro follows — chain anchor-to-anchor for flow sections.
  2. Combat: charged punches break armored enemies frontally (normally hover-laser-only targets).
  3. Secret-tech: punch-hover chaining extends horizontal reach past intended gaps — several early secrets assume it.
  4. Glove levels hide the most VIP bots per capita — the mechanic invites off-path anchors.
  5. The final boss assumes glove fluency. The tutorial was three galaxies long; you're ready.
🎁 Grapple-punch traversal and combat reach

The Sponge, the transformation suiteEasy

📍 One gadget per level, by design · 🎁 Each level's whole identity
  1. Sponge: absorb water to giant-size, slam to blast — soak/squeeze EVERY suspicious surface for secrets.
  2. Time-stopper: freeze projectiles and ride them; the puzzle depth peaks here.
  3. Rocket dog (the dashing dog): horizontal dashes through walls — dash-jump keeps skyward momentum.
  4. Each gadget appears, peaks, and bows out in one level — no bloat, pure design confidence.
  5. Post-game challenges remix gadgets in combinations the main game never dared. That's where mastery lives.

Laser Hover (the core verb)Easy

📍 Always equipped · 🎁 Astro's identity
  1. Hover after apex for maximum distance; hover-cancel early to drop-attack enemies below.
  2. The laser damages AND reveals: hidden platforms shimmer under hover-fire.
  3. Hover-laser breaks floor cracks — sweep low over any suspicious tile pattern.
  4. Advanced: jump-spin-hover triple extension crosses gaps the eye reads as impossible.
  5. The Grand Master Challenge's final stretch is a hover exam. Practice the triple extension until boring.

The giant ape (Gorilla Nebula finale)Easy

📍 Galaxy 1 boss arena · 🎁 First mothership part
  1. Phase 1: punch his incoming fists back with timed spins — the rhythm is generous.
  2. Climb the stunned arm to reach the head weak point; hover-laser the glow.
  3. Phase 2 adds fist slams with shockwave rings — jump-hover over rings while tracking fists.
  4. Phase 3 speeds everything up but adds nothing new — trust learned rhythms.
  5. Bots hide in the arena's edges between phases. Even bosses respect the census.
🎁 First mothership part
▶

Wako Tako (the octopus)Medium

📍 Ocean galaxy finale · 🎁 Mothership part, best boss music in the game
  1. Tentacle slams telegraph with shadows — reposition, punch the pinned tentacle, climb to the head.
  2. Ink phases blind sections of the arena: fight from the clean zones, hover across ink pools.
  3. His whirlpool phase pulls inward — jump-hover against the current in pulses, not continuously.
  4. Weak point: the forehead glow between his sunglasses. Disrespect it directly.
  5. Phase transitions rain coins. The economy comforts even mid-boss.
🎁 Mothership part, best boss music in the game

Lady Venomara (the snake)Medium

📍 Later galaxy finale · 🎁 Mothership part
  1. Her strikes shatter platforms permanently per phase — the arena shrinks; economize your ground.
  2. Spit arcs leave floor hazards: bait spits toward already-broken zones.
  3. Punch-return her lunges when they overextend past your platform, then chase the stagger to the head.
  4. The coil phase demands the glove grapple to escape — anchor points flash when it starts.
  5. Late phases interleave every prior pattern at double tempo. The game's midterm exam.
🎁 Mothership part

Nebulax (final boss)Medium-Hard

📍 Final galaxy · 🎁 The ending
  1. Phase rotation mirrors your journey: each stolen part he wields references a galaxy's mechanics.
  2. Punch back everything punchable; hover-laser exposed cores during his recovery gloats.
  3. The crown phase: glove-grapple the orbiting debris to reach his altitude.
  4. Rescued bots swarm assists between phases — your collect-a-thon diligence literally fights beside you.
  5. Damage tells escalate honestly; no cheap shots even at the end. Finish it, roll credits, feel feelings.
