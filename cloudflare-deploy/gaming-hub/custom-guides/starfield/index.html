Starfield — 100% Completion

0%

A thousand systems, an artifact that bends spacetime, and the question of what lies beyond the Unity. Bethesda's space epic — dramatically improved by two years of updates and the Shattered Space expansion — rewards players who chase its faction stories and temple powers. This guide covers the Constellation main quest, all four faction lines, the Mantis and other legendary gear runs, and the NG+ multiverse loop that changes everything.

⏱ 40-200 hrs · Medium · ⭐ 8.6/10

⚔ Main Quests (Constellation)

0%

One Small Step → The Old NeighborhoodEasy

📍 Vectera → New Atlantis → Sol system · 🎁 Frontier ship, Constellation membership, companions
  1. After the Vectera vision, take the Frontier and Vasco to the Lodge in New Atlantis — tour the Lodge fully; the basement workshop is your free crafting hub.
  2. Join Constellation and grab companions: science), Barrett, and soon Sam Coe and Andreja through story beats.
  3. The Old Neighborhood: follow Moara's trail through Sol — Mars's Cydonia, then Venus, then the Nova Galactic Staryard.
  4. First artifact collections teach the loop: scan, dig, vision, return.
  5. Level Boost Pack Training early: jetpacks change every fight and every mountain.
🎁 Frontier ship, Constellation membership, companions
▶

Into the Unknown — temples & the first powerMedium

📍 Constellation leads → scattered temples · 🎁 Anti-Gravity Field power, Starborn revelation
  1. Follow Vladimir's coordinates to two artifact sites, then the anomaly scanner leads to your first temple.
  2. Temple puzzle: the mirror opens — chase the light distortions, thrust through each.
  3. Walk the mirror: receive Anti-Gravity Field, your first power (powers use a separate meter).
  4. The Starborn ship appears afterward — a Starborn confrontation seeds the endgame's central question.
  5. Each future artifact spawns a new temple via Vladimir — powers stack up to 24 across the game (and rank up in NG+).
🎁 Anti-Gravity Field power, Starborn revelation
▶

High Price to Pay — the Lodge attackHard (emotionally)

📍 The Lodge / The Eye · 🎁 The story's hardest choice, permanent consequence
  1. Trigger warning of the quest's stakes: whoever you like most — consider which companion is WITH you versus at the Lodge/Eye.
  2. The attack splits the party: you choose which group to defend; the OTHER location loses a named companion. Your highest-affinity companion at the other site is typically the victim.
  3. There is no save-scumming a perfect outcome — someone dies. This is the story working as intended.
  4. Afterward: the funeral scene, the Hunter's terms, to the endgame begins.
  5. If romancing someone: at your side during this quest chain to protect them.
🎁 The story's hardest choice, permanent consequence
▶

Unearthed → Revelation — the Buried Temple & final gauntletHard

📍 The buried NASA site → Masada III · 🎁 The truth about Earth, endgame convergence
  1. Unearthed: the NASA facility dungeon explains Earth's death — the grav drive's true cost. One of Bethesda's best-written dungeons; read every terminal.
  2. Choose alignment: guidance), the Hunter (power), or neither (independence) — the choice shapes the final battle's sides.
  3. Revelation: the final temple gauntlet at Masada III throws Starborn waves and both Guardians at you depending on your stance.
  4. Siding with neither means fighting BOTH the Hunter and Emissary — hardest, most rewarding version.
  5. At the Unity: enter for NG+ (see Tips) or turn back and keep your universe. Both are endings. Both are beginnings.
🎁 The truth about Earth, endgame convergence
▶

Shattered Space (expansion): Va'ruun'kaiHard

📍 House Va'ruun homeworld · 🎁 The serpent cult's homeworld, new gear tier, allies
  1. Start: respond to the distress signal from the Oracle station drifting in space — the zero-G opening is the expansion's thesis.
  2. Grav-jump to Va'ruun'kai: the city of Dazra anchors a dense handcrafted map (no procedural tiles — all bespoke).
  3. The three Houses' political questlines interleave with the gravity anomaly mystery.
  4. New phantom enemies (the redeemed) demand particle weapons — the expansion's own gear tier answers them.
  5. Choices here decide House Va'ruun's future and echo across the base game's dialogue. Best played level 35+.
🎁 The serpent cult's homeworld, new gear tier, allies

Crimson Fleet: The Best There IsMedium

📍 UC SysDef ↔ The Key · 🎁 The Key as home port, legendary loot, double-agent drama
  1. Entry: via UC contact) — SysDef recruits you to infiltrate the Fleet.
  2. On the Key, earn trust through escalating jobs: the Ragana heist, the evidence gathering — you CHOOSE what evidence to actually deliver to SysDef.
  3. The Legacy's Legacy: a high-security vault run) for the Kryx fortune data.
  4. THE CHOICE at Legacy's End: side with the Fleet (keep the Key, pirate perks) or SysDef (legal, less fun, cleaner conscience). The space battle either way is the game's biggest.
  5. Fleet side pays better: the Keelhaul pistol, the Key's vendors and your own pirate berth. Sarah disapproves. Sarah always disapproves.
🎁 The Key as home port, legendary loot, double-agent drama

UC Vanguard: Grunt Work → A Legacy ForgedMedium

📍 New Atlantis, MAST district · 🎁 Citizenship, the Terrormorph truth, rewards
  1. Sign up at MAST, fly the qualification sim (high scores pay bonuses; the sim is repeatable for the leaderboard easter eggs).
  2. Grunt Work sends you to Tau Ceti's Terrormorph outbreak — the horror mission that sells the whole line.
  3. Follow the conspiracy: Hope's involvement, the Vae Victis reveal beneath MAST (the war criminal running counterintelligence).
  4. Final choice: expose Vae Victis or keep his secret; breeding vs. microbe weapon) — the ecologists' pick.
  5. Rewards: UC citizenship (unlocks the penthouse), Class A citizenship perks and the Vanguard armor line.
🎁 Citizenship, the Terrormorph truth, rewards

Ryujin Industries: corporate espionageMedium

📍 Neon, Ryujin Tower · 🎁 Manipulation skill, the best stealth content, an office with your name on it
  1. Apply at the Ryujin kiosk in Neon; the interview and coffee-run opener is deliberate satire — commit to the bit.
  2. Missions escalate from office politics to full infiltration: stealth builds shine).
  3. The internal mole subplot: read every inter-office email — the reveal is seeded from mission one.
  4. Final board vote on the neuroamp: control it, suppress it, the CEO's play — your Persuasion literally rewrites the vote.
  5. Reward: command NPCs), an executive office, and Neon's best vendor discounts.
🎁 Manipulation skill, the best stealth content, an office with your name on it

Freestar Rangers: The Hammer FallsEasy-Medium

📍 Akila City, The Rock · 🎁 Deputy → Ranger badge, the Star Eagle (best free ship in the game)
  1. Join after the bank hostage situation in Akila City (talk the robber down for style points).
  2. Deputy cases are procedural bounties — clear a few to unlock the core conspiracy chain.
  3. The trail leads through settler grievances to the Ron Hope reveal — the mercs squeezing the freeholds.
  4. Final confrontation at HopeTech: arrest him or gun him down — the Rangers' code allows both readings.
  5. Reward: THE STAR EAGLE — a top-3 ship in the game, free, fully yours. Worth the whole questline alone.
🎁 Deputy → Ranger badge, the Star Eagle (best free ship in the game)

Skill Magazines (100+)Medium

📍 POIs, faction hubs, story dungeons · 🎁 Permanent buffs per issue — they persist into NG+
  1. Key series: Solomon's Adventures (melee), Grunt Issue (combat), resistances), Tracker's Primer (bounties), Nova Galactic Manual (ship systems).
  2. Reliable clusters: the Lodge library, Cydonia's common areas, the Key's lounges, faction HQ side rooms.
  3. Story dungeons average one magazine each — sweep side rooms before objectives.
  4. They persist through NG+ (most collectibles don't) — magazine hunting is multiverse-efficient.
  5. Track via third-party checklists; in-game tracking is skill-menu only.
🎁 Permanent buffs per issue — they persist into NG+
▶

Temple Powers (24)Medium

📍 Anomaly-flagged planets, via scanner · 🎁 The full Starborn powerset
  1. After Into the Unknown, Vladimir feeds temple locations per artifact found; landed anomalies also self-flag via the hand scanner.
  2. Every temple is the same float-through-rings puzzle — thrust efficiency makes them 90-second visits.
  3. Priority powers: Phased Time (bullet time, endgame-defining), Gravity Well (crowd delete), O2 refill), Sense Star Stuff (living-target wallhack).
  4. Powers rank up in NG+ by re-collecting them — higher tiers come from repeat universes.
  5. Power meter regenerates; there is no cost to casting liberally. Be the space wizard.
🎁 The full Starborn powerset

Earth landmarks & the old world relicsEasy

📍 Earth, dead and waiting · 🎁 Earth landmark POIs + lore payoffs
  1. Landmark locations unlock via clues found in the main quest (Unearthed) and museum sites — each book names a site (e.g., the Empire State's surviving spire, the Shard).
  2. Land at flagged coordinates; its base.
  3. Snow globes display on your Lodge room shelf — pure trophy joy.
  4. The trek across dead Earth with the ambient score is the game's best mood piece. Go at local dawn.
  5. Combine with the Unearthed quest for the full 'what we lost' arc in one session.
🎁 Earth landmark POIs + lore payoffs

Unique POIs & the legendary landing spotsMedium

📍 The thousand worlds · 🎁 Handcrafted dungeons hiding endgame gear
  1. Always-good landings: the Almagest casino wreck (Olympus system — jackpot code in the maintenance shaft), a stealable ship), the Colander (horror derelict done right).
  2. Industrial POIs with 'Abandoned' prefixes carry boss-tier loot rooms behind master locks — bring Security 3+.
  3. Science outposts chain into local mini-stories via slates — follow the names.
  4. The Mantis (see Unique Items) remains the gold standard POI chain.
  5. Shattered Space's Dazra outskirts POIs are all handcrafted — the expansion region rewards full sweeps.

The Mantis: Razorleaf ship + armor setMedium (any level)

📍 Secret Outpost, Denebola I-B · 🎁 Free legendary ship + the Mantis armor, both early-game kings
  1. Start: loot the 'Secret Outpost!' slate from any Spacer enemy (common drops in early POIs like the Nova Galactic Staryard).
  2. Fly to Denebola I-B's Secret Outpost: the fan-hideout defenses.
  3. THE FLOOR PUZZLE: the pressure-plate corridor spells a word — step only the letters T-Y-R-A-N-N-I-S ('Sic semper tyrannis' — the clue notes spell it out; wrong tiles trigger turrets).
  4. Claim the Mantis armor (top-tier stats into the 30s) and the RAZORLEAF ship in the hangar.
  5. Flying the Razorleaf triggers unique pirate hails — some flee outright. The armor's ominous rep precedes you.
🎁 Free legendary ship + the Mantis armor, both early-game kings

Keelhaul & the faction legendariesStory

📍 Faction questline finales · 🎁 Best-in-slot named weapons
  1. Keelhaul (Crimson Fleet finale, Fleet side): legendary pistol with elite rolls — the pirate ending's crown jewel.
  2. UC line rewards include top Vanguard gear tiers; Ryujin pays in the Manipulation skill (a legendary that isn't an item).
  3. Sir Livingstone's Pistol: not faction — from your parents (Kid Stuff trait) over visits. Yes, the trait pays gear.
  4. The Star Eagle (Rangers) counts: Class B ship in the base game.
  5. Shattered Space adds the Va'ruun particle tier — the House gear outclasses base legendaries at range.
🎁 Best-in-slot named weapons

Ship building: the only system you must learnMedium

📍 Any ship technician · 🎁 Your ship IS your build
  1. Class basics: A = agile/light, B = balanced (needs Piloting 3), C = capital (Piloting 4) — engine class must match reactor class.
  2. The meta stat is MOBILITY (100 if possible) + top-tier reactor: turn rate wins fights, reactor wins everything.
  3. Shielded cargo (from the Porrima vendors) enables smuggling runs past scans.
  4. Particle beam turrets (auto-fire) trivialize space combat at any skill level — slot two banks minimum.
  5. Steal ships to sell: precision), fly to a port, register, profit. The Ecliptic Claymore fleet is your used-car lot.
🎁 Your ship IS your build
▶

Va'ruun'kai particle arsenal (Shattered Space)Medium

📍 Dazra vendors & House quests · 🎁 The expansion's weapon tier
  1. Dazra's central market sells the base particle line; House questlines award the named variants.
  2. Particle damage (energy+physical split) answers the expansion's phantom enemies and stays top-tier back in the base game.
  3. The Va'ruun blade line finally makes melee builds respectable — pair with Solomon's Adventures magazines.
  4. Serpent's Embrace trait holders get extra dialogue AND gear discounts throughout the House quests.
  5. Bring credits: the expansion's economy assumes an established character. Sell your outpost hoard first.
🎁 The expansion's weapon tier

The Hunter & The Emissary (Revelation)Very Hard (both-sided)

📍 Masada III temple gauntlet · 🎁 The final artifact, the Unity's door
  1. Both phase-shift (brief invulnerability blinks) and spam their signature powers — Phased Time on YOUR bar answers everything.
  2. The Hunter closes to melee relentlessly; the Emissary fights ranged with gravity powers — prioritize the Hunter in shared fights.
  3. The gauntlet's Starborn waves respawn between arena chambers: don't burn all consumables early.
  4. Anti-Gravity Field + shotgun is the humble-but-honest answer to phase-shifters.
  5. Their pre-fight dialogues change per your whole playthrough's choices — the debate is the real boss.
🎁 The final artifact, the Unity's door
▶

Terrormorphs (and the Tau Ceti nightmare)Hard

📍 UC Vanguard line + wild spawns · 🎁 Vanguard progression, the ecosystem truth
  1. They burrow-charge: sidestep at the dirt-plume, never backpedal.
  2. The scream stagger chains into the pounce — break line of sight behind terrain to reset their pattern.
  3. Heavy auto rifles (the Magshear) or the Gravity Well power handle adults; incendiary handles broods.
  4. The Vanguard line's Aceles reveal explains their spread — the quest's science subplot is genuinely good sci-fi.
  5. Late-game high-level variants guard elite POIs. The scream never stops being scary in headphones.
🎁 Vanguard progression, the ecosystem truth

The Legacy heist & the Lock diveHard

📍 Crimson Fleet finale approach · 🎁 Kryx's fortune data, the faction's climax
  1. The Lock: choose infiltration style — stealth or the maintenance approach; the warden's office holds the route intel.
  2. The Legacy derelict: zero-G sections with floating hazards — magboots rhythm and thrust discipline.
  3. The data core extraction trips the security gauntlet on the way OUT — pre-clear your exfil route's turrets.
  4. The gas giant's radiation timer soft-pressures the dive: loot fast, loot smart.
  5. Deliver to your true side at Legacy's End — the space battle over the Key follows immediately. Repair your ship first.
🎁 Kryx's fortune data, the faction's climax

The Vortex anomalies (Shattered Space)Very Hard

📍 Va'ruun'kai · 🎁 Expansion climax
  1. Phantom enemies phase like Starborn but hit harder — particle weapons and AoE powers answer them.
  2. Anomaly zones warp movement (low-G pockets, gravity slams) — the boost pack is survival equipment here.
  3. The House choices determine your allies in the final assault — all three paths converge with different support.
  4. The final anomaly arena layers every phantom type — deep ammo reserves.
  5. The closing choice writes House Va'ruun's canon for your universe. Choose like it matters; in NG+ you'll wonder.
🎁 Expansion climax

NG+ is the actual game (the Unity loop)Easy

📍 The Unity → new universes · 🎁 The multiverse structure, power ranks, meta-dialogue
  1. Entering the Unity restarts the universe: you keep skills, powers, skills and powers — gear and ships reset (exception: nothing else).
  2. NG+ universes ROLL VARIATIONS: some Lodges hold shocking alt-realities (meet yourself, empty Lodges, stranger things) — 10+ documented variants.
  3. You can speedrun main artifacts in NG+ and re-collect powers to rank them up each universe.
  4. Starborn dialogue options unlock everywhere — quests resolve in new skips because you literally know the future.
  5. The Starborn ship and suit upgrade each NG+ tier through NG+10. The grind is the point; the point is letting go.
🎁 The multiverse structure, power ranks, meta-dialogue
▶

Outposts are money printers (if you must)Medium

📍 Any resource-rich planet · 🎁 Passive credits & XP crafting loops
  1. Classic spot: survey scanner shows dual zones) — drop extractors on the seam.
  2. Craft adaptive frames en masse: each craft ticks XP — industrial crafting is the fastest legitimate leveling loop.
  3. Link cargo output to transfer containers; sell at volume to Trade Authority kiosks (they never ask questions).
  4. Sleep on your outpost bed for the Well Rested XP buff before craft sessions.
  5. Or skip it all and pirate ships for a living — morally flexible, more fun, similar margins.
🎁 Passive credits & XP crafting loops
▶

Skill priorities that actually matterEasy

📍 Level-up screen · 🎁 A build that feels good by level 15
  1. Boost Pack Training (Tech 1): jetpack. Non-negotiable, first point.
  2. Social tree lockpicking): master locks gate the best POI loot rooms.
  3. Persuasion (Social): skips half the game's fights and gates faction peaks.
  4. Weight Lifting + Payloads: Bethesda inventory tax relief.
  5. Weapon certs: pick ONE family (ballistic rifles carry the base game; particle for Shattered Space) and rank it deep rather than wide.
🎁 A build that feels good by level 15

The Red Mile, the Almagest & the wandering weirdnessMedium

📍 Porrima system & beyond · 🎁 The game's best unmarked content
  1. The Red Mile (Porrima III): run the death gauntlet for the leaderboard — the record holder's story is in the bar.
  2. The Almagest wreck (same system): zero-G casino heist; the office safe area.
  3. Random encounter ships: the grandma who feeds you, the colony ship generations from arrival, the smuggler with cold feet — always hail, always board when offered.
  4. Juno's rogue AI encounter poses the game's best unmarked moral dilemma.
  5. Keep music low and let the ambient score work. The loneliness is a feature.
🎁 The game's best unmarked content
